# Sky Altar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Sky Altar shows the stars, the Sun, the Moon and the planets behind your
phone, over the camera picture. The app sends no data to the developer.
The camera picture, your position and your settings stay on your phone.
The ads come from Google AdMob, which collects the data listed under
"Ads".

## What the app stores, and where

- **Settings** such as the theme, the language, the music and the ads
  choices stay in the app's storage on the phone.
- **Purchase** "Remove ads" is a Google Play purchase. Google Play keeps
  the record, and the app asks Google Play for it on each start.

## What the app does with phone permissions

- **Camera** shows the sky over the camera picture and finds the bright
  stars in it. The app looks at a still picture about every 1.5 seconds,
  on the phone, and keeps none of them. No picture leaves the phone.
- **Location** gives the rough position of the phone, so the stars sit in
  the right place. The position stays on the phone. Without it the app
  guesses the place from the time zone.
- **Motion sensors** tell the app where the phone points.
- **Notifications** in the version from workbench-soft.com, say that an
  update is ready to install.

## Network connections the app makes

- **Google AdMob**, to load the ads. See "Ads" below.
- **Google Play**, to sell "Remove ads" and check that you bought it.
- **GitHub**, in the version from workbench-soft.com only, to look for a
  newer version and download it. GitHub sees the request like any web
  request. The version from Google Play doesn't make this check.

The app sends no picture or position to the developer.

## Ads

A banner shows at the foot of the theme list and of Settings. A full
screen ad may play when the theme list closes, at most once every 30
minutes. The sky view itself shows no ads. "Remove ads" in Settings turns
them off for good on your Google account.

- **What Google collects** the advertising ID of the phone, an
  approximate location from the IP address, how you interact with the
  ads, and crash and performance data from the ads code.
- **Cookies and IDs** Google uses the advertising ID, and cookies or
  similar IDs, to show ads, to measure them and to stop fraud. Google
  explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.
- **Your choice** in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for
  consent before an ad loads. Settings, "Ad privacy choices" changes the
  answer. Elsewhere, Android Settings, Privacy, Ads resets or deletes the
  advertising ID.
- **No camera data** goes to Google. The ads code sees no camera picture
  and no exact position.

## Analytics, accounts

The app has no analytics of its own and no user accounts.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps, Sky
Altar, Storage. Either removes the settings. Google Play keeps the record
of "Remove ads", so it comes back after a reinstall.

## Children

The app is not meant for children, and it doesn't knowingly collect
data from them.

## This website

This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no
cookies and keeps no data that tells one visitor from another. Read the
[Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
The app sends no data to Cloudflare.
