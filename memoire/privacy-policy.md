# Memoire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Memoire teaches English and Russian words in short batches of cards. The
app has no user accounts and sends no data to the developer. Your cards,
answers and settings stay on your device. Google AdMob shows ads, and
Google collects the data listed under "Ads".

## What the app stores, and where

- **Cards** the words you learn or add, with their translations, meaning,
  example, topics and stars, in the app's storage.
- **Answers** when you saw each card and how you answered, to plan the
  next review, in the app's storage.
- **Level and goals** the level test result, the topics and the daily
  quota, in the app's storage.
- **Gemini key**, when you add one for talk practice, in the phone's
  secure store. It goes only to Google's Gemini service.
- **Settings** such as the language, the theme, the sound, the reminders
  and the ads choices, in the app's storage.
- **Purchases** "Remove ads" and "Memoire Pro" are Google Play purchases.
  Google Play keeps the record, and the app asks Google Play for it on
  each start.

## What the app does with device permissions

- **Notifications** for reminders at the times you set, only when words
  wait. They are made on the device.
- **Internet** for the lookups, pictures, ads and the services below.
- **Home screen widget** shows the number of waiting words and one word.
  Its data stays on the device.

## Network connections the app makes

- **Wiktionary data** (kaikki.org) and **MyMemory**
  (api.mymemory.translated.net), when you look up a word that isn't in
  the built in list. The app sends the word only.
- **Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons**, to show a picture for a word. The
  app sends the word only.
- **Google Gemini** (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), only when you add
  your own Gemini key and use talk practice or topics. The app sends your
  chat lines, the scene and some of your words with your key. Google's
  Gemini terms apply to that data.
- **Hugging Face**, once, when you pick the phone model for talk practice.
  The app downloads the model, and chats with it stay on the device.
- **Sync** (Google Firebase), only when you turn it on and pair devices
  with a code. Your cards go to a Firestore store under that code, with
  an anonymous sign in that holds no name or email. Anyone with the code
  sees those cards. Answers and settings stay on each device.
- **Google AdMob**, to load the ads. See "Ads" below.
- **Google Play**, to sell "Remove ads" and "Memoire Pro" and check that
  you bought them.
- **GitHub**, in the version from workbench-soft.com only, to look for a
  newer version and download it. The version from Google Play doesn't make
  this check.

## Ads

A banner shows at the bottom of the home screen, and a full screen ad may
show after a finished batch, at most once in 30 minutes. Cards, the level
test and talk practice show no ads. "Remove ads" or "Memoire Pro" turns
them off on your Google account.

- **What Google collects** the advertising ID of the phone, an
  approximate location from the IP address, how you interact with the
  ads, and crash and performance data from the ads code.
- **Cookies and IDs** Google uses the advertising ID, and cookies or
  similar IDs, to show ads, to measure them and to stop fraud. Google
  explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.
- **Your choice** in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for
  consent before an ad loads. Settings, "Ad privacy" changes the answer.
  Elsewhere, Android Settings, Privacy, Ads resets or deletes the
  advertising ID.
- **No words or answers** go to Google through the ads code.

## Analytics, accounts

The app has no analytics of its own and no user accounts.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps,
Memoire, Storage. Either removes the cards, answers, settings and the
Gemini key. To remove synced cards, delete them in the app while Sync is
on, then turn Sync off. Google Play keeps the record of your purchases, so
they come back after a reinstall. Email <hello@workbench-soft.com> to ask
for a synced deck to be deleted.

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly
collect data from them.
