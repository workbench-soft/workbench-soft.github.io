# Lumiere Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Lumiere Player plays your own videos and music from the device, a USB
stick or a network share, on Google TV and Android phones. The app sends
no data to the developer. Your files, playback positions, NAS logins and
settings stay on your device. On a phone a banner from Google AdMob shows
under the file list, and Google collects the data listed under "Ads".

## What the app stores, and where

- **Playback positions** for each file, in the app's storage. Android
  backup copies them to your Google account when backup is on, so they
  survive a reinstall.
- **NAS shares** you add, with their user name and password, in the app's
  storage. They stay out of Android backup and never leave the device,
  except to log in to that NAS.
- **Music library** the artist, album, title and cover of each song in the
  music folders you pick, in the app's storage, out of Android backup.
- **Settings** such as the language, the player buttons, Sponsor skip,
  voice control and the ads choices, in the app's storage.
- **Purchases** "Remove ads" and "Lumiere Pro" are Google Play purchases.
  Google Play keeps the record, and the app asks Google Play for it on
  each start.

## What the app does with device permissions

- **Videos, music and storage** to list and play the files on the device
  and on USB sticks. The files stay where they are.
- **Microphone**, only after you turn it on. The mic button on the Music
  screen listens for one command. On Android 13 and newer the words are
  recognised on the device. On older devices Android's speech service,
  usually Google's, may recognise them. Voice control, Hey Lumi keeps the
  microphone open to hear the wake phrase, which the app recognises on the
  device. No audio from the wake phrase leaves the device, and Android
  shows the microphone dot while it listens.
- **Notifications** for the music controls, for Hey Lumi while it
  listens, and in the version from workbench-soft.com for a ready update.
- **Run at start** brings Hey Lumi back after a reboot, when you turned it
  on.
- **Display over other apps**, asked once after you turn on Hey Lumi. It
  lets the app light the screen edge while it listens over another app,
  and come to the front for a command.
- **Bluetooth** lets "Hey Lumi, turn on Bluetooth" switch Bluetooth on.

## Network connections the app makes

- **Your NAS**, over SMB, with the login you saved, to list and play its
  files.
- **SponsorBlock** (sponsor.ajay.app), when Sponsor skip is on and a file
  name holds a YouTube video ID. The app sends the first 4 characters of
  the ID's SHA-256 hash, never the ID itself, and gets back the sponsor
  segments of many videos. See <https://sponsor.ajay.app>.
- **Wikidata** (wikidata.org), after a scan of your books, to find the
  author of each one. The app sends the book title.
- **MusicBrainz**, **Cover Art Archive** and **YouTube**, in the version
  from workbench-soft.com only, when you ask for music that isn't on your
  device. The app sends the artist, album or song name you say or type.
  Once a week on Wi-Fi it also downloads the newest yt-dlp from GitHub.
  The version from Google Play has no online music.
- **Google AdMob**, on a phone, to load the banner. See "Ads" below.
- **Google Play**, to sell "Remove ads" and "Lumiere Pro" and check that
  you bought them.
- **GitHub**, in the version from workbench-soft.com only, to look for a
  newer version and download it. The version from Google Play doesn't make
  this check.

## Ads

On a phone, a banner shows under the file list and the music library. A
video or song plays without ads, and Google TV shows no ads at all.
"Remove ads" or "Lumiere Pro" turns them off for good on your Google
account.

- **What Google collects** the advertising ID of the phone, an
  approximate location from the IP address, how you interact with the
  ads, and crash and performance data from the ads code.
- **Cookies and IDs** Google uses the advertising ID, and cookies or
  similar IDs, to show ads, to measure them and to stop fraud. Google
  explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.
- **Your choice** in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for
  consent before an ad loads. Settings, "Ad privacy" changes the answer.
  Elsewhere, Android Settings, Privacy, Ads resets or deletes the
  advertising ID.
- **No file names or audio** go to Google through the ads code.

## Analytics, accounts

The app has no analytics of its own and no user accounts.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps,
Lumiere Player, Storage. Either removes the positions, the NAS logins, the
music library and the settings. Positions already in Android backup go
when you delete the app's backup in your Google account. Google Play keeps
the record of your purchases, so they come back after a reinstall.

## Children

The app is not meant for children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data
from them.
