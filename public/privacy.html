Workbench Soft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page covers Workbench MCP, the internal tools Workbench Soft uses to manage its own apps. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page on workbench-soft.com.

Who uses it

Workbench MCP signs in only to the Google account of Workbench Soft. It isn't offered to anyone else, and no other person's data passes through it.

What it reads

  • AdMob the apps, ad units and reports of the Workbench Soft account, read only.
  • Google Play the apps, products and store details of the Workbench Soft developer account.

Where the data goes

The sign in token and the data it reads stay on the Workbench Soft PC. The tools send no data to Workbench Soft servers or to anyone else, and sell or share none.

Removing access

The account owner can remove access at any time in Google Account, Third party connections.
