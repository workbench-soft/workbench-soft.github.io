# SongCatcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

SongCatcher is an Android app that names the song playing around you after
you say "Shazam". The app sends no data to the developer. It has no ads, no
analytics and no accounts of its own. No sound leaves your phone. The
services under "Network connections the app makes" get only what is listed
there.

## What the app does with the microphone

- **Only after you turn it on** the switch "Listen for “Shazam”" starts the
  microphone, and the same switch stops it. While it listens, Android shows
  a notification and the microphone dot.
- **The wake word** the phone checks the sound for the word "Shazam" on its
  own. The app keeps none of that sound and sends none of it.
- **After the wake word** the app records up to 12 seconds of the music in
  memory and turns it into an audio fingerprint on the phone. The fingerprint
  is a short list of numbers. Music or speech can't be rebuilt from it. The
  recording is dropped once the catch ends.
- **Shared microphone** Android lets one app record at a time. With
  KeepPad, Lumiere Player, ServoHop or SpotTheCam from the same developer on
  the phone, one of them listens for each app's phrase and tells the other
  app which phrase it heard. No sound passes between the apps.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Caught songs** the last 50 songs with the title, the artist, the cover
  link and the time of the catch, in the app's storage on the phone.
- **Lyrics** the words of the songs you opened, in the app's cache.
- **Spotify sign-in** the tokens Spotify gives the app after you connect, in
  the app's storage. The app doesn't see your Spotify password.
- **Backup** Android backup is off for the app, so none of this goes to
  Google Drive.

## What the app does with phone permissions

- **Microphone** hears the wake word and the music after it, as described
  above.
- **Notifications** the notification Android requires while the app
  listens, with the state of a catch.
- **Internet** the connections listed below.

## Network connections the app makes

- **Apple ShazamKit**, after the wake word, to find the song. The app sends
  the audio fingerprint and gets back the title, the artist and the cover.
  Apple's policy is at <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.
- **Spotify**, only after you tap **Connect Spotify**. You sign in on
  Spotify's own page. The app then looks the caught song up and saves it to
  your Liked Songs. Spotify's policy is at
  <https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/>.
- **LRCLIB** (lrclib.net), when you tap **Lyrics**. The app sends the title
  and the artist of that song.
- **Cover pictures** the app loads the cover of a caught song from the link
  in ShazamKit's answer.
- **Lumiere Player** and **Guitar Upscale**, when they are on the same
  phone. The app hands the title and the artist to them on the phone, with
  no network in between.

## Analytics, ads, accounts

The app has no analytics, no ads and no user accounts. The developer gets
no data from the app.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps,
SongCatcher, Storage. Either removes the caught songs, the lyrics and the
Spotify sign-in. A song saved to Spotify stays in your Spotify library until
you remove it there. To cut the link from Spotify's side, open
<https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/> and remove SongCatcher.

## Children

The app is not meant for children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data
from them.
