# Tripforge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Tripforge finds cheap round trips around the dates you ask for, and the
sights and secret spots of the city. To do that it sends what you search
to the Tripforge server. Saved trips and your settings stay on your phone.
The ads come from Google AdMob, which collects the data listed under
"Ads".

## What the app sends, and where

- **Your search** the places, the date, how far the dates may shift, the
  nights away, the number of adults and children and the home airport go
  to the Tripforge server at `tripforge-api.workbench-soft.com`. It runs on
  Cloudflare and keeps no record of who searched.
- **Fares** the server asks the Travelpayouts Data API for recent Aviasales
  fares on the route. It sends the airports and the dates only. See the
  [Travelpayouts privacy policy](https://www.travelpayouts.com/en/privacy-policy).
- **Places** the server asks Wikidata, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage and Wikimedia
  Commons about the city. It sends the city name only. See the
  [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).
- **Cache** the server keeps the answers for the same route and city for a
  while, so the next search is faster. The cache holds fares and places,
  with nothing that tells one person from another.

No name, email, account or exact position leaves the phone.

## What the app stores on the phone

- **Settings** such as the language and the ads choices.
- **Saved trips** in Tripforge Pro, up to 50 trips you save on the ask
  screen.
- **Search count** the number of searches today, for the free daily limit.
- **Purchases** "Remove ads" and "Tripforge Pro" are Google Play purchases.
  Google Play keeps the record, and the app asks Google Play for it on each
  start.

## Links to other sites

A tap on an offer opens Aviasales or Google Flights in the browser, with
the route, the dates and the group filled in. A tap on a place opens its
page. The Tickalot button opens tickalot.web.app with the city, the
dates and the nights in the link. Those sites have their own privacy
policies.

## Ads

A banner shows under the ask and places screens. A full screen ad may play
at most once every 30 minutes. The booking links show no ads. "Remove ads"
or "Tripforge Pro" turns them off for good on your Google account.

- **What Google collects** the advertising ID of the phone, an approximate
  location from the IP address, how you interact with the ads, and crash
  and performance data from the ads code.
- **Cookies and IDs** Google uses the advertising ID, and cookies or
  similar IDs, to show ads, to measure them and to stop fraud. Google
  explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.
- **Your choice** in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for
  consent before an ad loads, and Settings changes the answer. Elsewhere,
  Android Settings, Privacy, Ads resets or deletes the advertising ID.

## Analytics, accounts

The app has no analytics of its own and no user accounts.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps,
Tripforge, Storage. Either removes the settings and the saved trips.
Google Play keeps the record of your purchases, so they come back after a
reinstall.

## Children

The app is not meant for children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data
from them.

## This website

This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no
cookies and keeps no data that tells one visitor from another. The web
version of Tripforge at <https://tripforge.workbench-soft.com> sends your
search to the same server and keeps your settings in the browser's
storage. Read the
[Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
