## The trine in short

- **Angle** 120 degrees, a third of the circle.
- **Usual orb** about 6 to 8 degrees.
- **Symbol** a triangle: △.
- **Key words** harmony, ease, flow, natural talent, support.

## What a trine is

A trine forms when two planets are about 120 degrees apart, four signs from each other. Astrologers read it as the most harmonious of the major aspects. The two planets share the same element, so they speak the same language and help each other without effort.

Old astrologers counted the trine as fully fortunate. Modern astrologers agree that trines show natural gifts: things that come easily, often from childhood. They add a small warning. What comes easily can be taken for granted, so a trine shines brightest when a person chooses to develop it.

## Which signs a trine links

Signs four apart always share an element:

- **Fire** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius.
- **Earth** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn.
- **Air** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius.
- **Water** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces.

That is why the [compatibility pages](../../compatibility/leo/) often call signs of the same element a natural match. Read more in the guides to the [elements](../../elements/water/).

## Reading a trine

To read a trine, ask what flows easily between the two planets:

- **Sun trine Moon** head and heart agree. A sense of inner ease.
- **Moon trine Venus** warmth and charm. People feel at home with you.
- **Mercury trine Mars** quick, confident thinking and speech.
- **Venus trine Jupiter** generosity, good taste and a happy social life.
- **Mars trine Saturn** steady, patient effort that gets results.
- **Sun trine Neptune** imagination and a gift for art or care.

Add the houses to see where the gift shows. A trine from the sixth to the tenth house may link daily skills with career success.

## The grand trine

When three planets each sit about 120 degrees apart, they form a large triangle across the chart called a grand trine. All three are in the same element. Astrologers read it as a deep reservoir of talent in that element: a fire grand trine full of enthusiasm, an earth grand trine full of practical sense. The classic advice is to put that gift to work, since a grand trine can also invite comfort and coasting.

## Trines in time

When moving planets form trines to planets in your birth chart, astrologers speak of supportive chapters. Things may flow more easily, and help may come from friends. Jupiter trines are especially popular in forecasts. Like any aspect, they describe a mood, not an outcome.

## The trine in the real sky

Two planets 120 degrees apart sit far apart in the sky, often one rising in the east while the other is high in the west. Waxing gibbous Moon a few days before full stands about 120 degrees from the Sun. The Sky Altar app shows where each planet is, so you can find these wide angles over the horizon.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. A trine is a way to think about your natural strengths. It does not promise success. Read more about the [sextile](../../aspects/sextile/), the [square](../../aspects/square/) and the [natal chart](../../basics/natal-chart/).
