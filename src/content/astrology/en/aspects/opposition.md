## The opposition in short

- **Angle** 180 degrees, half of the circle.
- **Usual orb** about 8 degrees, wider for the Sun and the Moon.
- **Symbol** two small circles joined by a line: ☍.
- **Key words** polarity, balance, awareness, relationship, tug of war.

## What an opposition is

An opposition forms when two planets sit on opposite sides of the zodiac, six signs apart. Astrologers read it as a see-saw. The two planets face each other directly, each pulling the person toward its own side. The challenge is to find a balance that honours both.

Oppositions are often linked with other people. Because we tend to own one side of the see-saw and see the other in those around us, oppositions often play out in relationships. A person with a strong opposition may meet the other side of themselves in a partner, a boss or a rival. Over time, many learn to hold both ends.

## Which signs face each other

Opposite signs share a modality and have friendly elements:

- **Aries and Libra** self and other.
- **Taurus and Scorpio** what is mine and what is shared.
- **Gemini and Sagittarius** facts and meaning.
- **Cancer and Capricorn** home and career.
- **Leo and Aquarius** the heart and the group.
- **Virgo and Pisces** order and flow.

Each pair is two halves of one theme. Read more in the [sign articles](../../signs/aries/).

## Reading an opposition

To read an opposition, ask what two needs pull against each other and how they could work together:

- **Sun opposite Moon** born near a full Moon. Awareness of others and a wish for balance between will and feeling.
- **Venus opposite Mars** strong attraction to opposites in love.
- **Mercury opposite Jupiter** a mind torn between detail and the big picture.
- **Moon opposite Saturn** feelings meet duty. Learning to care for yourself as well as others.
- **Mars opposite Uranus** a need for freedom that meets a strong will.

## Oppositions in time

Each month at full Moon, the Moon stands opposite the Sun. Astrologers read full Moons as times of culmination, when things come to light. Slow planets opposing their own birth position mark life stages, such as the Uranus opposition around age 42 or the Saturn opposition around 44.

## The opposition in the real sky

The full Moon is the opposition you can see. It rises in the east as the Sun sets in the west and shines all night. Outer planets like Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are at their brightest when they stand opposite the Sun, since Earth then lies between them and the Sun. Astronomers call this moment opposition too. The Sky Altar app shows the bright planet high at midnight when it happens.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. An opposition is a way to think about balance and relationships. It does not predict conflict. Read more about the [conjunction](../../aspects/conjunction/), the [square](../../aspects/square/) and the [natal chart](../../basics/natal-chart/).
