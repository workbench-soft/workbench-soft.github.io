## The square in short

- **Angle** 90 degrees, a quarter of the circle.
- **Usual orb** about 6 to 8 degrees.
- **Symbol** a small square: □.
- **Key words** tension, friction, challenge, effort, growth.

## What a square is

A square forms when two planets are about 90 degrees apart, three signs from each other. Astrologers read it as a clash. The two planets want different things and push against each other, like two people trying to go through a door at the same time.

That sounds hard, and squares can feel hard. Yet astrologers value them highly. A square creates energy. The friction pushes a person to act, learn and find a creative solution. Many of the strongest skills in a chart grow out of its squares.

## Which signs a square links

Signs three apart share a modality but differ in element:

- **Cardinal signs** Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn square each other.
- **Fixed signs** Taurus, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius square each other.
- **Mutable signs** Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius and Pisces square each other.

Each pair has the same kind of drive but a different way of using it. That is the source of the friction. Read more about the [modalities](../../modalities/cardinal/).

## Reading a square

To read a square, ask where the two planets pull against each other, and what skill could grow from it:

- **Sun square Moon** will and feelings pull apart. Learning to honour both brings inner strength.
- **Venus square Mars** a strong but restless love life. Passion meets the wish for harmony.
- **Mercury square Saturn** a mind that doubts itself and then masters a subject through hard work.
- **Moon square Jupiter** big feelings and a love of more. Learning when enough is enough.
- **Mars square Pluto** great drive that learns to be used wisely.

## The T-square and grand cross

When two planets in opposition both square a third, astrologers call it a T-square. The third planet becomes the place where the tension lands and must be resolved. When four planets form two oppositions and four squares, it is a grand cross, a rare and dynamic pattern.

## Squares in time

The Moon makes a square to the Sun twice a month, at the first and last quarter. These are times of action in the lunar cycle. Slow planets forming squares to planets in your birth chart are read as chapters of challenge and growth. The Saturn square at about age 7, 14, 21 and 29 is well known in popular astrology.

## The square in the real sky

At first quarter the Moon stands about 90 degrees east of the Sun, half lit and high in the sky at sunset. That is the most famous square you can see. In the Sky Altar app, look for the Moon at first quarter and see how far it sits from the setting Sun.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. A square is a way to think about inner tension and growth. It does not predict conflict or bad luck. Read more about the [opposition](../../aspects/opposition/), the [trine](../../aspects/trine/) and the [natal chart](../../basics/natal-chart/).
