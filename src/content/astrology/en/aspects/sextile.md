## The sextile in short

- **Angle** 60 degrees, one sixth of the circle.
- **Usual orb** about 4 to 6 degrees.
- **Symbol** a six pointed star: ⚹.
- **Key words** opportunity, friendship, cooperation, talent you can use.

## What a sextile is

A sextile forms when two planets are about 60 degrees apart, two signs away from each other. Astrologers read it as a friendly link. The planets get on well and help each other, but the help comes as an opportunity. You have to reach out and use it.

The sextile is often called a soft aspect, along with the trine. It brings ease without the sleepiness that a very easy aspect can bring. Many astrologers see it as a door left open: a talent, contact or idea that grows once you walk through.

## Which signs a sextile links

Signs two apart are always of friendly elements:

- **Fire and air** Aries with Gemini, Leo with Libra, Sagittarius with Aquarius.
- **Earth and water** Taurus with Cancer, Virgo with Scorpio, Capricorn with Pisces.

Fire is fed by air, and earth is nourished by water, so the sextile blends two energies that support each other. Read more in the guides to the [elements](../../elements/fire/).

## Reading a sextile

To read a sextile, ask how the two planets can help each other in a light, practical way:

- **Sun sextile Moon** head and heart get on. Inner balance comes easily.
- **Mercury sextile Venus** a pleasant way with words. Good for writing and diplomacy.
- **Venus sextile Mars** easy charm and attraction.
- **Mars sextile Jupiter** energy for big plans and sport.
- **Moon sextile Saturn** steady feelings and good sense.
- **Jupiter sextile Saturn** a gift for growing things patiently.

Then add the houses. A sextile between the third and fifth houses may link writing with creative play.

## Sextile or trine

Both are soft aspects, but they feel different. A trine is like a gift you were born with: natural, effortless, sometimes taken for granted. A sextile is more like a friendly invitation: it rewards effort. Some astrologers say people often make more of their sextiles than their trines, because they have to act on them.

## Sextiles in time

When moving planets form sextiles to planets in your birth chart, astrologers speak of open doors. A friend may suggest a job, or a class may appear at the right moment. The classic advice is simple: when chances come, say yes to the ones that fit.

## The sextile in the sky

In the real sky, two planets 60 degrees apart are separated by about six fist widths held at arm's length. On an evening when the Moon is a few days past new, it may form a sextile with the Sun that has just set. The Sky Altar app shows each planet's place, so you can measure these angles over the horizon.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. A sextile is a way to think about easy links between parts of yourself. It does not promise luck. Read more about the [trine](../../aspects/trine/), the [conjunction](../../aspects/conjunction/) and the [natal chart](../../basics/natal-chart/).
