## Earth in short

- **Signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn.
- **Qualities** cold and dry, in the old language of the four humours.
- **Key words** stability, patience, practical sense, the body, results.
- **Season link** the earth signs fall in the middle of spring, the end of summer and the start of winter.

## Where the elements come from

The four elements came from Greek philosophy. Empedocles wrote of four roots that mix to make the world, and later thinkers such as Aristotle described each by two qualities: hot or cold, dry or wet. Astrologers gave each sign an element in a repeating cycle: fire, earth, air, water. Earth comes second, at Taurus.

The three earth signs sit 120 degrees apart and form a triangle. Astrologers say they share a common language of the senses and the solid world.

## What earth stands for

Earth is the element of form and matter. Astrologers read it as the ability to build, to grow things slowly and to deal with the real world: money, work, the body, food and nature. People with a lot of earth in their charts are often described as reliable, patient, sensible and loyal. They trust what they can see and touch.

Earth's shadow side is rigidity. An earth sign can become stuck, overly cautious or focused on material things. Like a field, it needs rest and fresh seeds now and then.

## The three earth signs

- **Taurus** fixed earth. The fertile field. It enjoys, keeps and steadies.
- **Virgo** mutable earth. The harvest being sorted. It refines, serves and perfects.
- **Capricorn** cardinal earth. The mountain. It plans, builds and climbs.

## Earth in a chart

A chart heavy in earth suggests a grounded, practical, patient temperament. Such a person often has a knack for making ideas real. A chart with little earth may describe someone who lives more in ideas, feelings or plans, and who benefits from routines, lists, gardening, cooking or time in nature to feel settled.

As with every element, the Sun, the Moon and the rising sign weigh most.

## Earth and the other elements

- **Earth and earth** natural partners who build together.
- **Earth and water** water nourishes earth. A caring, steady match.
- **Earth and fire** fire warms earth, or scorches it. Each can learn from the other's pace.
- **Earth and air** air brings ideas, earth makes them real. They may seem to speak different languages at first.

These are broad themes. A real chart has a mix of all four.

## Bringing in earth

For those who feel scattered, astrologers often suggest earthy habits: a walk in a park, a meal cooked from scratch, a simple budget, a regular bedtime. These are good for anyone, whatever the chart.

## Earth in the sky

The earth signs mark key turning points of the farming year in the northern hemisphere. Taurus is the time of planting and blossom. Virgo is the harvest. Capricorn is the winter store. Old almanacs used these signs to guide work in the fields.
