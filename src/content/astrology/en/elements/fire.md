## Fire in short

- **Signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius.
- **Qualities** hot and dry, in the old language of the four humours.
- **Key words** energy, enthusiasm, courage, inspiration, action.
- **Season link** the fire signs fall at the start of spring, the height of summer and the end of autumn.

## Where the elements come from

Greek thinkers such as Empedocles taught that the world is made of four roots: fire, earth, air and water. Astrologers in Hellenistic Egypt gave each of the twelve signs one of these elements, in a repeating order around the zodiac. Fire comes first, at Aries, so fire opens the zodiac.

Signs of the same element sit 120 degrees apart and form a triangle, called a triplicity. That is why fire signs are said to get on naturally with each other.

## What fire stands for

Fire is the element of spirit and drive. Astrologers read it as the spark that starts things, the warmth that draws people in and the faith that keeps going. People with a lot of fire in their charts are often described as lively, brave, honest and generous. They need action, fun and a goal to aim at.

Fire's shadow side is impatience. A fire sign can rush, burn out or forget to listen. Like a real flame, it needs fuel and care to burn steadily.

## The three fire signs

- **Aries** cardinal fire. The spark that lights the match. It starts, leads and pushes forward.
- **Leo** fixed fire. The steady hearth. It warms, creates and shines with loyalty.
- **Sagittarius** mutable fire. The wildfire that spreads. It explores, teaches and seeks meaning.

## Fire in a chart

Astrologers count how many planets sit in each element. A chart heavy in fire suggests a lively, warm and active temperament. A chart with little or no fire may describe a person who is more careful or reflective, who finds energy through other means and benefits from things that inspire them: sport, music, travel, people with spark.

The Sun, the Moon and the rising sign count most in this tally.

## Fire and the other elements

- **Fire and fire** natural friends, full of energy and fun.
- **Fire and air** air feeds fire. Ideas and action spark each other.
- **Fire and earth** earth can ground fire, or smother it. Patience helps both.
- **Fire and water** water can calm fire, or put it out, and fire can make water boil. Care is needed.

These are broad themes. A real chart has a mix of all four.

## Bringing in fire

Astrologers who notice little fire in a chart sometimes suggest small ways to bring it in: a new sport, a bold goal, a dance class, time in the sun or a weekend trip. Anyone can enjoy these, whatever their chart.
