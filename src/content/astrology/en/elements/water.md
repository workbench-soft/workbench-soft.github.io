## Water in short

- **Signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces.
- **Qualities** cold and wet, in the old language of the four humours.
- **Key words** feeling, intuition, care, memory, imagination.
- **Season link** the water signs fall at the start of summer, the middle of autumn and the end of winter.

## Where the elements come from

Astrologers took the four elements from Greek philosophy and gave each sign one in a repeating order: fire, earth, air, water. Water comes fourth, at Cancer, and closes each cycle of four. The three water signs sit 120 degrees apart and form a triangle.

All three water signs are creatures that live in or near water: the Crab, the Scorpion, which old sources sometimes linked with the marsh, and the Fishes. Astrologers read water as the element of everything that flows below the surface.

## What water stands for

Water is the element of feeling. Astrologers read it as the ability to sense moods, to care, to remember and to imagine. People with a lot of water in their charts are often described as sensitive, intuitive, loyal and kind. They need closeness, safety and time to process what they feel.

Water's shadow side is being overwhelmed. A water sign can soak up other people's moods, hold on to hurts or retreat into its shell. Like a river, it needs banks to give it shape.

## The three water signs

- **Cancer** cardinal water. The tide that comes in. It protects, nurtures and remembers.
- **Scorpio** fixed water. The deep still lake. It feels intensely, holds on and transforms.
- **Pisces** mutable water. The ocean. It dreams, merges and forgives.

## Water in a chart

A chart heavy in water suggests an emotional, intuitive and caring temperament. Such a person often picks up on what others feel without words. A chart with little water may describe someone who leads with logic or action, and who benefits from music, art, time near water or a close friend to share feelings with.

The Sun, the Moon and the rising sign weigh most in this tally.

## Water and the other elements

- **Water and water** deep understanding, sometimes a flood of feelings.
- **Water and earth** earth gives water a shape, water makes earth bloom. A nurturing match.
- **Water and fire** steam: passion, but also tension. Care and patience help.
- **Water and air** feeling meets thinking. They can learn a great deal from each other.

These are broad themes. A real chart has a mix of all four.

## Bringing in water

For those who feel out of touch with their feelings, astrologers often suggest watery habits: a long bath, a swim, a sad film, music, a journal of dreams or an honest talk with a friend.

## Water in the sky

Pisces lies in the part of the sky that old astronomers called the Sea, near Capricornus, Aquarius and Cetus the Whale. Cancer glows faintly between the Twins and the Lion, and Scorpius curls across the brightest part of the Milky Way. The Sky Altar app shows them along the path of the Sun, drawn in each culture's style.
