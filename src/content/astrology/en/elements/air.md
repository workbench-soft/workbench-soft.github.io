## Air in short

- **Signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius.
- **Qualities** hot and wet, in the old language of the four humours.
- **Key words** ideas, words, connection, fairness, perspective.
- **Season link** the air signs fall at the end of spring, the start of autumn and the middle of winter.

## Where the elements come from

The four elements of Greek philosophy passed into astrology in Hellenistic times. Each sign took an element in a repeating order: fire, earth, air, water. Air comes third, at Gemini. The three air signs sit 120 degrees apart and form a triangle.

Unlike the other elements, all three air signs are shown as people or human made things: the Twins, the Scales and the Water Bearer. Astrologers read this as a hint that air is the most human element, the one of thought and society.

## What air stands for

Air is the element of mind and connection. Astrologers read it as the ability to think, talk, compare and see from many sides. People with a lot of air in their charts are often described as curious, sociable, fair and quick. They need conversation, new ideas and room to move.

Air's shadow side is detachment. An air sign can live in its head, talk instead of feel or skip from topic to topic. Like the wind, it needs something to blow toward.

## The three air signs

- **Gemini** mutable air. The breeze that carries news. It learns, talks and connects.
- **Libra** cardinal air. The balance of the scales. It relates, weighs and makes peace.
- **Aquarius** fixed air. The steady current of ideas. It invents, gathers groups and looks ahead.

## Air in a chart

A chart heavy in air suggests a mental, social and communicative temperament. Such a person often thinks things through before acting. A chart with little air may describe someone who acts on instinct or feeling, and who benefits from talking things out, writing, reading or joining a group.

The Sun, the Moon and the rising sign count most in this tally.

## Air and the other elements

- **Air and air** easy friends who love to talk.
- **Air and fire** air feeds fire. Ideas become action.
- **Air and earth** air brings ideas, earth gives them form. Patience bridges the gap.
- **Air and water** head meets heart. Air can stir water into waves, or water can make air feel heavy. Kindness helps.

These are broad themes. A real chart has a mix of all four.

## Bringing in air

For those who feel stuck, astrologers often suggest airy habits: a chat with a friend, a journal, a new book, a club or a class. Fresh air in a literal sense helps too, such as a walk on a windy day.

## Air in the sky

Air signs mark the turn of seasons in the north. Gemini brings the long days before the solstice. Libra holds the autumn equinox. Aquarius sits in the coldest part of winter, when people gather inside to talk and plan. Sky Altar shows the three air constellations along the path of the Sun.
