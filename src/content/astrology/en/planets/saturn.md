## Saturn in short

- **Rules** Capricorn, and Aquarius in the older tradition.
- **Symbol** a cross above a curved hook, like a sickle: ♄.
- **Cycle** about 29 and a half years around the zodiac, about two and a half years in each sign.
- **Key words** time, discipline, limits, responsibility, patience, mastery.

## What Saturn stands for

Saturn was the farthest planet the ancients could see, slow and pale at the edge of the known sky. It became the planet of time, limits and old age. In a chart, Saturn shows where you face tests, where you need patience and where, through steady work, you can build something that lasts.

Old astrology called Saturn the greater malefic and feared it. Modern astrologers often call it the teacher. Its lessons can feel heavy, but they lead to maturity, skill and self respect. Many people find that the area of Saturn in their chart becomes a source of strength later in life.

## Saturn through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, Saturn teaches patience with one's own drive and pride.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, Saturn feels at home. It builds slowly and well.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, Saturn brings structure to thought, fairness and social life.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, Saturn asks for emotional boundaries and resilience.

## The signs it rules

In Capricorn, Saturn is the builder and the manager: ambitious, careful and responsible. In the older tradition it also ruled Aquarius, where its structure serves the group and the future. Since the discovery of Uranus, many astrologers give Aquarius to Uranus and keep Saturn as a co-ruler.

## Saturn in the houses

The house of Saturn shows where you may feel tested or slow to start. In the second house it can link with care around money. In the seventh it may bring serious, committed partnerships or late marriage. In the tenth it suits a long, steady climb in a career. Over time, astrologers say, the Saturn house becomes the house of mastery.

## The Saturn return

Saturn returns to its birth position around the ages of 29, 58 and 87. The first Saturn return is famous in popular astrology. Many people in their late twenties feel a push to grow up: to choose a direction, end what no longer fits and take on real responsibility. Whatever its cause, it is a common turning point, and talking about it can help.

## The real Saturn in the sky

Saturn shines with a steady pale gold light. To the eye it looks like a bright star, but even a small telescope reveals its rings, one of the finest sights in the sky. It takes nearly three decades to circle the zodiac, so it stays in the same constellation for a couple of years. The Sky Altar app marks it over your camera.

## Myths of Saturn

The Romans honoured Saturn as a god of farming and of a lost golden age. Their winter festival, Saturnalia, turned the world upside down for a few days of feasting. The Greeks linked him with Kronos, the Titan who ruled before Zeus. Our Saturday still carries his name.
