## Pluto in short

- **Rules** Scorpio in modern astrology.
- **Symbol** a circle over a crescent over a cross, or a P and L joined: ♇.
- **Cycle** about 248 years around the zodiac, from 12 to 31 years in a sign because of its long oval orbit.
- **Key words** transformation, power, depth, endings, renewal, the hidden.

## A small world with a big meaning

Clyde Tombaugh found Pluto in 1930 at the Lowell Observatory in Arizona, after months of comparing photographs of the night sky. An eleven year old English girl, Venetia Burney, suggested the name of the Roman god of the underworld. In 2006 astronomers reclassed Pluto as a dwarf planet, but astrologers kept it in the chart. Size, they say, is not the same as meaning.

## What Pluto stands for

In a chart, Pluto shows where life asks you to go deep and to change at the root. It is the energy of endings and new beginnings, of power and letting go, of what is hidden coming to light. Like the phoenix, it suggests that something must burn away before something new can rise.

Pluto is linked with psychology, research, crisis, shared money and the forces in a society that are too big for one person to control.

## Why it rules Scorpio

Before 1930, Mars ruled Scorpio. The new planet of the underworld matched Scorpio's themes of depth, secrets and change so well that astrologers made Pluto its modern ruler. Mars remains Scorpio's traditional co-ruler.

## Pluto generations

Pluto moves very slowly, so it marks whole generations. Astrologers read it as the deep changes each generation lives through and brings about. Pluto in Scorpio, from about 1983 to 1995, is linked with a generation that faces taboos openly. Pluto in Capricorn, from 2008 to 2024, is linked with shake ups in banks, governments and big institutions. Pluto entered Aquarius in 2024, and astrologers expect themes of technology and collective power.

Because millions share each Pluto sign, astrologers focus on Pluto's house and its aspects to personal planets for a single chart.

## Pluto in the houses

The house of Pluto shows where you meet deep change and hidden strength. In the first house it gives a powerful, private presence. In the fourth it may point to deep family stories. In the eighth, its natural home, it explores shared resources and life's big passages. In the tenth it may drive a wish for influence and real impact.

## Pluto transits

When Pluto touches a personal planet by aspect, astrologers speak of a slow, deep chapter that can last a couple of years. Old habits and roles may fall away. Many people look back on such times as turning points in which they found their real strength.

## The real Pluto in the sky

Pluto is far too faint to see without a large telescope. In 2015 the New Horizons spacecraft flew past it and sent back pictures of a world with mountains of ice and a pale heart shaped plain. Pluto's long, tilted orbit sometimes brings it closer to the Sun than Neptune. The Sky Altar app marks its place among the stars.

## Myths of Pluto

Pluto, the Greek Hades, ruled the underworld and its hidden riches. He carried Persephone down to his kingdom, and her yearly return to the world above was said to bring the spring. The myth fits Pluto's astrological theme of loss and renewal.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. Pluto in a chart is a way to reflect on change and inner strength. It does not foretell crisis or loss. Read more about [Scorpio](../../signs/scorpio/), [Mars](../../planets/mars/) and the [eighth house](../../houses/8/).
