## Mercury in short

- **Rules** Gemini and Virgo.
- **Symbol** a circle on a cross with two small horns: ☿.
- **Cycle** about one year around the zodiac, at most 28 degrees from the Sun.
- **Key words** thinking, talking, writing, learning, trade, travel.

## What Mercury stands for

Mercury was the messenger of the Roman gods, quick on his winged sandals. In a chart, Mercury stands for how you think and communicate: how you learn, how you speak and write, how you make plans and how you handle the small trips and errands of daily life.

Because Mercury stays close to the Sun, it is always in the same sign as your Sun or in one of the signs next to it. That ties the way you think closely to who you are.

## Mercury through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, Mercury thinks fast, speaks boldly and loves big ideas.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, Mercury thinks practically and carefully, and learns by doing.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, Mercury is lively, social and curious, at home with words.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, Mercury thinks through feeling and memory, and picks up on what is unsaid.

## The two signs it rules

In Gemini, Mercury shows its curious, talkative, many sided face. In Virgo it shows its careful, skilled and useful face. Together the two signs hint at the full range of the mind: gathering facts and sorting them.

## Mercury in the houses

The house of Mercury shows what you most like to think and talk about. In the third house it is busy with neighbours, siblings and local news. In the ninth it reaches for philosophy and travel. In the sixth it focuses on work methods and daily tasks. In the tenth it may make a career of words.

## Mercury retrograde

About three times a year, Mercury seems to stop and move backwards against the stars for about three weeks. This is an optical effect: Earth overtakes Mercury on the inside track, much like a faster car seems to slide back as you pass it.

Astrologers read these periods as a time to review, revise and reconnect. Popular advice suggests double checking travel plans, backing up files and reading contracts twice. Those are good habits at any time. There is no evidence that Mercury retrograde causes problems, so use it as a gentle reminder to slow down, not as a reason to worry.

## The real Mercury in the sky

Mercury is the hardest of the bright planets to see. It hangs low near the horizon just after sunset or before sunrise, and only for a few weeks at a time. Copernicus is said to have regretted that he did not once see it. The Sky Altar app shows where Mercury sits even when the Sun's glare hides it.

## Myths of Mercury

The Greeks called him Hermes, the trickster god of travellers, thieves and merchants, who guided souls to the underworld. The Babylonians linked the planet with Nabu, the god of writing and wisdom. In many cultures the quick, elusive planet stood for messages between worlds.
