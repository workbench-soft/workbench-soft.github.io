## Jupiter in short

- **Rules** Sagittarius, and Pisces in the older tradition.
- **Symbol** a shape like the number 4: ♃.
- **Cycle** about 12 years around the zodiac, about one year in each sign.
- **Key words** growth, hope, wisdom, generosity, travel, faith, abundance.

## What Jupiter stands for

Jupiter was the king of the Roman gods, and the largest planet in the solar system. In a chart, Jupiter stands for growth and expansion. It shows where you find hope, meaning and good fortune, and where you are generous. It also links with higher learning, travel, law and belief.

Old astrology called Jupiter the greater benefic, the most helpful planet. Modern astrologers add that too much of a good thing is still too much. Jupiter can also stand for excess, overconfidence and promising more than you can give.

## Jupiter through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, Jupiter brings big enthusiasm, courage and a love of adventure.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, Jupiter grows things steadily and finds luck in hard work.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, Jupiter grows through ideas, learning and people.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, Jupiter grows through care, faith and compassion.

## The signs it rules

In Sagittarius, Jupiter is the explorer and the teacher, always seeking the bigger picture. In the older tradition it also ruled Pisces, where its generosity turns toward compassion and spiritual life. Since the discovery of Neptune, many astrologers give Pisces to Neptune and keep Jupiter as co-ruler.

## Jupiter in the houses

The house of Jupiter shows where life tends to open up. In the second house it may link with generosity around money. In the ninth house it loves travel and study. In the seventh it favours kind partners. In the tenth it hints at public success. Astrologers add that Jupiter's gifts tend to come through effort and openness, not by waiting.

## The Jupiter return and generations

Because Jupiter takes about twelve years to circle the zodiac, it returns to its birth position around the ages of 12, 24, 36, 48 and so on. Astrologers read these Jupiter returns as times of fresh growth and new chapters.

The twelve year cycle also links Jupiter with the Chinese zodiac. Ancient Chinese astronomers used Jupiter, the year star, to mark years, and its cycle helped shape the twelve animal calendar. Read more in the [Chinese zodiac overview](../../chinese-zodiac/overview/).

## The real Jupiter in the sky

Jupiter shines bright and steady, often the brightest point in the night sky after Venus. Even small binoculars show its four large moons, discovered by Galileo in 1610 and still called the Galilean moons. Its Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth. The Sky Altar app marks Jupiter over your camera, along with the sign it sits in.

## Myths of Jupiter

The Greeks called him Zeus, lord of the sky and thunder. The Babylonians linked the planet with Marduk, the chief god of Babylon. In Indian astrology Jupiter is Guru or Brihaspati, the teacher of the gods. Each tradition sees it as a wise and powerful ruler.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. Jupiter in a chart is a way to reflect on hope and growth. It does not promise luck, money or success. Read more about [Sagittarius](../../signs/sagittarius/), [Pisces](../../signs/pisces/) and the [ninth house](../../houses/9/).
