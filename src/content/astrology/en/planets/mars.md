## Mars in short

- **Rules** Aries, and Scorpio in the older tradition.
- **Symbol** a circle with an arrow pointing up and to the right: ♂.
- **Cycle** about two years around the zodiac, around six weeks in each sign.
- **Key words** action, energy, courage, desire, competition, anger.

## What Mars stands for

Mars was the Roman god of war, and the red planet has carried that fiery meaning for thousands of years. In a chart, Mars shows how you act and how you go after what you want. It is your drive, your courage, your physical energy and the way you handle conflict.

Old astrology called Mars the lesser malefic, a planet that could bring strife. Modern astrologers see it in a balanced way. Without Mars there is no get up and go. Well used, it gives the strength to defend, to build and to begin.

## Mars through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, Mars is bold, quick and enthusiastic. It acts first and thinks later.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, Mars is steady and persistent. It works hard and builds results.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, Mars acts through words, plans and debate.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, Mars acts on feeling. It can be protective, intense or indirect.

## The signs it rules

In Aries, Mars is direct, brave and pioneering. In the older tradition it also ruled Scorpio, where its energy turns inward into willpower, focus and endurance. Since the discovery of Pluto in 1930, many modern astrologers give Scorpio to Pluto and keep Mars as a co-ruler.

## Mars in the houses

The house of Mars shows where you put the most energy and where you may meet the most friction. In the first house it gives a lively, forceful personality. In the sixth house it pours effort into work and fitness. In the tenth it drives ambition. In the seventh it may bring passionate or competitive partners.

## Mars and attraction

In comparing charts, Mars stands for desire and Venus for attraction. One person's Mars touching another's Venus is a classic sign of chemistry. Mars also shows how a couple argues and makes up, which matters as much as how they flirt.

## Mars retrograde

Every two years or so, Mars appears to move backwards for about ten weeks. Earth is overtaking it on the inside track. During this time Mars is closest to us and at its brightest. Astrologers read it as a time to rethink plans and redirect energy rather than push forward blindly.

## The real Mars in the sky

Mars shines with a clear orange red light, easy to tell apart from the white stars. At its closest it outshines all but Venus, the Moon and Jupiter. Its colour comes from iron rich dust on its surface. Its two small moons are named Phobos and Deimos, fear and dread, after the sons of the war god. The Sky Altar app marks Mars over your camera wherever it is in the zodiac.

## Myths of Mars

The Greeks called him Ares, the restless god of battle. The Babylonians linked the red planet with Nergal, a god of plague and the underworld. The Romans made Mars a father of their nation and a guardian of farms as well as soldiers, and named the month of March after him.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. Mars in a chart is a way to reflect on your energy and how you assert yourself. It does not predict accidents, illness or fights. Read more about [Aries](../../signs/aries/), [Scorpio](../../signs/scorpio/) and [Venus](../../planets/venus/).
