## Neptune in short

- **Rules** Pisces in modern astrology.
- **Symbol** the trident of the sea god: ♆.
- **Cycle** about 165 years around the zodiac, about 14 years in each sign.
- **Key words** dreams, imagination, intuition, compassion, mystery, illusion.

## The planet found with a pen

Neptune was found by mathematics before anyone saw it. Astronomers noticed that Uranus wandered off its expected path, as if something unseen were pulling it. In 1846 Urbain Le Verrier worked out where that unseen planet should be, and Johann Galle found it within a degree of the spot on his first night of looking. Astrologers find it fitting that the planet of the hidden and the invisible was found this way.

## What Neptune stands for

In a chart, Neptune shows where you dream, imagine and feel connected to something larger than yourself. It is the realm of music, film, poetry, faith and compassion. It dissolves boundaries, so it can bring empathy and inspiration.

It can also bring fog. Neptune is linked with confusion, wishful thinking and seeing what we want to see. Astrologers describe it as a beautiful mist: lovely to walk in, easy to get lost in.

## Why it rules Pisces

Before Neptune's discovery, Jupiter ruled Pisces. The dreamy, compassionate, watery mood of the last sign fit the new sea god so well that astrologers made Neptune its modern ruler. Jupiter remains its traditional co-ruler.

## Neptune generations

Neptune spends about fourteen years in each sign, so it marks long generations. Astrologers read it as the dreams and ideals of an era. Neptune in Capricorn, from about 1984 to 1998, is linked with ideals about work and institutions. Neptune in Pisces, from about 2011 to 2026, is linked with streaming, virtual worlds and a wave of interest in spiritual life.

As with Uranus, astrologers give more weight to Neptune's house and its aspects to personal planets than to its sign.

## Neptune in the houses

The house of Neptune shows where life feels magical and where it may feel unclear. In the fifth house it may bring artistic gifts and romantic ideals. In the seventh it may idealise partners. In the tenth it suits careers in the arts or in helping others. In the twelfth, its natural home, it favours quiet, retreat and inner life.

## Neptune and creativity

Many musicians, painters and filmmakers have strong Neptune placements, astrologers like to point out. Whether or not the stars play a part, Neptune is a useful word for the part of us that dreams and creates. Making art, listening to music or being near water are classic ways to work with Neptune's energy.

## The real Neptune in the sky

Neptune is too faint to see without a telescope. Through one it shows as a tiny blue dot. It is the windiest planet known, with storms that race faster than the speed of sound. It takes about 165 years to orbit the Sun and completed its first orbit since discovery only in 2011. The Sky Altar app marks its place among the stars.

## Myths of Neptune

Neptune was the Roman god of the sea, the Greek Poseidon. He ruled the oceans with his trident, raised storms and calmed them, and was the father of many sea creatures. Sailors prayed to him for safe passage.
