## Venus in short

- **Rules** Taurus and Libra.
- **Symbol** a circle above a small cross: ♀.
- **Cycle** about one year around the zodiac, never more than 47 degrees from the Sun.
- **Key words** love, attraction, beauty, pleasure, harmony, money and values.

## What Venus stands for

Venus is named after the Roman goddess of love and beauty. In a chart, Venus shows what you love and how you love: what attracts you, how you show affection, what you find beautiful and what you value. It also shows how you enjoy life, from food and music to friendship.

Old astrology called Venus the lesser benefic, the smaller of the two helpful planets. Modern astrologers read it as the pull toward harmony, pleasure and connection.

## Venus through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, Venus loves boldly. It likes romance with spark, fun and grand gestures.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, Venus loves loyally and practically. It shows care through touch, gifts and acts of help.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, Venus loves through talk, wit and shared ideas. Friendship comes first.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, Venus loves deeply and tenderly. It seeks a soul bond.

## The two signs it rules

In Taurus, Venus is sensual and steady: it loves comfort, nature and lasting things. In Libra, Venus is social and refined: it loves balance, art and partnership. One sign enjoys beauty through the body, the other through the eye and the mind.

## Venus in the houses

The house of Venus shows where life feels sweetest. In the fifth house it loves romance and play. In the seventh it seeks a partner. In the second house it links with money, comfort and possessions. In the tenth it may bring charm to a career, often in the arts, design or diplomacy.

## Venus and relationships

When astrologers compare two charts, Venus is one of the first planets they look at. One person's Venus touching the other's Mars or Sun is a classic sign of attraction. The [compatibility pages](../../compatibility/libra/) use the same idea for the signs.

## The real Venus in the sky

Venus is the brightest point of light in the night sky after the Moon. It shines as the evening star after sunset for several months, then as the morning star before dawn. Many people who report a UFO have seen Venus low in the west. Over eight years, Venus traces a near perfect five petal pattern against the zodiac, which ancient sky watchers knew well.

## Myths of Venus

The Greeks called her Aphrodite, born from the foam of the sea. The Babylonians honoured the planet as Ishtar, goddess of love and war. The Maya tracked Venus with great care and timed rituals by its cycle. Sky Altar's Maya and Sumerian themes draw on those traditions.
