## The Moon in short

- **Rules** Cancer.
- **Symbol** a crescent: ☽.
- **Cycle** one trip around the zodiac in about 27 days, two and a half days in each sign.
- **Key words** feelings, needs, habits, family, care.

## What the Moon stands for

The Moon is the closest body to the Earth and the fastest moving in the chart. Astrologers read it as the inner life: feelings, moods and the instinctive reactions that come before thought. It shows what makes you feel safe, how you were cared for and how you care for others.

In old astrology the Moon was linked with mothers, the home, the body's rhythms and the public mood. Its light changes every night, so it came to stand for all that waxes and wanes in us.

## The Moon through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, feelings flare up and pass quickly. The person needs action and warmth.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, feelings are steady and practical. Comfort comes from routine and the senses.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, feelings are talked through and thought about. Friends and ideas soothe.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, feelings run deep and strong. Quiet and closeness soothe.

## The Moon in the houses

The house of the Moon shows where you look for emotional comfort. In the fourth house it is happiest at home. In the seventh it leans on partners. In the tenth it may feel tied to its public role. In the sixth it finds calm in daily routines and in helping.

## The phases of the Moon

The Moon goes from new to full and back in about 29 and a half days. Astrologers give each phase a mood:

1. **New Moon** a time to plant ideas and set intentions.
2. **Waxing crescent and first quarter** a time to build and push through.
3. **Full Moon** a time when things come to light and feelings peak.
4. **Waning Moon** a time to finish, clear out and rest.

Many gardeners and fishers in old cultures followed these phases, and people still enjoy marking them.

## The Moon and the tides

The Moon's pull raises the tides of the oceans twice a day. Ancient sky watchers noticed this long before anyone could explain it, and it helped the Moon earn its link with water, the water signs and the ebb and flow of feeling.

## The real Moon in the sky

The Moon is easy to follow against the stars. It moves about its own width every hour and a whole sign in about two and a half days. The [Today page](../../today/) shows which sign the Moon is in today. In the Sky Altar app you can point your phone at it and see the constellation behind it.

## Myths of the Moon

The Greeks honoured Selene, who drove the Moon across the night, and Artemis, the huntress. In Chinese legend Chang'e lives on the Moon with a jade rabbit. In Norse myth Máni drives the Moon, chased by a wolf. Sky Altar's culture themes paint the Moon in many of these styles.
