## The Sun in short

- **Rules** Leo.
- **Symbol** a circle with a dot in the centre: ☉.
- **Cycle** one trip around the zodiac each year, about 30 days in each sign.
- **Key words** self, will, pride, creativity, vitality.

## What the Sun stands for

The Sun is the heart of the chart, as it is the heart of the solar system. Every other planet reflects its light. Astrologers read it as your core identity: the person you are growing into, what you are proud of and what gives you energy.

In old astrology the Sun was linked with kings, fathers and leaders. Modern astrologers tend to read it more personally. It is the part of you that wants to be seen and to create something of your own. When people feel aligned with their Sun, they often say they feel alive and like themselves.

## The Sun through the elements

- **In fire signs** Aries, Leo and Sagittarius, the Sun burns bright and outgoing. It seeks action, praise and adventure.
- **In earth signs** Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn, the Sun shines through work, craft and results it can touch.
- **In air signs** Gemini, Libra and Aquarius, the Sun shines through ideas, talk and people.
- **In water signs** Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, the Sun shines through feeling, care and imagination.

Each of the twelve [sign articles](../../signs/leo/) goes deeper into what the Sun in that sign is said to bring.

## The Sun in the houses

The house of the Sun shows the area of life where you most want to shine. In the first house it shows up in your personality and looks. In the fifth house it shines through play, romance and creative work. In the tenth house it seeks a public role. In the twelfth house it may shine behind the scenes, through quiet service or spiritual life.

## The Sun and aspects

Planets that touch the Sun by aspect colour the core self strongly. A Sun joined to Venus adds charm. A Sun square Saturn may bring a sense of pressure that builds strength over time. A Sun trine Jupiter is classic good cheer. The [aspects guide](../../aspects/conjunction/) explains each angle.

## The Sun as a clock

The Sun's place in the zodiac marks the seasons. It enters Aries at the March equinox, Cancer at the June solstice, Libra at the September equinox and Capricorn at the December solstice. Many old calendars, from Persia to the Celts, held festivals at these turning points. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, but the signs keep the same dates.

The Sun's return to the exact degree it held at your birth happens once a year, near your birthday. Astrologers call the chart for that moment the solar return and read it as a theme for the year ahead.

## The real Sun in the sky

The Sun moves about one degree a day along the ecliptic, the line the zodiac follows. You cannot see the stars behind it in daylight, but the Sky Altar app can. Point your phone at the Sun and the app shows the constellation it sits in today, drawn in the style of an old culture.

## Myths of the Sun

Nearly every culture honoured the Sun. The Greeks had Helios driving his chariot across the sky, the Egyptians Ra sailing his boat, the Inca Inti as the father of their kings. Japan traces its imperial line to the sun goddess Amaterasu. Sky Altar's culture themes draw on many of these traditions.
