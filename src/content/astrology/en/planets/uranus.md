## Uranus in short

- **Rules** Aquarius in modern astrology.
- **Symbol** a circle with a dot under a cross with two side bars: ♅, or a variant drawn like a capital H.
- **Cycle** about 84 years around the zodiac, about seven years in each sign.
- **Key words** change, freedom, invention, rebellion, surprise, awakening.

## The first new planet

Uranus was the first planet found with a telescope. William Herschel spotted it in 1781 from his garden in Bath, England. It doubled the size of the known solar system overnight. Its discovery came in an age of revolutions in America and France and of the first great machines, and astrologers came to link Uranus with sudden change, invention and the urge to break free.

## What Uranus stands for

In a chart, Uranus shows where you want to be free, different and original. It is the spark of a sudden idea, the urge to rebel against old rules and the restless side that wants to try something new. It can also bring surprise: the plan that changes overnight, the unexpected meeting.

Uranus is linked with science, technology, electricity, flight and astrology itself. It speaks for the outsider and the inventor.

## Why it rules Aquarius

Before 1781, Saturn ruled Aquarius. After Herschel's discovery, astrologers noticed that Aquarius, the sign of friendship, progress and the future, matched the new planet's mood. Today Uranus is the modern ruler of Aquarius, with Saturn as its traditional co-ruler.

## Uranus generations

Uranus spends about seven years in each sign, so whole generations share the same Uranus sign. Astrologers read it as the style of change a generation brings. Uranus in Aquarius, from about 1995 to 2003, is linked with the generation that grew up with the internet. Uranus in Taurus, from about 2018 to 2026, is linked with changes in money, food and the land.

Because these placements are shared by millions, astrologers give more weight to the house of Uranus and its aspects to personal planets.

## Uranus in the houses

The house of Uranus shows where life brings surprises and where you need freedom most. In the first house it gives an original, sometimes eccentric personality. In the seventh it may bring unusual relationships or a need for space with a partner. In the tenth it suits an unusual career or many changes of path. In the eleventh it feels at home among friends and groups.

## The Uranus opposition

Around age 42, Uranus stands opposite its birth position. Popular astrology links this time with the midlife wish to shake things up: a new hobby, a new look, a bold change of path. Many people find it a time to ask what freedom means to them now.

## The real Uranus in the sky

Uranus is just at the edge of what the naked eye can see under a very dark sky. Through a telescope it shows a small blue green disk. It spins on its side, tilted almost flat, perhaps from a giant collision long ago. It takes 84 years to orbit the Sun, so a person sees it circle the zodiac about once in a lifetime. The Sky Altar app marks it among the stars.

## Myths of Uranus

Ouranos was the Greek god of the sky itself, the father of the Titans. His son Kronos, whom the Romans called Saturn, overthrew him. It is fitting that the planet beyond Saturn took the name of Saturn's father.

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. Uranus in a chart is a way to reflect on change and freedom. It does not predict sudden events. Read more about [Aquarius](../../signs/aquarius/), [Saturn](../../planets/saturn/) and the [eleventh house](../../houses/11/).
