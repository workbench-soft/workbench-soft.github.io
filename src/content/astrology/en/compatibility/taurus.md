## What Taurus looks for

Taurus wants love it can trust. It looks for a partner who is kind, loyal and steady, and who enjoys the simple pleasures of life: good food, music, touch and a comfortable home. It takes its time to commit, but once it does, it stays. Sudden changes, mixed signals and drama make it uneasy.

In astrology, signs of the same element share a natural rhythm, and earth signs find it easy to settle with each other. Water signs bring Taurus the emotional depth it quietly craves. Fire and air signs move faster, which can feel exciting or tiring to the Bull. Each pairing below is a broad sketch, since a full chart comparison shows far more.

## Taurus and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Taurus and Aries

Aries rushes in where Taurus wants to look first. The Bull can find the Ram restless, and the Ram can find the Bull slow. Still, Aries brings spark and Taurus brings comfort. When Taurus enjoys the adventure and Aries enjoys the calm, the match is warm and lasting.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Taurus and Leo

A square between two fixed signs, both loyal and both stubborn. Leo loves glamour and praise, Taurus loves comfort and quality. Both enjoy the good life and a beautiful home. Neither likes to give way, so small compromises matter more than big promises.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Taurus and Sagittarius

A puzzling mix. Sagittarius wants to roam, Taurus wants to put down roots. The Archer can widen the Bull's world, and the Bull can give the Archer a home to return to. The pair needs clear agreements about freedom and money.

## Taurus and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Taurus and Taurus

Two Bulls make a cosy, sensual and very stable pair. They share values, tastes and a love of routine. Life together can be peaceful and pleasant. The risk is getting stuck. Planning a new experience now and then keeps the field fresh.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Taurus and Virgo

An easy earth trine. Virgo's care and Taurus' calm fit well. Both are practical, loyal and good with money and work. Virgo helps Taurus improve, and Taurus helps Virgo relax. This is one of the most dependable matches in the zodiac.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Taurus and Capricorn

Another natural earth trine. Both value security, hard work and lasting results. Capricorn brings ambition, Taurus brings warmth and comfort. Together they build a solid life step by step. They should remember to make time for fun as well as goals.

## Taurus and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Taurus and Gemini

Neighbours with different speeds. Gemini loves change and talk, Taurus loves calm and touch. Gemini can bring humour and new ideas into Taurus' life, and Taurus can give Gemini a place to land. Patience and a sense of humour are the keys here.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Taurus and Libra

Both signs are ruled by Venus, so they share a love of beauty, art and romance. Libra is social and airy, Taurus is earthy and private. They enjoy pleasant evenings and a lovely home. Decisions can drag, since Libra weighs and Taurus waits.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Taurus and Aquarius

A square between two fixed signs with very different values. Taurus wants security, Aquarius wants freedom and change. They may clash over routine and money. If each respects the other's needs, they can learn a great deal and form a loyal, unusual bond.

## Taurus and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Taurus and Cancer

A sweet sextile. Both love home, food and family, and both are loyal. Cancer feels safe with Taurus, and Taurus feels cherished by Cancer. This can be one of the coziest pairings in the zodiac. Moods and stubbornness are the only clouds.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Taurus and Scorpio

Opposite signs with strong mutual pull. Both are loyal, sensual and determined. Scorpio brings depth, Taurus brings steadiness. Jealousy and stubbornness can flare, so trust and open talk are vital. When it works, the bond is deep and lasting.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Taurus and Pisces

A gentle sextile. Pisces brings dreams, art and tenderness, Taurus brings comfort and a safe base. Taurus keeps Pisces grounded, and Pisces softens Taurus. They share a love of music and beauty. This pairing often feels quietly romantic.
