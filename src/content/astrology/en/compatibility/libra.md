## What Libra looks for

Libra is the sign of partnership, and love matters to it a great deal. It looks for a partner who is kind, fair, well mannered and good company, someone to share art, music, friends and long conversations with. It gives charm and consideration in return. Rudeness, open conflict and one sided effort leave it unhappy.

In astrology, Libra flows easily with the other air signs and with fire signs, whose energy it balances. Earth and water signs offer depth and stability, though Libra may need to adjust to their rhythm. These are broad Sun sign sketches, and a comparison of two full charts goes much further.

## Libra and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Libra and Aries

Opposite signs with a strong pull. Aries is bold and self directed, Libra is graceful and partner minded. Aries teaches Libra to act, and Libra teaches Aries to consider others. The attraction is real, and so is the learning.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Libra and Leo

A glamorous sextile. Leo loves Libra's charm, and Libra admires Leo's warmth and style. They share a love of beauty, parties and romance. Both enjoy compliments, so they rarely run short of them.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Libra and Sagittarius

A cheerful sextile. Sagittarius brings adventure and humour, Libra brings grace and company. They love travel, culture and good talks. Sagittarius' bluntness may jar Libra's sense of tact, but both forgive easily.

## Libra and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Libra and Taurus

Two Venus ruled signs who share a love of beauty and comfort. Taurus is private and steady, Libra is social and light. They enjoy fine food, art and a lovely home. Decisions may take a long time, since neither likes to rush.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Libra and Virgo

Neighbours with refined tastes. Virgo brings care and practical help, Libra brings ease and charm. They make a polished pair. Virgo's critical eye should stay kind, and Libra should share its real opinions.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Libra and Capricorn

A square between two cardinal signs who both like to lead in their own way. Capricorn focuses on goals, Libra on relationships. Together they can make an elegant, successful team. Making time for romance keeps Capricorn's ambition from taking over.

## Libra and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Libra and Gemini

An easy air trine. Gemini's wit and Libra's charm make sparkling conversation. They share friends, books and outings. Both may avoid hard topics, so a little courage in honest talk keeps the bond deep.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Libra and Libra

Two Libras make a gracious, harmonious couple. They share a love of beauty, fairness and peace. The risk is that neither wants to decide or to raise a problem. Taking turns at choosing keeps the scales from swinging forever.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Libra and Aquarius

A natural air trine. Both care about people, ideas and fairness. Aquarius brings originality, Libra brings grace. They make good friends and good partners. Aquarius should share its warmth, and Libra should give Aquarius room.

## Libra and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Libra and Cancer

A square between two cardinal signs who both value relationships. Cancer wants emotional closeness, Libra wants harmony and a social life. Both are considerate. Learning the other's way of showing love is the main task here.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Libra and Scorpio

Neighbours with very different moods. Scorpio is intense and private, Libra is light and social. Scorpio's depth fascinates Libra, and Libra's charm soothes Scorpio. Trust grows when Libra is open and Scorpio is gentle.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Libra and Pisces

A romantic, artistic pair. Both love beauty, music and kindness. Pisces brings dreams, Libra brings grace. They can create a gentle, lovely life. Both avoid conflict, so problems may need a gentle push to come into the open.
