## What Sagittarius looks for

Sagittarius wants a partner who is also a friend and a fellow traveller. It looks for humour, honesty, curiosity and a love of adventure. It gives warmth, generosity and fun. Jealousy, tight rules and a small, closed world make it want to run for the horizon.

Astrologers say Sagittarius flows easily with the other fire signs and with air signs, who share its love of ideas. Earth and water signs offer roots and depth, though the Archer may need to adjust its pace. These notes look at Sun signs only, and a full chart comparison tells far more.

## Sagittarius and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Sagittarius and Aries

An adventurous fire trine. Both love freedom, honesty and action. They can travel, play sport and laugh together for years. Someone needs to keep track of practical matters, but the friendship at the heart of this pair is strong.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Sagittarius and Leo

A joyful fire trine. Leo brings warmth and loyalty, Sagittarius brings humour and adventure. Both are generous and optimistic. Leo wants attention, so the Archer should show its devotion often.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Sagittarius and Sagittarius

Two Archers make an easy going, adventurous couple. They share a love of travel, ideas and freedom. The risk is that neither wants to commit to plans. A shared dream, like a big trip, gives the pair a direction.

## Sagittarius and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Sagittarius and Taurus

A puzzling mix. Taurus wants roots and routine, Sagittarius wants open roads. Taurus can give the Archer a cosy home, and the Archer can widen the Bull's world. Clear agreements about money and freedom help.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Sagittarius and Virgo

A square between two mutable signs. Virgo looks at details, Sagittarius at the big picture. Both love learning. Virgo may worry about the Archer's carelessness, and the Archer may find Virgo too cautious. Patience brings real growth.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Sagittarius and Capricorn

Neighbours with different styles. Capricorn plans, Sagittarius dreams. Capricorn can help Sagittarius reach its goals, and Sagittarius can help Capricorn relax and laugh. Respect for each other's pace makes this pair work.

## Sagittarius and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Sagittarius and Gemini

Opposite signs and natural partners in curiosity. Gemini collects facts, Sagittarius seeks meaning. They talk, travel and learn together. Both love freedom, so commitment needs a clear choice from both sides.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Sagittarius and Libra

A cheerful sextile. Libra brings grace and company, Sagittarius brings humour and adventure. They love culture, travel and people. Libra may need tact from the frank Archer, but both forgive quickly.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Sagittarius and Aquarius

A free spirited sextile. Both value independence, ideas and friendship. They can share causes, travels and long talks about the future. This pair gives each other room, which keeps the bond fresh.

## Sagittarius and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Sagittarius and Cancer

Cancer wants home and closeness, Sagittarius wants freedom and travel. The Archer's frankness can hurt the Crab. Yet Cancer gives Sagittarius a place to belong, and Sagittarius widens Cancer's world. Gentle honesty helps.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Sagittarius and Scorpio

Neighbours with very different moods. Scorpio is intense and private, Sagittarius is open and light. Scorpio may want more depth than the Archer shows. Shared interest in big questions can draw them together.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Sagittarius and Pisces

A square between two mutable signs, both ruled by Jupiter in the old tradition. Both are idealistic and kind. Sagittarius seeks truth, Pisces seeks meaning through feeling. They can share spiritual or artistic dreams. Clear plans keep them on course.
