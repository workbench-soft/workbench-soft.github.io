## What Aquarius looks for

For Aquarius, love starts as friendship. It wants a partner who respects its freedom, shares its interests or causes and enjoys talking about ideas and the future. It is loyal and caring in its own cool way. Possessive behaviour, rigid rules and narrow thinking push it away.

Astrologers say Aquarius flows easily with the other air signs and with fire signs, who bring energy to its ideas. Earth and water signs offer stability and depth, though they may want more closeness or routine than Aquarius at first. These notes are Sun sign sketches, and comparing two full charts goes much further.

## Aquarius and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Aquarius and Aries

A lively sextile. Aries brings action, Aquarius brings vision. Both value independence and new ideas. They make great friends and exciting partners. Aries should respect Aquarius' need for space, and Aquarius should show warmth.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Aquarius and Leo

Opposite signs: the group and the heart. Leo wants personal devotion, Aquarius wants freedom and a cause. They are drawn to each other's differences. Sharing the spotlight and showing warmth turn this into a creative bond.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Aquarius and Sagittarius

A free spirited sextile. Both love freedom, ideas and adventure. They can talk about the future for hours and travel together happily. They give each other room, which keeps the relationship fresh.

## Aquarius and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Aquarius and Taurus

A square between two fixed signs with different values. Taurus wants security, Aquarius wants change. Both are loyal and stubborn. If they respect each other's needs, they learn a great deal and build an unusual, lasting bond.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Aquarius and Virgo

A thoughtful mix. Virgo works on details, Aquarius dreams of the big picture. Both want to improve the world. They can share projects and causes. Virgo may find Aquarius distant, and Aquarius may find Virgo fussy.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Aquarius and Capricorn

Neighbours that share the old ruler Saturn. Capricorn honours tradition, Aquarius seeks progress. Both are serious about their goals. With respect, they combine solid structure with fresh ideas.

## Aquarius and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Aquarius and Gemini

A natural air trine. Both love ideas, talk and friends. They can be best friends and partners at the same time. Each gives the other plenty of room. Sharing feelings as well as thoughts deepens the bond.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Aquarius and Libra

An easy air trine. Libra brings grace, Aquarius brings originality. Both care about fairness and people. They enjoy a busy social life and good conversation. Warmth from Aquarius and space from Libra keep them close.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Aquarius and Aquarius

Two Water Bearers make an original, friendly and independent couple. They share causes, hobbies and a love of the new. The risk is too much distance. Making time for closeness keeps this unusual pair warm.

## Aquarius and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Aquarius and Cancer

An unusual pairing. Cancer is warm and attached, Aquarius is cool and free. Cancer may feel lonely, and Aquarius may feel crowded. Yet both care deeply about people, and shared projects can bring them close.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Aquarius and Scorpio

A square between two fixed signs with strong views. Scorpio wants depth and closeness, Aquarius wants freedom and ideas. Both are loyal and determined. A shared cause helps them meet in the middle.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Aquarius and Pisces

Neighbours with dreams of a better world. Aquarius thinks about humanity, Pisces feels for it. They can share ideals and creative projects. Aquarius should offer warmth, and Pisces should share its needs clearly.
