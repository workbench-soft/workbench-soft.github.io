## What Scorpio looks for

Scorpio loves deeply or not at all. It wants a partner it can trust completely, who is honest, loyal and unafraid of real feelings. It gives intense devotion and protects the people it loves. Small talk, half truths and games make it pull back fast.

Astrologers usually pair Scorpio well with the other water signs, who share its depth, and with earth signs, whose steadiness it respects. Fire and air signs bring energy and lightness, which may challenge or refresh the Scorpion. These sketches look at Sun signs only, and a full chart comparison shows much more.

## Scorpio and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Scorpio and Aries

Both signs share the old ruler Mars, so the passion is strong. Aries fights openly, Scorpio strategically. Power struggles can flare. When they trust each other, they make a bold, loyal and unstoppable pair.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Scorpio and Leo

A square between two fixed, strong willed signs. Leo is open and proud, Scorpio is private and intense. The attraction is powerful. Respect, loyalty and avoiding jealousy keep this dramatic pair together.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Scorpio and Sagittarius

Neighbours with very different styles. Sagittarius is open, light and free, Scorpio is deep and private. Scorpio may want more closeness than the Archer gives. Shared interest in big questions, travel or philosophy can bridge the gap.

## Scorpio and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Scorpio and Taurus

Opposite signs with a magnetic pull. Both are loyal, sensual and stubborn. Taurus brings calm, Scorpio brings depth. Jealousy and fixed opinions can clash, but when trust is strong this bond can last a lifetime.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Scorpio and Virgo

A thoughtful sextile. Both are perceptive, private and loyal. Virgo brings clarity and care, Scorpio brings passion and depth. They trust slowly but deeply, and they work well together on serious goals.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Scorpio and Capricorn

A powerful sextile. Both are ambitious, determined and loyal. Capricorn brings structure, Scorpio brings emotional depth. They make a formidable team. Both can hide their feelings, so honest moments matter.

## Scorpio and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Scorpio and Gemini

An intriguing mix. Gemini is curious and light, Scorpio is intense and private. Scorpio may find Gemini hard to pin down, and Gemini may find Scorpio heavy. A shared love of mysteries and people can bring them together.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Scorpio and Libra

Neighbours with different moods. Libra is social and charming, Scorpio is private and deep. Libra's grace soothes Scorpio, and Scorpio's depth intrigues Libra. Honesty and patience help trust grow.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Scorpio and Aquarius

A square between two fixed signs with strong views. Aquarius values freedom and ideas, Scorpio values closeness and feeling. They can clash, but both are loyal and determined. Shared causes or projects help them find common ground.

## Scorpio and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Scorpio and Cancer

A deep water trine. Both feel intensely and value loyalty. Cancer nurtures, Scorpio protects. Together they create a safe, close world. Moods and jealousy are the only clouds, and trust clears them.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Scorpio and Scorpio

Two Scorpions understand each other's depths without words. The bond is passionate, loyal and intense. The risk is power struggles or long silences. Openness and forgiveness let this powerful pair grow together.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Scorpio and Pisces

A gentle water trine. Pisces brings tenderness and imagination, Scorpio brings strength and devotion. They feel deeply understood with each other. Scorpio protects Pisces, and Pisces softens Scorpio.
