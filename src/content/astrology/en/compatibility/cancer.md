## What Cancer looks for

Cancer wants a partner who feels like home. It looks for kindness, loyalty and emotional honesty, and it gives deep care in return. It moves carefully at first, testing whether it is safe to open its shell. Coldness, teasing about feelings and broken promises hurt it more than it shows.

In astrology, water signs share Cancer's emotional language, and earth signs give it the security it needs. Fire and air signs can bring excitement and fresh ideas, though Cancer may need extra reassurance with them. Treat these notes as broad sketches. A comparison of two full birth charts shows much more.

## Cancer and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Cancer and Aries

A square between two cardinal signs. Aries charges ahead, Cancer protects the nest. Aries' bluntness can sting, and Cancer's moods can puzzle the Ram. Both are loyal and brave in their own way, and with tenderness they make a strong family team.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Cancer and Leo

Neighbours who both have big hearts. Leo shines in public, Cancer cares in private. Leo enjoys Cancer's devotion, and Cancer enjoys Leo's warmth. Both need to feel appreciated. Regular praise and quiet time at home keep them happy.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Cancer and Sagittarius

A tricky mix. Sagittarius wants open roads, Cancer wants a cosy home. The Archer's frank humour can hurt the Crab. Yet Sagittarius can widen Cancer's world, and Cancer can give Sagittarius a place to belong. Clear talk about freedom helps.

## Cancer and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Cancer and Taurus

A sweet sextile. Both love home, food and family, and both value loyalty. Taurus gives Cancer steadiness, and Cancer gives Taurus tender care. This is often a cosy, lasting match with a full fridge and a warm kitchen.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Cancer and Virgo

A helpful sextile. Virgo shows love by doing, Cancer by caring. Both are thoughtful and loyal. Virgo can calm Cancer's worries with practical help, and Cancer can soften Virgo's self criticism. Gentle words keep the bond strong.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Cancer and Capricorn

Opposite signs: home and career, feeling and structure. Each has what the other lacks. Capricorn provides security, Cancer provides warmth. They can build a solid family life. Both may hide feelings, so honest talk is important.

## Cancer and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Cancer and Gemini

Neighbours with different needs. Gemini wants talk and variety, Cancer wants closeness and safety. Gemini can lift Cancer's mood with humour, and Cancer can teach Gemini about feelings. Patience on both sides makes this match warm.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Cancer and Libra

A square between two cardinal signs who both value relationships. Libra wants harmony and a social life, Cancer wants intimacy and home. They are both kind and considerate. Learning each other's way of showing love is the main task.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Cancer and Aquarius

An unusual match. Aquarius is cool and independent, Cancer is warm and attached. Cancer may feel lonely, and Aquarius may feel crowded. Yet both care about people in their own way, and shared projects can bring them close.

## Cancer and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Cancer and Cancer

Two Crabs understand each other's moods without words. They build a deeply caring home and protect each other fiercely. The risk is that both withdraw at the same time. Taking turns at being the strong one keeps this tender bond healthy.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Cancer and Scorpio

A deep water trine. Both feel intensely and value loyalty above all. Scorpio protects, Cancer nurtures. This pairing can feel like coming home. Jealousy and sulks are the only storms, and trust calms them.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Cancer and Pisces

A gentle water trine. Both are kind, intuitive and romantic. Pisces brings dreams, Cancer brings care. They create a soft, safe world together. Someone needs to watch the bills and the calendar, but the emotional bond is strong.
