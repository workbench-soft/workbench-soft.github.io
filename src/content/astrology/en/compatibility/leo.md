## What Leo looks for

Leo loves with a big, generous heart. It wants a partner it can be proud of, who admires it openly and enjoys romance, fun and a bit of glamour. It is loyal and protective once it commits. Coldness, criticism in public and being ignored hurt the Lion's pride.

Astrologers usually pair Leo easily with the other fire signs, and with air signs that fan its flame. Earth and water signs bring steadiness and depth, though the pace and style may differ. Use these notes as broad sketches. Two full birth charts compared side by side say much more than Sun signs.

## Leo and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Leo and Aries

A bright fire trine. Aries brings courage, Leo brings warmth and flair. They enjoy adventure, celebration and honest talk. Both like to lead, so sharing the spotlight keeps this exciting pair happy.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Leo and Leo

Two Lions make a glamorous, warm and generous couple. They love parties, romance and grand gestures. Both need praise, so they must remember to give as much as they hope to get. When they do, they shine together.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Leo and Sagittarius

A joyful fire trine. Both are optimistic, playful and generous. Sagittarius brings adventure and humour, Leo brings loyalty and heart. Leo may want more attention than the free spirited Archer gives, but laughter heals most rifts.

## Leo and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Leo and Taurus

A square between two fixed signs. Both enjoy luxury, comfort and loyalty, and both can be stubborn. Leo wants applause, Taurus wants peace. They build a lovely home if they learn to bend now and then.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Leo and Virgo

Neighbours with different styles. Leo shines, Virgo serves. Virgo can help Leo polish its plans, and Leo can help Virgo believe in itself. Virgo's critiques need a warm wrapping, since the Lion takes criticism to heart.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Leo and Capricorn

Two ambitious signs with different methods. Leo leads with charisma, Capricorn with strategy. Together they can build an impressive life. Capricorn may seem cold to Leo, and Leo may seem showy to Capricorn. Shared goals bridge the gap.

## Leo and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Leo and Gemini

A playful sextile. Gemini's wit amuses Leo, and Leo's warmth charms Gemini. They enjoy a busy social life full of games and stories. Leo needs loyalty, so Gemini should show that its heart stays home.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Leo and Libra

A graceful sextile. Libra admires Leo's style, and Leo loves Libra's charm. They share a love of beauty, art and romance. This pair often looks good together and enjoys the good life. Both like flattery, so honesty needs care.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Leo and Aquarius

Opposite signs: the heart and the group. Leo wants personal devotion, Aquarius wants freedom and a cause. They are drawn to each other's differences. When Leo shares the stage and Aquarius shares its warmth, they make a creative, loyal team.

## Leo and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Leo and Cancer

Neighbours who both care deeply about loved ones. Cancer nurtures, Leo protects and celebrates. Cancer enjoys Leo's warmth, and Leo enjoys Cancer's devotion. They need to balance Leo's social life with Cancer's love of home.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Leo and Scorpio

A square between two fixed and powerful signs. The attraction is strong and so is the will on both sides. Leo is open, Scorpio is private. Loyalty is deep, but so can be jealousy. Respect and trust keep this intense pair together.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Leo and Pisces

A dreamy mix. Pisces adores Leo's warmth, and Leo enjoys Pisces' tenderness. Both are creative and romantic. Leo may need to be gentle with Pisces' feelings, and Pisces may need to speak up. Art and shared dreams bring them close.
