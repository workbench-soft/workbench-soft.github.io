## What Capricorn looks for

Capricorn takes love seriously. It wants a partner who is reliable, honest and has goals of their own, someone to build a life with over many years. It shows love through loyalty, planning and practical support, and its dry humour comes out with people it trusts. Flakiness and drama push it away.

Astrologers often pair Capricorn with the other earth signs, who share its patience, and with water signs, who warm its heart. Fire and air signs bring spark and ideas that can either inspire or unsettle the Sea Goat. These are broad Sun sign sketches, and a full chart comparison reveals much more.

## Capricorn and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Capricorn and Aries

A square between two ambitious cardinal signs. Aries races, Capricorn climbs. Both like to be in charge. If they share a goal and split the roles, they become a powerful team. Otherwise, battles over control are likely.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Capricorn and Leo

Two strong signs with different styles. Leo leads with charm, Capricorn with method. Leo brings warmth to Capricorn's world, and Capricorn brings structure to Leo's dreams. Mutual respect and praise keep them close.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Capricorn and Sagittarius

Neighbours with different rhythms. Sagittarius dreams big, Capricorn plans carefully. Capricorn helps the Archer reach the target, and the Archer reminds Capricorn to enjoy the journey. Patience makes this an educational match.

## Capricorn and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Capricorn and Taurus

A natural earth trine. Both value security, loyalty and hard work. Taurus brings comfort and pleasure, Capricorn brings ambition and structure. They build a solid, lasting life. Scheduling time for fun keeps it sweet.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Capricorn and Virgo

A strong earth trine. Both are responsible, practical and loyal. Virgo refines the details, Capricorn steers the course. They work well together and trust each other. A little more play keeps the romance alive.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Capricorn and Capricorn

Two Sea Goats make a determined, loyal and successful pair. They share values, goals and a love of order. The risk is all work and no rest. Planning holidays as carefully as careers keeps this partnership happy.

## Capricorn and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Capricorn and Gemini

A careful pairing. Gemini lives for variety, Capricorn for long term plans. Capricorn can help Gemini focus, and Gemini can help Capricorn laugh. Respect for each other's different rhythms is essential.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Capricorn and Libra

A square between two cardinal signs who both like to lead. Libra leads through charm and diplomacy, Capricorn through structure. Together they make an elegant and successful team. Romance needs space alongside ambition.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Capricorn and Aquarius

Neighbours that share the old ruler Saturn. Capricorn values tradition, Aquarius values change. They both take their ideals seriously. If they respect each other's views, they can build something both solid and new.

## Capricorn and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Capricorn and Cancer

Opposite signs: career and home. Capricorn provides security, Cancer provides warmth. Each has what the other lacks. They can build a strong family together. Both tend to hide feelings, so open talk matters.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Capricorn and Scorpio

A powerful sextile. Both are determined, loyal and private. Scorpio brings depth and passion, Capricorn brings stability and structure. They make a strong, quietly devoted team that can weather many storms.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Capricorn and Pisces

A gentle sextile. Pisces brings dreams and compassion, Capricorn brings structure and protection. Capricorn grounds Pisces, and Pisces softens Capricorn. This pair can be surprisingly tender and supportive.
