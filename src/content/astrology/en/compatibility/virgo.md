## What Virgo looks for

Virgo wants a partner it can trust and respect. It looks for kindness, honesty, good manners and a sense that the two of them can build a useful, well run life together. It shows love through small acts of help and attention. Carelessness, chaos and broken promises wear on it.

Astrologers often pair Virgo with the other earth signs, who share its practical sense, and with water signs, whose warmth helps Virgo relax. Fire and air signs bring energy and ideas, which can be exciting or unsettling for the Maiden. These notes are broad sketches based on Sun signs, and a full chart comparison adds far more.

## Virgo and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Virgo and Aries

Aries acts on impulse, Virgo plans each step. Virgo may see Aries as reckless, and Aries may see Virgo as fussy. Yet Aries can give Virgo courage, and Virgo can help Aries finish what it starts. Respect turns this into a useful team.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Virgo and Leo

Neighbours with different gifts. Leo wants to shine, Virgo wants to help. Virgo can make Leo's ideas work, and Leo can make Virgo feel special. Virgo should praise as often as it corrects, and Leo should value Virgo's quiet work.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Virgo and Sagittarius

A square between two mutable signs who both love to learn. Sagittarius sees the big picture, Virgo sees the details. They can teach each other a great deal. Sagittarius' carelessness and Virgo's worry can clash, so patience is needed.

## Virgo and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Virgo and Taurus

A comfortable earth trine. Both are practical, loyal and fond of a calm life. Taurus helps Virgo relax and enjoy, and Virgo helps Taurus improve and organise. This is a steady, dependable match.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Virgo and Virgo

Two Virgos understand each other's need for order and usefulness. They make a tidy, thoughtful home. The risk is too much criticism, of each other or of themselves. Remembering to laugh and rest keeps this pair gentle.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Virgo and Capricorn

A strong earth trine. Both are hard working, responsible and loyal. Capricorn sets the goal, Virgo perfects the plan. They build a secure life together. Making space for fun and feelings brings warmth to their efficient partnership.

## Virgo and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Virgo and Gemini

Two Mercury ruled signs who love to think and talk. Gemini jumps between ideas, Virgo sorts them. They can make a clever team. Virgo may find Gemini scattered, and Gemini may find Virgo critical. Kind words make the difference.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Virgo and Libra

Neighbours who both value grace and good manners. Libra brings charm and social ease, Virgo brings care and practical help. They make a refined pair. Decisions may take time, and honest talk about small issues stops them from piling up.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Virgo and Aquarius

An unusual pair of thinkers. Aquarius dreams of a better world, Virgo works to make it real. They can share causes and projects. Aquarius may seem detached, and Virgo may seem picky. Shared goals help them meet in the middle.

## Virgo and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Virgo and Cancer

A caring sextile. Cancer nurtures, Virgo helps. Both are loyal and thoughtful. Virgo calms Cancer's worries with practical support, and Cancer warms Virgo's heart. This can be a quiet, devoted match.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Virgo and Scorpio

A thoughtful sextile. Both are perceptive, private and loyal. Scorpio brings depth and passion, Virgo brings care and clarity. They trust slowly but deeply. Together they make a strong team in love and work.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Virgo and Pisces

Opposite signs: order and flow. Pisces dreams, Virgo plans. Each has what the other needs. Virgo gives Pisces structure, and Pisces gives Virgo compassion and imagination. Gentleness in criticism keeps this tender bond healthy.
