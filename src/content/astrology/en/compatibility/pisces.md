## What Pisces looks for

Pisces dreams of a soulmate. It wants a partner who is kind, patient and willing to share feelings openly, someone who understands its moods and its dreams. It gives tenderness, imagination and deep compassion. Harshness, coldness and cynicism hurt it deeply.

Astrologers usually pair Pisces with the other water signs, who share its emotional depth, and with earth signs, who give it a safe harbour. Fire and air signs bring energy and ideas that can either inspire or overwhelm the Fishes. These sketches look at Sun signs alone, and a comparison of full charts says much more.

## Pisces and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Pisces and Aries

Neighbours with different natures. Aries is bold and direct, Pisces is gentle and dreamy. Aries can protect Pisces, and Pisces can soften Aries. The pair works when Aries slows down and Pisces says what it needs.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Pisces and Leo

A dreamy mix. Leo's warmth draws Pisces in, and Pisces' tenderness touches Leo's heart. Both are creative and romantic. Leo should be gentle with Pisces' feelings, and Pisces should speak up for itself.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Pisces and Sagittarius

A square between two mutable signs, both ruled by Jupiter in the old tradition. Both are idealistic and generous. Sagittarius seeks truth, Pisces seeks connection. Shared spiritual or artistic dreams bring them together.

## Pisces and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Pisces and Taurus

A gentle sextile. Taurus gives Pisces comfort and safety, and Pisces gives Taurus romance and imagination. They share a love of music and beauty. This pairing often feels quietly happy.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Pisces and Virgo

Opposite signs: flow and order. Virgo plans, Pisces dreams. Each has what the other needs. Virgo gives Pisces structure, and Pisces gives Virgo compassion. Gentleness keeps this tender bond healthy.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Pisces and Capricorn

A supportive sextile. Capricorn brings stability and protection, Pisces brings warmth and imagination. Capricorn grounds Pisces, and Pisces softens Capricorn. This pair can be surprisingly devoted.

## Pisces and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Pisces and Gemini

A square between two mutable signs. Both are adaptable and imaginative, but Gemini thinks while Pisces feels. They can enjoy stories, music and art together. Clear plans and gentle honesty keep them from drifting.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Pisces and Libra

A romantic, artistic pair. Both love beauty and kindness. Libra brings grace, Pisces brings dreams. They create a gentle, lovely world. Both avoid conflict, so problems need a gentle push into the open.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Pisces and Aquarius

Neighbours with ideals. Aquarius thinks about a better world, Pisces feels for the people in it. They can share causes and creative work. Aquarius should show its warmth, and Pisces should share its needs clearly.

## Pisces and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Pisces and Cancer

A gentle water trine. Both are kind, intuitive and romantic. Cancer brings care, Pisces brings dreams. They build a soft, safe world together. One of them should keep an eye on the bills.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Pisces and Scorpio

A deep water trine. Scorpio brings strength and devotion, Pisces brings tenderness and imagination. They feel deeply understood with each other. Scorpio protects Pisces, and Pisces softens Scorpio.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Pisces and Pisces

Two Fishes share a rich inner world of dreams, art and feeling. They understand each other without words. The risk is drifting away from practical life. A shared routine and a trusted friend help them stay afloat.
