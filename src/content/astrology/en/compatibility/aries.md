## What Aries looks for

Aries loves with energy and honesty. It wants a partner who can keep up, enjoys a little friendly rivalry and says what they mean. It falls fast and shows it. It needs freedom to act and a sense that the relationship is moving somewhere. Long silences, games and slow, vague plans tend to frustrate it.

Astrologers read sign compatibility through the elements and the angles between signs. Signs of the same element form a trine and flow easily. Fire and air signs feed each other, as do earth and water. Signs that square or oppose each other bring more friction, which can be exciting and can teach a lot. Every pairing can work. A full comparison of two birth charts says much more than the Sun signs alone.

## Aries and the fire signs

<a id="aries"></a>

### Aries and Aries

Two Rams make a fast, playful, sometimes fiery couple. They understand each other's need for action and honesty. The challenge is that both want to lead. Taking turns at choosing the plan, and laughing after a clash, keeps this lively match warm.

<a id="leo"></a>

### Aries and Leo

A classic fire trine. Leo admires Aries' courage, and Aries loves Leo's warmth and style. Together they enjoy adventure, parties and big gestures. Pride is the only real snag: both like to be right, and a sincere compliment goes a long way.

<a id="sagittarius"></a>

### Aries and Sagittarius

An easy, adventurous trine. Both love freedom, travel and honesty, and neither wants to be fenced in. The pair can feel like best friends on a long road trip. They do best when someone remembers to book the tickets and pay the bills.

## Aries and the earth signs

<a id="taurus"></a>

### Aries and Taurus

Neighbours on the zodiac wheel with very different paces. Aries wants to go now, Taurus wants to be sure first. Aries brings excitement to Taurus, and Taurus offers Aries a calm, sensual base. Patience from Aries and flexibility from Taurus turn this into a warm match.

<a id="virgo"></a>

### Aries and Virgo

An unusual pairing. Aries acts on instinct while Virgo thinks things through. Virgo may see Aries as rash, and Aries may find Virgo picky. Yet the mix of drive and method works well in shared projects, and respect for each other's strengths helps love grow.

<a id="capricorn"></a>

### Aries and Capricorn

A square between two cardinal signs, both ambitious and strong willed. Aries sprints, Capricorn climbs. Clashes about control are common. If they share a goal and divide the roles, they become a formidable team with a real sense of achievement.

## Aries and the air signs

<a id="gemini"></a>

### Aries and Gemini

A lively sextile. Gemini's quick wit keeps Aries entertained, and Aries' energy gets Gemini's plans off the ground. Both love new things and hate boredom. The pair needs some steadiness from outside, but rarely runs out of fun.

<a id="libra"></a>

### Aries and Libra

Opposite signs and a classic attraction of opposites. Aries is direct and self driven, Libra is charming and partner minded. Each has what the other lacks. Aries learns tact and teamwork, and Libra learns to ask for what it wants.

<a id="aquarius"></a>

### Aries and Aquarius

A friendly sextile full of ideas. Both value independence and like to break new ground. Aquarius brings vision, Aries brings action. They make great friends and exciting partners, as long as Aries respects Aquarius' need for space.

## Aries and the water signs

<a id="cancer"></a>

### Aries and Cancer

A square between fire and water. Aries is bold and blunt, Cancer is tender and protective. Aries may hurt Cancer without noticing, and Cancer's moods may puzzle Aries. Both are loyal, though, and with gentleness they can build a brave and caring home.

<a id="scorpio"></a>

### Aries and Scorpio

Both signs share the old ruler Mars, so this pair is passionate and intense. Aries fights in the open, Scorpio holds its feelings close. Trust and honesty matter a great deal. When they align, few couples match their energy and loyalty.

<a id="pisces"></a>

### Aries and Pisces

Neighbours with very different natures. Aries is direct and practical about action, Pisces is dreamy and gentle. Aries can protect Pisces, and Pisces can soften Aries. The pair works when Aries slows down and Pisces says clearly what it needs.
