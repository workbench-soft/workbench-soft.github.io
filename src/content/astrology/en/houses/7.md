## The 7th house in short

- **Natural sign** Libra.
- **Natural ruler** Venus.
- **Type** angular, one of the four strongest houses.
- **Key words** partners, marriage, close friends, business partners, contracts, open rivals.

## What the 7th house covers

The seventh house begins at the Descendant, the point of the zodiac setting in the west at the moment of birth. It sits exactly opposite the Ascendant. If the first house is me, the seventh is you: the people we meet one to one.

Astrologers read it as committed relationships of every kind: marriage and long term love, business partners, close friends and anyone you sign an agreement with. Old texts also put open enemies here, since a rival is another kind of partner who mirrors us. The house shows what you look for in others and what you learn through them.

## The sign on the 7th house

The sign on the Descendant is always opposite your rising sign. Astrologers say we are drawn to qualities we have not yet grown in ourselves:

- **Fire signs** attraction to bold, lively, confident partners.
- **Earth signs** attraction to steady, practical, loyal partners.
- **Air signs** attraction to witty, social, thoughtful partners.
- **Water signs** attraction to tender, deep, caring partners.

The ruler of that sign adds where you may meet partners or what you share with them.

## Planets in the 7th house

- **Sun** identity shaped through partnership. Others play a big role.
- **Moon** a need for emotional closeness and a caring partner.
- **Mercury** a partner who is also a good friend to talk with.
- **Venus** at home here. Charm, harmony and a love of being a couple.
- **Mars** lively, passionate partnerships, sometimes with friendly sparks.
- **Jupiter** generous, wise or worldly partners.
- **Saturn** serious commitments, often slow to start and built to last.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** unusual bonds, idealised love or deep, life changing ties.

## Seeing yourself through others

Many astrologers read the first and seventh houses as a pair. What we admire or dislike strongly in other people can point to parts of ourselves we have not owned yet. The seventh house invites you to notice what partners bring out in you.

## The 7th house in the sky

The seventh house begins at the western horizon. Planets there were setting at the moment of birth. In the Sky Altar app, face west to see the part of the zodiac that is setting now.

## When planets pass through

When planets travel through your seventh house, astrologers read it as a time when other people take centre stage. Venus passing through may bring pleasant meetings. Saturn passing through may ask for honest talks about commitment.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Partners** what qualities do you look for in a close partner?
- **Balance** where do you give more, and where do you take more?
- **Mirrors** which traits in others draw you in or annoy you most?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. The seventh house does not predict marriage, divorce or who you will meet. Read more about [Libra](../../signs/libra/), [Venus](../../planets/venus/) and the [first house](../../houses/1/), its opposite.
