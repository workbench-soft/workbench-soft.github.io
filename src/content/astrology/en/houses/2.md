## The 2nd house in short

- **Natural sign** Taurus.
- **Natural ruler** Venus.
- **Type** succedent, a house that steadies what the angular house starts.
- **Key words** money, possessions, resources, values, self worth, the senses.

## What the 2nd house covers

After the first house sets out who you are, the second house asks what you have and what you value. Astrologers read it as your own resources: money you earn, things you own, skills you can rely on and your sense of your own worth.

The second house is about more than a bank balance. It covers what makes you feel secure, what you will spend time and effort on, and how you enjoy the physical world: food, comfort, nature and touch. Its link with Taurus gives it a steady, earthy tone.

## The sign on the 2nd house

The sign on the second house cusp shows your style with resources:

- **Fire signs** earn and spend with enthusiasm, often on experiences.
- **Earth signs** save, build and value quality and lasting things.
- **Air signs** earn through ideas, words and people, and value knowledge.
- **Water signs** value what has emotional meaning, such as family items or keepsakes.

The ruler of that sign, wherever it sits in the chart, adds detail about where resources tend to come from.

## Planets in the 2nd house

- **Sun** pride in what you build. Self worth grows from earning and making.
- **Moon** feeling safe depends on comfort and a full cupboard.
- **Mercury** earning through talk, trade, writing or teaching.
- **Venus** a love of beautiful things and a taste for comfort.
- **Mars** energy for earning, and sometimes impulsive spending.
- **Jupiter** generosity and big plans with resources.
- **Saturn** care and caution with money, and slow, solid gains.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** unusual income, idealism about money or deep changes in resources.

These are traditional themes for reflection. The second house does not predict what you will earn.

## Self worth and values

Modern astrologers stress the inner side of this house. Self worth, they say, comes before net worth. A person who knows what they value and what they are good at tends to make wiser choices with time and money. The second house invites you to ask what you would keep if you could keep only a few things.

## The 2nd house in the sky

In the sky, the second house sits roughly below the eastern horizon, under the first. Planets there rise a few hours after the Ascendant. In daily life, astrologers link it with the steady, practical hours after a day begins.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Values** what matters most to you, beyond money?
- **Security** what makes you feel safe and settled?
- **Talents** which skills could you rely on in any job?
