## The 10th house in short

- **Natural sign** Capricorn.
- **Natural ruler** Saturn.
- **Type** angular, one of the four strongest houses.
- **Key words** career, calling, reputation, public life, achievement, authority.

## What the 10th house covers

The tenth house begins at the Midheaven, often written MC, from the Latin Medium Coeli, the middle of the sky. It is the highest point the zodiac reached overhead at the moment of birth. Astrologers read it as your public life: career, reputation, ambitions and the mark you want to leave on the world.

Where the fourth house at the bottom of the chart is private and rooted, the tenth at the top is public and visible. It covers how others see your achievements, your relationship with bosses and authority, and the long climb toward goals that take years.

## The sign on the 10th house

The sign on the Midheaven shows the style of your public role:

- **Fire signs** a career that leads, inspires or performs.
- **Earth signs** a career that builds, manages or produces real results.
- **Air signs** a career with ideas, people and communication.
- **Water signs** a career that cares, heals or creates.

The ruler of that sign, and the house it sits in, add where your calling may lead.

## Planets in the 10th house

- **Sun** a wish to be seen and to achieve. Natural leadership.
- **Moon** a public role that cares for people. Reputation matters emotionally.
- **Mercury** a career with words, ideas or trade.
- **Venus** charm in public and a link with art, design or diplomacy.
- **Mars** strong ambition and the drive to compete and lead.
- **Jupiter** a hopeful career path and a generous public image.
- **Saturn** at home here. A long, steady climb and lasting achievements.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** an unusual career, an inspired calling or a quest for real influence.

## A calling, not only a job

Modern astrologers read the tenth house as a sense of direction that may or may not match a job title. Raising a family, volunteering, art made in spare time or a role in a community can all be tenth house callings. The house invites you to ask what you want to be known for.

## The 10th house in the sky

The Midheaven is the point of the zodiac crossing the meridian, the line from south to north through the point straight overhead, at birth. If you were born at noon, the Sun sits near your Midheaven. Planets in the tenth house shone high in the sky at your birth.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Direction** what kind of work or contribution feels like your calling?
- **Reputation** what would you like people to say about your work?
- **Steps** what is the next small step toward a long term goal?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. The tenth house does not decide your career or success, and these notes are not career or money advice. Read more about [Capricorn](../../signs/capricorn/), [Saturn](../../planets/saturn/) and the [fourth house](../../houses/4/), its opposite.
