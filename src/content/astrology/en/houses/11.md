## The 11th house in short

- **Natural sign** Aquarius.
- **Natural rulers** Uranus, and Saturn in the older tradition.
- **Type** succedent.
- **Key words** friends, groups, communities, networks, causes, hopes, wishes.

## What the 11th house covers

The eleventh house is the house of friends and the future. Astrologers read it as the people you choose to spend time with outside family and romance: friends, clubs, teams, online communities and the wider networks you belong to. It also covers causes you care about and the hopes and wishes you hold for years to come.

Old texts called it the house of good spirit and linked it with allies and benefactors, the people who help us reach our goals. Where the tenth house shows what you achieve, the eleventh shows who you achieve it with and what you hope it will lead to.

## The sign on the 11th house

The sign on the eleventh house cusp shows your style of friendship:

- **Fire signs** lively friends, shared adventures and team spirit.
- **Earth signs** a few loyal friends and practical, useful groups.
- **Air signs** wide circles, clubs and friends met through ideas.
- **Water signs** close, caring friendships that feel like family.

The ruler of that sign adds where friendships tend to begin.

## Planets in the 11th house

- **Sun** identity shaped by friends and causes. A natural team player.
- **Moon** comfort among friends. Groups feel like home.
- **Mercury** lively group talk and a wide network.
- **Venus** popular and kind. Friends may turn into partners.
- **Mars** energy for teams, causes and shared goals.
- **Jupiter** generous friends and big hopes.
- **Saturn** few but loyal friends, and long term goals pursued with patience.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** unusual friends, idealistic causes or groups that change lives.

## Hopes and wishes

Astrologers often suggest a simple exercise for the eleventh house: write down three hopes for the next few years and the people who could help you reach them. The house invites you to think about the future you want, and about the communities that share it.

## The 11th house in the sky

In the Placidus system the eleventh house sits high in the eastern sky, between the Ascendant and the Midheaven. Planets there had risen a few hours before birth and were still climbing.

## When planets pass through

When planets travel through your eleventh house, astrologers read it as a social season. Venus or Jupiter passing through may bring new friends and invitations. Saturn passing through may thin out a circle until the loyal friends remain, and Uranus may bring friends from unexpected places.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Friends** which friends bring out your best?
- **Groups** which communities do you feel part of?
- **Hopes** what do you hope for in the next five years?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. The eleventh house is a way to reflect on friendship and hope. It does not predict who your friends will be. Read more about [Aquarius](../../signs/aquarius/), [Uranus](../../planets/uranus/) and the [fifth house](../../houses/5/), its opposite.
