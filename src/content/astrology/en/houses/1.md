## The 1st house in short

- **Natural sign** Aries.
- **Natural ruler** Mars.
- **Type** angular, one of the four strongest houses.
- **Key words** self, body, appearance, personality, new beginnings.

## What the 1st house covers

The first house begins at the Ascendant, the point of the zodiac rising in the east at the moment of birth. Astrologers read it as you in the most direct sense: your body, your looks, your energy and the way you meet the world. It is the front door of the chart.

Where the Sun shows who you are at the core, the first house shows how that core comes across. It covers first impressions, personal style, the way you start things and how others see you before they know you well. Many astrologers also read it as your overall approach to life.

## The sign on the 1st house

The sign on the cusp of the first house is your [rising sign](../../basics/rising-sign/). It sets the tone of the whole house. With a fire sign there, the person tends to come across as lively and bold. With an earth sign, as calm and solid. With an air sign, as friendly and talkative. With a water sign, as gentle and perceptive.

The planet that rules that sign is called the chart ruler. Its sign and house tell you where the energy of the self tends to flow.

## Planets in the 1st house

A planet in the first house is easy for others to see. It colours the personality strongly:

- **Sun** a strong, warm presence. The person likes to lead and be noticed.
- **Moon** feelings show on the face. The person is sensitive and responsive.
- **Mercury** quick, talkative, curious about everything.
- **Venus** charm, grace and an eye for style.
- **Mars** energy, drive and a direct manner.
- **Jupiter** warmth, optimism and a generous, big hearted style.
- **Saturn** a serious, reliable manner that softens with age.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** an original, dreamy or intense air.

An empty first house is common and means nothing is missing. The rising sign and the chart ruler still describe it fully.

## Placidus and the 1st house

In the Placidus system used by Sky Altar, the house sizes depend on your birth place and time. Near the equator the houses are fairly even. Far north or south some houses become very wide and others very narrow. The first house always starts at the Ascendant, whatever the system.

## The 1st house in the sky

The first house is the slice of sky just below the eastern horizon, the part about to rise. Planets there were climbing into view in the hours after your birth. In the Sky Altar app, face east to see which stars are rising now.

## Questions to reflect on

- **First impressions** how do people describe you when they first meet you?
- **Starting** how do you begin new things: boldly, slowly, with a plan, with a feeling?
- **Body** what helps you feel at home in yourself?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition for reflection, not a science. The first house says nothing about health or fate. Read more about [Aries](../../signs/aries/), [Mars](../../planets/mars/) and the [rising sign](../../basics/rising-sign/).
