## The 4th house in short

- **Natural sign** Cancer.
- **Natural ruler** the Moon.
- **Type** angular, one of the four strongest houses.
- **Key words** home, family, roots, ancestry, private life, the end of things.

## What the 4th house covers

The fourth house is linked with the lowest point of the chart, called the IC, from the Latin Imum Coeli, the bottom of the sky. At the moment of birth this point lay directly beneath your feet, on the far side of the Earth. Astrologers read it as your foundations: home, family, childhood and the private self that few people see.

The fourth house covers the place you live and the people you grew up with. It covers family history, the land or culture you come from and the feeling of belonging. Older texts also link it with the last chapter of life, the way a day ends at home.

## The sign on the 4th house

The sign of the fourth house shows what home means to you:

- **Fire signs** a lively home, full of activity, guests or creative projects.
- **Earth signs** a solid, comfortable home, often with a garden or good food.
- **Air signs** a social home, full of books, talk and friends dropping by.
- **Water signs** a cosy, emotional home that feels like a shell.

The ruler of that sign shows where you look for a sense of home outside the house.

## Planets in the 4th house

- **Sun** pride in family and home. A strong private life.
- **Moon** at home here. Deep ties to family and a love of nesting.
- **Mercury** a home full of talk and books, or working from home.
- **Venus** a beautiful, peaceful home. Warm family ties.
- **Mars** an active home, sometimes with lively family debates.
- **Jupiter** a big or generous home, open to guests.
- **Saturn** a sense of duty to family. A home built slowly and with care.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** frequent moves, an idealised home or deep family stories.

## Home and family stories

Many people use the fourth house to reflect on where they come from: the customs, recipes and stories passed down, as well as the patterns they choose to keep or change. Astrologers suggest that understanding your roots can help you build the home you want now.

## The 4th house in the sky

The fourth house lies below the horizon, around the point of the zodiac directly underfoot. Planets there were hidden on the far side of the Earth at birth. If you were born at midnight, the Sun sits near the IC.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Home** what makes a place feel like home to you?
- **Family** which family traditions do you treasure?
- **Rest** where do you go to feel safe and recharge?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. The fourth house is a way to think about home and family, not a statement about your parents or your future. Read more about [Cancer](../../signs/cancer/), the [Moon](../../planets/moon/) and the [tenth house](../../houses/10/), its opposite.
