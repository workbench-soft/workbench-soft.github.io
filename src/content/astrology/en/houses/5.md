## The 5th house in short

- **Natural sign** Leo.
- **Natural ruler** the Sun.
- **Type** succedent.
- **Key words** creativity, romance, play, hobbies, children, joy, self expression.

## What the 5th house covers

The fifth house is the house of joy. Astrologers read it as the things you do for the love of it: art, music, games, sport, dating, hobbies and parties. It covers the inner child and the urge to create something that carries your own stamp.

Old texts called it the house of good fortune. It covers romance in the early, playful sense: flirting, dates and falling in love, as opposed to the long term partnership of the seventh house. Traditionally it also covers children and the joy of raising them, along with teaching and anything that helps young people grow.

## The sign on the 5th house

The sign on the fifth house cusp shows how you like to have fun:

- **Fire signs** bold play: sport, performance, adventure and big romance.
- **Earth signs** fun you can touch: cooking, gardening, crafts, building things.
- **Air signs** fun with people and ideas: games, writing, clubs and long talks.
- **Water signs** fun with feeling: music, film, poetry and close, tender romance.

The ruler of that sign adds where your creative spark tends to land.

## Planets in the 5th house

- **Sun** at home here. A natural performer with a big creative drive.
- **Moon** joy in caring, play and creative comfort.
- **Mercury** fun with words, puzzles, games and wit.
- **Venus** a love of beauty and romance, and an eye for art.
- **Mars** competitive fun, sport and passionate romance.
- **Jupiter** generous fun and a big heart for children and play.
- **Saturn** serious about creative work. Play that becomes a craft over time.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** unusual hobbies, dreamy art or intense romance.

## A creative side in each chart

Astrologers stress that every chart has a fifth house, so every person has a creative side. It does not have to be fine art. A well cooked meal, a good joke, a garden or a model railway all count. The fifth house asks where you feel most like yourself while making or playing.

## The 5th house in the sky

In the sky, the fifth house sits roughly below the western horizon, between the lowest point of the chart and the sixth house. Planets there had set several hours before birth. Leo's link with the Sun gives this house a warm, sunny tone.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Joy** what do you do that makes you lose track of time?
- **Play** when did you last play just for fun?
- **Creation** what would you like to make this year?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. The fifth house is a way to reflect on joy and creativity. It makes no promise about love or children. Read more about [Leo](../../signs/leo/), the [Sun](../../planets/sun/) and the [eleventh house](../../houses/11/), its opposite.
