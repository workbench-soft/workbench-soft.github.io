## The 8th house in short

- **Natural sign** Scorpio.
- **Natural rulers** Pluto, and Mars in the older tradition.
- **Type** succedent.
- **Key words** shared money, debts, inheritance, intimacy, trust, transformation, mystery.

## What the 8th house covers

The eighth house follows the seventh house of partners. It asks what happens when two lives merge. Astrologers read it as all that is shared: joint money, loans, taxes, inheritances and the resources of a partner. It also covers emotional and physical intimacy, the deep trust that close bonds need.

The house has a reputation for being mysterious. Old texts linked it with endings of all kinds. Modern astrologers read it as transformation: the times in life when something old ends and something new begins, and the inner strength those times reveal.

## The sign on the 8th house

The sign on the eighth house cusp shows how you handle closeness and change:

- **Fire signs** face change head on and share generously.
- **Earth signs** want clear, fair agreements and move through change slowly.
- **Air signs** talk through trust and like to understand change.
- **Water signs** feel shared life deeply and need strong trust.

The ruler of that sign adds where shared matters tend to come up.

## Planets in the 8th house

- **Sun** a deep, private nature drawn to life's big questions.
- **Moon** strong emotional bonds and a need for real trust.
- **Mercury** a mind for research, psychology and mysteries.
- **Venus** deep loyalty in love and shared pleasures.
- **Mars** intense passions and a strong will in times of change.
- **Jupiter** generosity in shared life and an open mind about deep topics.
- **Saturn** care and caution with shared money and with trust.
- **Uranus, Neptune, Pluto** sudden changes, spiritual depth or great inner strength.

## A house of renewal

The eighth house can sound heavy, but astrologers often describe it as the house of rebirth. Its sign, Scorpio, is linked with the phoenix as well as the scorpion. Hard chapters can lead to growth. The house invites you to think about what you hold on to, what you share and what you are ready to let go.

## The 8th house in the sky

In the Placidus system the eighth house lies above the western horizon, just past the Descendant. Planets there stood low in the western sky at birth and set within a couple of hours. It is part of the visible sky.

## When planets pass through

When planets travel through your eighth house, astrologers read it as a time to look at what you share and what you are ready to change. The Moon crosses it each month, a small reminder to check in with close bonds. Slow planets passing through mark longer chapters of inner change.

## Questions to reflect on

- **Trust** what helps you trust someone deeply?
- **Sharing** how do you feel about sharing money or space?
- **Change** what did a past ending teach you?

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. The eighth house does not predict loss, death or inheritance, and these notes are not financial advice. Read more about [Scorpio](../../signs/scorpio/), [Pluto](../../planets/pluto/) and the [second house](../../houses/2/), its opposite.
