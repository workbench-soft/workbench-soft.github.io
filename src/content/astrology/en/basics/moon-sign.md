## The inner side of the chart

If the Sun is the self you show by choice, the Moon is the self that reacts before you think. Astrologers read the Moon sign as your feelings, moods, habits and what makes you feel safe. It shows how you comfort yourself, what you need from a home and how you care for others.

The Moon moves fast. It crosses the whole zodiac in about 27 days and spends around two and a half days in each sign. So to know your Moon sign for sure, you need the date and, when the Moon changed sign that day, the time of birth.

## What the Moon stands for

- **Emotions** how you feel and how quickly feelings rise and fall.
- **Needs** what you need to feel calm, fed and at home.
- **Memory and roots** family, childhood and the habits you learned early.
- **Care** the way you nurture others and like to be nurtured.

## The Moon in each sign

- **Aries Moon** quick feelings, quickly over. Needs action and honesty.
- **Taurus Moon** steady and calm. Finds comfort in food, nature and routine.
- **Gemini Moon** talks feelings through. Needs variety and good conversation.
- **Cancer Moon** deep, caring and home loving. Feels at home in its own sign.
- **Leo Moon** warm and open. Needs to feel appreciated and to play.
- **Virgo Moon** feels better when life is in order. Shows care by helping.
- **Libra Moon** needs harmony and company. Dislikes conflict.
- **Scorpio Moon** intense and private. Needs trust before it opens up.
- **Sagittarius Moon** needs freedom, humour and a sense of meaning.
- **Capricorn Moon** reserved and responsible. Feels safe when in control.
- **Aquarius Moon** independent and friendly. Processes feelings through ideas.
- **Pisces Moon** sensitive and dreamy. Needs quiet time and creativity.

## Sun and Moon together

The pair of Sun and Moon is a big part of any chart reading. When both sit in signs of the same element, such as a Leo Sun with a Sagittarius Moon, the person may feel quite whole and direct. When they sit in signs that clash, such as a Taurus Sun with an Aquarius Moon, the head and the heart may want different things. Astrologers see that tension as a source of growth rather than a problem.

The Moon's phase at birth also adds colour. People born near a new Moon are said to be more instinctive and forward looking. People born near a full Moon are said to be more aware of relationships and balance.

## The house of the Moon

The house where your Moon falls shows where you most look for comfort. A Moon in the fourth house feels at home in family life. A Moon in the tenth house may feel things through career and public life. A Moon in the eleventh house finds comfort with friends and groups.

## The Moon in the real sky

The Moon is the fastest moving body in the sky and the easiest to follow. Over one evening you can watch it drift against the stars by about its own width every hour. The Today page of this guide shows which sign the Moon is in today, and the Sky Altar app shows it over your camera, against the real constellations.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. A Moon sign is a way to think about your feelings and needs, not a diagnosis. If your mood troubles you, talk to a friend or a professional.
