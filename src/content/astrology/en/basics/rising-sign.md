## The sign on the horizon

The rising sign, also called the Ascendant, is the sign of the zodiac that was rising over the eastern horizon at the moment you were born. As the Earth turns, the whole zodiac passes over the horizon once a day. A new sign rises roughly every two hours, though some rise faster and others slower depending on where you are.

That is why the rising sign needs an exact birth time. Being born an hour earlier or later can change it. Astrologers often say the rising sign is the most personal part of a chart, because it depends on the moment and the place as well as the day.

## What the rising sign stands for

- **First impressions** how strangers see you before they know you.
- **Style** the way you dress, move and meet new situations.
- **Approach** your instinctive way of starting things.
- **The doorway** the mask or front door through which the rest of the chart is met.

Some astrologers compare the Sun to the person, the Moon to the inner life and the rising sign to the clothes and the walk.

## The rising sign and the houses

The Ascendant marks the start of the first house. From there the twelve houses go round the chart. So the rising sign decides which signs rule which areas of life. With Aries rising, Libra sits on the seventh house of partners. With Cancer rising, Capricorn sits on the seventh house. That gives each rising sign its own pattern.

The planet that rules the rising sign is called the chart ruler. With Leo rising, the Sun is the chart ruler. With Gemini rising, Mercury is. Astrologers look closely at the sign and house of the chart ruler.

## Each rising sign in brief

- **Aries rising** direct, energetic, quick to start.
- **Taurus rising** calm, pleasant, steady.
- **Gemini rising** chatty, curious, youthful.
- **Cancer rising** gentle, caring, a little guarded at first.
- **Leo rising** warm, striking, confident.
- **Virgo rising** neat, modest, observant.
- **Libra rising** charming, polite, graceful.
- **Scorpio rising** magnetic, private, intense.
- **Sagittarius rising** friendly, open, ready for adventure.
- **Capricorn rising** serious, polished, reliable.
- **Aquarius rising** unusual, friendly, a little detached.
- **Pisces rising** soft, dreamy, kind.

## When you do not know your birth time

Many people do not know their exact birth time. A birth certificate, a hospital record or a family member may help. Without it, astrologers often draw a chart for noon and leave out the houses and the rising sign. Some try to work the time backwards from life events, a practice called rectification.

## Rising in the real sky

The rising sign is a real place in the sky. Look east at any hour and part of the zodiac is coming up over the horizon. In the Sky Altar app you can turn toward the east and see which constellations are rising right now. The sign on the horizon at your birth was doing the same thing then.
