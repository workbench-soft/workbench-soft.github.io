## The sign most people know

When someone asks your star sign, they mean your Sun sign. It is the sign of the zodiac the Sun stood in on the day you were born. Newspaper horoscopes, mugs and birthday cards all use it, because you only need a birth date to find it.

In the tropical zodiac the Sun enters Aries at the March equinox and moves through one sign roughly each month. That is why each sign has a familiar date range, such as Leo from about July 23 to August 22.

## What the Sun stands for

In a chart, the Sun is the centre, just as it is in the solar system. Astrologers read it as:

- **Identity** the sense of who you are and who you are becoming.
- **Vitality** what gives you energy and makes you feel alive.
- **Purpose** the goals and values that feel most like your own.
- **The conscious self** the side of you that you show by choice.

The sign colours how that core expresses itself. A Sun in Aries wants to lead and begin. A Sun in Cancer wants to care and protect. A Sun in Aquarius wants to be free and to change things for the better.

## Why the dates shift a little

The Sun does not change sign at the same minute each year. Our calendar has 365 days, the solar year about 365 and a quarter, and leap years catch up the difference. As a result the change of sign can fall on the 19th, 20th, 21st or 22nd of a month depending on the year and your time zone.

If you were born on the edge between two signs, people sometimes say you were born on the cusp. In a tropical chart the Sun is always in one sign or the other. A chart drawn for your exact birth time and place settles which one.

## The Sun in the house system

The house where the Sun falls shows where you most want to shine. A Sun in the tenth house often looks for a public role or career. A Sun in the fourth house shines at home and in family life. A Sun in the seventh house comes alive through partners. You need a birth time to know the house.

## More than one sign

Your Sun sign is only one of many parts of a chart. That is why two people with the same Sun sign can be very different. The [Moon sign](../../basics/moon-sign/) shows your feelings and needs. The [rising sign](../../basics/rising-sign/) shows your outward style. Mercury, Venus and Mars add how you think, love and act.

Astrologers often describe the Sun as the light of the chart and the other planets as the colours it passes through. A Leo Sun with a Virgo Moon and a Capricorn rising feels very different from a Leo with a Sagittarius Moon and Leo rising.

## The real Sun in the sky

The Sun's yearly path across the stars is called the ecliptic. The zodiac signs are equal 30 degree slices of that path, measured from the March equinox. The constellations behind them vary in size, and over two thousand years the slow wobble of Earth's axis has shifted the signs away from their namesake stars by almost a whole sign.

The Sky Altar app shows the Sun's place on the ecliptic over your camera, so you can see which stars it hides today.

## A gentle reminder

Astrology is a tradition, not a science, and a Sun sign is a story rather than a rule. Use it as a starting point for reflection about what lights you up, not as a label that limits you.
