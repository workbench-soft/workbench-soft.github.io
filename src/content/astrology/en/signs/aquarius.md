## Aquarius at a glance

- **Dates** about January 20 to February 18, the eleventh 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** air, shared with Gemini and Libra.
- **Modality** fixed, the signs in the middle of each season.
- **Ruling planets** Uranus in modern astrology, Saturn in the older tradition.
- **Symbol** the Water Bearer, drawn as two waves: ♒.
- **Opposite sign** Leo.

## The character of Aquarius

Aquarius falls in the depth of the northern winter, when people gather indoors and think about the year ahead. Astrologers read the sign as the mind of the group: friendship, ideas, progress and the future. Despite its name, Aquarius is an air sign. The water it pours is often read as knowledge shared with all people.

People with strong Aquarius placements are often described as original, friendly and independent. They care about fairness and about society as a whole. They like new tech, odd hobbies and people who think differently. They can be warm to a crowd and still keep a little distance in private.

## Strengths

- **Originality** Aquarius sees a new way to do an old thing.
- **Friendship** the sign is loyal to friends and open to all kinds of people.
- **Fairness** it speaks up for those left out.
- **Clear thinking** it can stay calm and logical when emotions run high.

## Challenges

The cool head that helps in a crisis can feel distant in a close relationship. An Aquarius may explain feelings rather than share them. Its fixed side can also make it stubborn about its ideas, even while it calls itself open minded.

Astrologers advise Aquarius to bring its care for humanity home to the people right next to it. A warm word to one friend can matter as much as a grand plan for the world.

## Aquarius in love and friendship

For Aquarius, love often grows out of friendship. It wants a partner who is a true companion, respects its freedom and shares its interests or causes. Possessive behaviour and rigid rules push it away. Surprise, honesty and shared ideas keep the bond alive.

Air signs Gemini and Libra share its love of talk and ideas. Fire signs Aries and Sagittarius bring energy and adventure. Its opposite, Leo, teaches it about the heart. The [Aquarius compatibility page](../../compatibility/aquarius/) looks at each match.

## Aquarius at work

Aquarius tends to thrive in work that looks to the future or serves a community. Science, tech, engineering, social causes, research, astrology itself and any team working on something new suit the classic profile. It likes freedom to try ideas and dislikes being micromanaged. It does well in groups where every voice counts.

## The myth and the stars

The Water Bearer is often linked with Ganymede, a young Trojan of great beauty. Zeus, in the form of an eagle, carried him to Mount Olympus to pour drinks for the gods. The nearby constellation Aquila, the Eagle, recalls that flight. Older Babylonian sky lore showed a god pouring streams of water from a jar, linked with the rainy season.

The constellation Aquarius is large and faint, with a small Y shape of stars called the Water Jar. It lies in the watery region of the sky along with Pisces, Capricornus and Cetus the Whale. Sky Altar shows the streams of water flowing from the jar toward the bright star Fomalhaut.

## Aquarius in your chart

An Aquarius Moon needs freedom and friendship and may handle feelings through ideas. An Aquarius rising often looks friendly, unusual and a little detached. The eleventh house of friends and groups shares the Water Bearer's themes.
