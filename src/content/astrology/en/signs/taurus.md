## Taurus at a glance

- **Dates** about April 20 to May 20, the second 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** earth, shared with Virgo and Capricorn.
- **Modality** fixed, the signs in the middle of each season.
- **Ruling planet** Venus, the planet of pleasure, beauty and value.
- **Symbol** the Bull, drawn as a circle with horns: ♉.
- **Opposite sign** Scorpio.

## The character of Taurus

Taurus arrives when spring is in full bloom in the northern hemisphere. The fields are green and the days are warm, and astrologers read the sign as the season's wish to settle, grow and enjoy. Where Aries starts things, Taurus makes them last.

People with strong Taurus placements are often described as calm, patient and down to earth. They like to know where they stand. They trust what they can touch, taste and test. Good food, soft fabrics, a garden, music and a well kept home all speak to the Venus side of the sign.

## Strengths

- **Patience** Taurus is happy to take the long road if it leads somewhere solid.
- **Loyalty** once the Bull commits to a friend, a partner or a job, it stays.
- **Practical sense** the sign tends to know what things cost and what they are worth.
- **Calm** a Taurus friend is a good person to sit with when life feels shaky.

## Challenges

The same steadiness that makes Taurus reliable can turn into stubbornness. Astrologers joke that you can lead a Bull, but you cannot push it. Change for its own sake feels pointless to Taurus, so it may hold on to a habit, a job or an opinion longer than it helps.

Comfort can also become a trap. The classic advice for Taurus is to keep a little room for new experiences. Now and then it helps to ask whether a routine still brings joy or only safety.

## Taurus in love and friendship

In love Taurus is affectionate, sensual and faithful. It shows care through actions: cooking a meal, fixing a shelf, remembering a favourite flower. It moves slowly at first and dislikes games or sudden drama. Once it feels safe, it offers deep warmth and stability.

Earth signs Virgo and Capricorn share its pace and its practical outlook. Water signs Cancer and Pisces bring the emotional depth that Taurus quietly enjoys. The [Taurus compatibility page](../../compatibility/taurus/) covers each pairing.

## Taurus at work

Taurus tends to do well in jobs that build lasting value or deal with the senses. Finance, farming, building, cooking, design, music and crafts suit the classic profile. The Bull works at a steady pace and finishes what it starts. It likes clear rules, fair pay and a pleasant place to work, and it dislikes being rushed.

## The myth and the stars

In Greek myth, Zeus took the form of a gentle white bull to win Europa, a princess of Phoenicia. She climbed onto his back, and he swam with her across the sea to Crete. The bull was later honoured in the stars.

The constellation Taurus is one of the easiest to find. Its red giant star Aldebaran marks the bull's eye, and the V shaped Hyades cluster forms its face. Nearby sits the Pleiades, the Seven Sisters, a small dipper of blue stars known to almost every culture. On a winter evening Sky Altar can show the Bull right above Orion.

## Taurus in your chart

A Taurus Moon is often described as one of the most content placements. It finds comfort in routine, food and familiar faces. A Taurus rising tends to look calm, solid and pleasant to strangers. Venus, the ruler, matters too: the sign and house of Venus in your chart show how the Taurus themes play out for you.
