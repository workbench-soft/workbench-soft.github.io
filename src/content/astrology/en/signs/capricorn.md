## Capricorn at a glance

- **Dates** about December 22 to January 19, from the December solstice through the next 30 degrees.
- **Element** earth, shared with Taurus and Virgo.
- **Modality** cardinal, the signs that start each season.
- **Ruling planet** Saturn, the planet of time, structure and effort.
- **Symbol** the Sea Goat, a goat with the tail of a fish: ♑.
- **Opposite sign** Cancer.

## The character of Capricorn

Capricorn begins at the December solstice, the shortest day in the northern hemisphere. From there the light slowly returns. Astrologers read the sign as patience in hard times: the goat that climbs a cold mountain one careful step at a time, and reaches the top.

People with strong Capricorn placements are often described as responsible, ambitious and dry humoured. They plan ahead, keep their word and take the long view. Many seem older than their years when young and lighter in spirit as they age, as if they grow into themselves.

## Strengths

- **Discipline** Capricorn does the work, even when it is dull.
- **Reliability** if a Capricorn says it will be there, it will.
- **Ambition** the sign sets real goals and climbs toward them.
- **Wisdom with time** it knows that good things take years to build.

## Challenges

Capricorn's sense of duty can harden into worry about status, money or what others think. It may work too much, rest too little and hide its feelings behind a calm face. Fear of failure can keep it from trying something new.

Astrologers remind Capricorn that the fish tail of its symbol belongs to the sea of feelings. Play, rest and asking for help are part of a well built life, not breaks from it.

## Capricorn in love and friendship

In love Capricorn is careful at first and deeply committed once it chooses. It shows love through loyalty, plans for the future and practical support. It respects a partner who has their own goals. Its humour, often very dry, comes out with people it trusts.

Earth signs Taurus and Virgo share its steady outlook. Water signs Scorpio and Pisces soften it and bring emotional depth. Its opposite, Cancer, teaches it about home and feeling. The [Capricorn compatibility page](../../compatibility/capricorn/) covers each pairing.

## Capricorn at work

Capricorn is often called the manager of the zodiac. Business, government, engineering, architecture, finance, law and any field with a clear ladder to climb suit the classic profile. It likes clear rules, real responsibility and rewards that match effort. It is often the person others turn to when a project needs a firm hand.

## The myth and the stars

The Sea Goat is very old. The Babylonians already saw a goat fish in these stars and linked it with Enki, the god of fresh water and wisdom. The Greeks told how the god Pan, fleeing the monster Typhon, jumped into a river and turned half into a fish. Zeus placed his strange shape among the stars.

The constellation Capricornus is faint and shaped like a wide triangle or a smile. It sits in a region of the sky the ancients called the Sea, near Aquarius and Pisces. The Tropic of Capricorn takes its name from the time when the Sun stood here at the December solstice.

## Capricorn in your chart

A Capricorn Moon may hide its feelings and find comfort in being useful and in control. A Capricorn rising often looks serious, polished and older than its age.
