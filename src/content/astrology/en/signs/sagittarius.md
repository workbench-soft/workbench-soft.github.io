## Sagittarius at a glance

- **Dates** about November 22 to December 21, the ninth 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** fire, shared with Aries and Leo.
- **Modality** mutable, the signs that close each season.
- **Ruling planet** Jupiter, the planet of growth, faith and good fortune.
- **Symbol** the Archer, drawn as an arrow pointing up and away: ♐.
- **Opposite sign** Gemini.

## The character of Sagittarius

Sagittarius closes the northern autumn and leads up to the longest night of the year. Astrologers read the sign as a flame that keeps the spirit warm: hope, adventure and the search for meaning. The Archer aims its arrow far beyond the horizon and then goes to see where it landed.

People with strong Sagittarius placements are often described as cheerful, honest and restless. They love travel, big ideas, good stories and open roads. They ask why, and they want an answer that makes sense of the whole picture.

## Strengths

- **Optimism** Sagittarius finds the bright side and helps others see it.
- **Honesty** the sign says what it believes, often with a laugh.
- **Curiosity** foreign places, languages, faiths and philosophies draw it in.
- **Generosity** it shares what it has, from a meal to a lucky break.

## Challenges

Freedom matters so much to Sagittarius that commitment can feel like a fence. It may promise more than it can deliver, or leave before the boring part of a project begins. Its frankness can sting when tact would serve better.

Astrologers advise the Archer to aim with care as well as joy. A plan, a budget and a kind word go a long way toward turning big dreams into real journeys.

## Sagittarius in love and friendship

In love Sagittarius is fun, warm and adventurous. It wants a partner who is also a friend and a travel companion, someone who laughs easily and gives it room to roam. Jealousy and clinging push it away, while shared plans and honesty keep it close.

Fire signs Aries and Leo share its spark. Air signs Libra and Aquarius bring ideas and good company. Its opposite, Gemini, matches its curiosity. The [Sagittarius compatibility page](../../compatibility/sagittarius/) explores each match.

## Sagittarius at work

Sagittarius tends to do well in work that teaches, travels or inspires. Teaching, publishing, tourism, law, outdoor work, sport, religion and international business suit the classic profile. It likes freedom in how it reaches a goal. Tight rules and long hours at the same desk drain its fire.

## The myth and the stars

The Archer is often linked with Chiron, the wisest of the centaurs. Unlike his wild cousins, Chiron was a healer and a teacher of heroes such as Achilles and Jason. When he was wounded by a poisoned arrow that would not let him die, he gave up his immortality, and Zeus placed him among the stars.

The constellation Sagittarius points straight toward the centre of our galaxy. Its brightest stars form a shape that many people call the Teapot, with the Milky Way rising like steam from its spout. On a dark night the star clouds there are among the richest in the sky. Sky Altar shows the Archer aiming along that glowing band.

## Sagittarius in your chart

A Sagittarius Moon needs space, humour and a sense of purpose to feel at ease. A Sagittarius rising often looks friendly, sporty and ready to go. The ninth house of travel and learning carries the Archer's flavour in every chart.
