## Cancer at a glance

- **Dates** about June 21 to July 22, from the June solstice through the next 30 degrees.
- **Element** water, shared with Scorpio and Pisces.
- **Modality** cardinal, the signs that start each season.
- **Ruling planet** the Moon, the light of feelings and memory.
- **Symbol** the Crab, drawn as two curls: ♋.
- **Opposite sign** Capricorn.

## The character of Cancer

Cancer begins at the June solstice, the longest day in the northern hemisphere. From that point the days start to shorten. Astrologers read the sign as a turn inward: toward home, family and the people we hold close. Its ruler is the Moon, which waxes and wanes, so Cancer is linked with moods that come and go like tides.

People with strong Cancer placements are often described as caring, protective and sensitive. Like the crab, they may have a hard shell and a soft inside. They remember birthdays, keep old letters and know how to make a guest feel at home.

## Strengths

- **Care** Cancer notices when someone is upset and knows how to comfort them.
- **Memory** the sign holds on to stories, recipes and family history.
- **Loyalty** it protects its people fiercely.
- **Intuition** Cancer reads the mood of a room before anyone speaks.

## Challenges

The crab's shell can close too quickly. When Cancer feels hurt, it may withdraw, sulk or hint instead of saying what it needs. Old wounds can linger for a long time because the sign remembers so well.

Cancer also tends to look after others first and itself last. Astrologers advise the sign to treat its own needs with the same tenderness it gives others. Speaking up plainly works better than waiting to be understood.

## Cancer in love and friendship

In love Cancer is devoted, romantic and nurturing. It wants a partner who feels like home. It moves sideways at first, testing the water, but once it trusts someone it gives a great deal. Small gestures matter: a call when you said you would, a meal shared, a remembered detail.

Water signs Scorpio and Pisces understand its emotional depth. Earth signs Taurus and Virgo give it the safety it craves. The [Cancer compatibility page](../../compatibility/cancer/) covers each pairing.

## Cancer at work

Cancer tends to thrive in work that cares for people or keeps traditions alive. Teaching, nursing, social work, hospitality, cooking, history, property and running a family business all fit the classic profile. It works best in a team that feels a bit like family, where loyalty is valued.

## The myth and the stars

In Greek myth, the goddess Hera sent a giant crab to distract Hercules while he fought the many headed Hydra. The crab pinched his foot, and the hero crushed it. Hera, grateful for its effort, placed the crab among the stars.

The constellation Cancer is the faintest of the zodiac. Its best treasure is the Beehive Cluster, a soft patch of light in its centre. Ancient sky watchers used it to forecast the weather: if they could not see it, rain was coming. Long ago the Sun stood in Cancer at the June solstice, which is why the northern line of the tropics is still called the Tropic of Cancer.

## Cancer in your chart

The Moon rules Cancer, so a Cancer Moon sits in its home sign. Feelings run deep and family matters a lot. A Cancer rising often looks gentle, kind and a little reserved at first meeting.
