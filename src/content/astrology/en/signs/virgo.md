## Virgo at a glance

- **Dates** about August 23 to September 22, the sixth 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** earth, shared with Taurus and Capricorn.
- **Modality** mutable, the signs that close each season.
- **Ruling planet** Mercury, the planet of thought, detail and skill.
- **Symbol** the Maiden, often holding a sheaf of wheat: ♍.
- **Opposite sign** Pisces.

## The character of Virgo

Virgo closes the northern summer at harvest time. The grain is cut, sorted and stored, and astrologers read the sign in the same light: it sorts, refines and puts things in good order. Mercury rules Virgo as well as Gemini, but here the quick mind turns toward craft and usefulness.

People with strong Virgo placements are often described as modest, helpful and precise. They notice the typo, the loose thread and the better way to stack the dishwasher. Their care is practical. They show love by fixing, tidying and showing up.

## Strengths

- **Attention to detail** Virgo spots what others miss.
- **Service** the sign likes to be useful and often helps without being asked.
- **Skill** patient practice turns a Virgo into a true craftsperson.
- **Clear thinking** it can break a big problem into small steps.

## Challenges

The eye for detail can turn on itself. A Virgo may worry, overthink or judge its own work too harshly. Perfectionism can delay a project that is already good enough. Criticism meant to help may land as nagging if it comes without warmth.

Astrologers advise Virgo to give itself the same patience it gives a task. A little mess, a day off and an imperfect first draft are part of a healthy life.

## Virgo in love and friendship

In love Virgo is shy at first, loyal and thoughtful. It shows care through small, useful acts: making soup when you are ill, sorting out your train times, remembering what you said last week. It values honesty and good manners and takes a while to open up.

Earth signs Taurus and Capricorn share its practical outlook. Water signs Cancer and Scorpio bring the depth that helps Virgo relax. Its opposite, Pisces, teaches it to let go. The [Virgo compatibility page](../../compatibility/virgo/) covers each pairing.

## Virgo at work

Virgo shines in work that needs care, method and skill. Editing, accounting, research, data, crafts, gardening, nursing and any job where quality matters suit the classic profile. It is the colleague who reads the instructions and finds the bug. Clear standards and a calm place to work bring out its best.

## The myth and the stars

The Maiden has many names. The Greeks linked her with Astraea, the star goddess of justice who was the last immortal to leave the earth when people grew cruel. Others saw her as Demeter or her daughter Persephone, goddesses of grain and the harvest. That is why Virgo often holds an ear of wheat.

The constellation Virgo is the largest in the zodiac and the second largest in the sky. Its brightest star, Spica, means ear of grain and shines blue white in the evenings of the northern spring. Sky Altar draws the Maiden across those wide, faint stars in each culture's own style.

## Virgo in your chart

A Virgo Moon feels calm when life is in order and finds comfort in useful routines. A Virgo rising often looks neat, composed and a little reserved.
