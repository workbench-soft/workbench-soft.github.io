## Pisces at a glance

- **Dates** about February 19 to March 20, the last 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** water, shared with Cancer and Scorpio.
- **Modality** mutable, the signs that close each season.
- **Ruling planets** Neptune in modern astrology, Jupiter in the older tradition.
- **Symbol** two Fishes swimming in opposite directions, joined by a cord: ♓.
- **Opposite sign** Virgo.

## The character of Pisces

Pisces closes the zodiac year, just before the spring equinox. Winter melts into spring, and the boundaries between seasons blur. Astrologers read the last sign as the place where everything flows together: dreams, imagination, compassion and the sense that we are all connected.

People with strong Pisces placements are often described as gentle, imaginative and kind. They feel other people's moods as if they were their own. Music, art, film, water and quiet places speak to them. Many have a rich inner world and a strong sense of something larger than themselves.

## Strengths

- **Compassion** Pisces understands pain and offers comfort without judging.
- **Imagination** the sign dreams in colour and often has artistic gifts.
- **Intuition** it picks up on feelings and hints others miss.
- **Adaptability** like water, it finds a way around obstacles.

## Challenges

The open heart of Pisces can absorb too much. It may take on other people's problems, struggle to say no or drift away from hard facts into daydreams. Clear limits and practical plans do not come easily to the Fishes.

Astrologers advise Pisces to protect its kindness with gentle boundaries. Time alone, creative work and a friend who helps with the practical side can keep the Fishes swimming in the right direction.

## Pisces in love and friendship

In love Pisces is romantic, devoted and tender. It dreams of a soulmate and gives its whole heart. It notices how a partner feels and wants to make life softer for them. It needs a partner who is kind, patient and willing to share feelings openly.

Water signs Cancer and Scorpio meet its emotional depth. Earth signs Taurus and Capricorn give it a safe harbour. Its opposite, Virgo, can bring order to its dreams. The [Pisces compatibility page](../../compatibility/pisces/) covers each pairing.

## Pisces at work

Pisces tends to thrive in work that heals, inspires or creates. Music, art, film, photography, poetry, nursing, charity work, therapy and spiritual work suit the classic profile. It works best where kindness and imagination are valued. Harsh competition and cold numbers wear it out, while a creative team brings out its talent.

## The myth and the stars

In one Greek myth, Aphrodite and her son Eros fled the monster Typhon by turning into fish and diving into a river. They tied themselves together with a cord so they would not lose each other in the water. The two fish joined by a cord became the constellation Pisces.

The constellation Pisces is faint and wide, shaped like a large V with a small ring of stars called the Circlet at one end. Today the Sun stands in Pisces at the March equinox, a result of the slow wobble of Earth's axis. That is why people speak of the end of the Age of Pisces and the coming Age of Aquarius. Sky Altar shows the two Fishes swimming apart, held by their cord.

## Pisces in your chart

A Pisces Moon feels everything deeply and needs quiet time to recharge. A Pisces rising often looks soft, dreamy and kind. The twelfth house of rest and the unseen shares the Fishes' themes.
