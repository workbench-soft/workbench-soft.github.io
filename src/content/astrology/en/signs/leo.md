## Leo at a glance

- **Dates** about July 23 to August 22, the fifth 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** fire, shared with Aries and Sagittarius.
- **Modality** fixed, the signs in the middle of each season.
- **Ruling planet** the Sun, the centre of the solar system and of the chart.
- **Symbol** the Lion, drawn as a curling mane or tail: ♌.
- **Opposite sign** Aquarius.

## The character of Leo

Leo rules the height of the northern summer, when the Sun feels strongest and the days are long and golden. Astrologers give the sign the Sun itself as its ruler. Its theme is the heart: warmth, pride, play and the wish to shine.

People with strong Leo placements are often described as generous, confident and dramatic in the best sense. They light up a room, remember to celebrate and make others feel special. Under the bright mane there is often a soft wish to be loved and appreciated.

## Strengths

- **Warmth** Leo gives praise freely and makes friends feel seen.
- **Creativity** acting, art, music and play come naturally to the sign.
- **Courage** the Lion stands up for its people and its ideals.
- **Leadership** others often follow Leo's lead because it believes in them.

## Challenges

The Leo shadow is pride. A Lion that feels ignored can sulk, show off or take criticism too personally. The sign may also struggle to admit a mistake in public, since its self image matters so much.

The traditional advice for Leo is simple. Let the light shine on others too, and remember that the people who love you do not need a performance. Real confidence can share the stage.

## Leo in love and friendship

In love Leo is romantic, loyal and generous. It likes grand gestures, surprise dates and open affection. It wants a partner who admires it and whom it can be proud of. Loyalty runs deep: once a Lion gives its heart, it protects that bond.

Fire signs Aries and Sagittarius share its zest. Air signs Gemini and Libra keep it amused and feed its social side. The opposite sign, Aquarius, teaches it about the group as well as the self. The [Leo compatibility page](../../compatibility/leo/) explores each match.

## Leo at work

Leo tends to do well where it can create, perform or lead. Theatre, film, design, teaching, event planning, management and anything with an audience suit the classic profile. It works hard for praise and for a cause it believes in. A dull back room with no recognition is the hardest place for a Lion.

## The myth and the stars

The first labour of Hercules was to defeat the Nemean lion, a beast whose golden hide no weapon could pierce. Hercules wrestled it with his bare hands and wore its skin as armour ever after. Zeus placed the lion in the sky to honour the fight.

The constellation Leo is one of the few that truly looks like its name. A backward question mark of stars, called the Sickle, forms the lion's head and mane. Its brightest star is Regulus, the little king, which sits almost exactly on the path of the Sun. Sky Altar draws the Lion as a striding golden figure across those stars.

## Leo in your chart

A Leo Moon needs warmth and appreciation to feel safe, and it shows its feelings openly. A Leo rising tends to make a striking first impression, often with good hair and a big smile, as the old books like to say.
