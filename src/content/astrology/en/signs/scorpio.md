## Scorpio at a glance

- **Dates** about October 23 to November 21, the eighth 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** water, shared with Cancer and Pisces.
- **Modality** fixed, the signs in the middle of each season.
- **Ruling planets** Pluto in modern astrology, Mars in the older tradition.
- **Symbol** the Scorpion, drawn as an M with a raised stinger: ♏.
- **Opposite sign** Taurus.

## The character of Scorpio

Scorpio falls in the heart of the northern autumn, when leaves drop and the year turns toward its dark half. Many cultures hold festivals of the dead at this time. Astrologers read Scorpio as the sign of depth, change and renewal: what ends, what stays hidden and what grows back stronger.

People with strong Scorpio placements are often described as intense, private and loyal. They feel things fully and rarely do anything halfway. They prefer one honest friend to a crowd of acquaintances. Their gaze is famous in the old books, steady and hard to read.

## Strengths

- **Depth** Scorpio looks under the surface of people and problems.
- **Loyalty** once you have its trust, it stands by you.
- **Willpower** the sign can see a hard task through to the end.
- **Courage with feelings** it does not run from grief, anger or truth.

## Challenges

Strong feelings can turn into jealousy or a long memory for hurts. Scorpio may test people before it trusts them, or keep secrets out of habit. When it feels betrayed, it can cut someone off completely.

Astrologers suggest that Scorpio's greatest power is change. The sign is linked with the phoenix as well as the scorpion. Letting go of an old grudge or an old role often frees a Scorpio to rise in a new form.

## Scorpio in love and friendship

In love Scorpio is passionate, devoted and all in. It wants closeness of body, mind and soul, and it values honesty above polish. Small talk bores it. It notices the smallest change in a partner's mood, so trust and openness matter a great deal.

Water signs Cancer and Pisces meet its emotional depth. Earth signs Virgo and Capricorn offer the steadiness it respects. Its opposite, Taurus, can be a strong and stubborn match. The [Scorpio compatibility page](../../compatibility/scorpio/) covers each pairing.

## Scorpio at work

Scorpio tends to do well in work that digs deep or deals with the hidden side of life. Research, investigation, psychology, medicine, finance, crisis work and anything that needs focus and discretion suit the classic profile. It works best with real responsibility and trust. Office politics on the surface bore it, while a real mystery holds its interest for months.

## The myth and the stars

In Greek myth, the hunter Orion boasted that he could kill every animal on earth. The earth goddess sent a scorpion that stung him. Both were placed in the sky, but on opposite sides. When Scorpius rises in the east, Orion sets in the west, as if still fleeing.

The constellation Scorpius is one of the most beautiful in the sky, with a long curved tail of stars. At its heart glows Antares, a red supergiant whose name means rival of Mars. From the southern hemisphere the Scorpion rides high overhead on winter nights. Sky Altar shows it right where the Milky Way is brightest.

## Scorpio in your chart

A Scorpio Moon feels deeply and privately and may show little on the surface. A Scorpio rising often seems magnetic, calm and hard to read.
