## Libra at a glance

- **Dates** about September 23 to October 22, from the September equinox through the next 30 degrees.
- **Element** air, shared with Gemini and Aquarius.
- **Modality** cardinal, the signs that start each season.
- **Ruling planet** Venus, the planet of harmony, beauty and love.
- **Symbol** the Scales, drawn as a balance beam over the horizon: ♎.
- **Opposite sign** Aries.

## The character of Libra

Libra begins at the September equinox, when day and night are about equal all over the world. Astrologers read the sign through that image of balance. It is the only sign of the zodiac shown as an object rather than a living being, and its theme is relationship: me and you, this and that, finding the fair middle.

People with strong Libra placements are often described as charming, polite and diplomatic. They notice when a room feels tense and try to smooth it. They care about beauty, good design and good manners, and they tend to see both sides of an argument.

## Strengths

- **Fairness** Libra wants each person to be heard.
- **Charm** the sign is easy to talk to and good at making friends.
- **Taste** art, music, fashion and design speak to its Venus side.
- **Teamwork** Libra brings people together and helps them agree.

## Challenges

Seeing both sides can make choices hard. A Libra may weigh the menu, the holiday or the job offer for so long that the moment passes. The wish to keep the peace can also lead it to hide its own opinion, or to say yes when it means no.

Astrologers advise Libra to remember that a fair balance includes its own needs. A kind but honest no often makes a relationship stronger, not weaker.

## Libra in love and friendship

Libra is the sign most linked with partnership. It enjoys romance, courtship and the art of being a couple. It wants a partner who is kind, well mannered and good company. Rough edges and open conflict make it uneasy, so it does best with someone who can talk things through calmly.

Air signs Gemini and Aquarius share its love of ideas and people. Fire signs Leo and Sagittarius bring warmth and fun. Its opposite, Aries, teaches it to act. The [Libra compatibility page](../../compatibility/libra/) looks at each match.

## Libra at work

Libra tends to do well in work that brings people together or creates beauty. Law, mediation, diplomacy, design, fashion, the arts, human resources and client work suit the classic profile. It likes a pleasant workplace and fair colleagues. Constant conflict wears it down, while a team with shared goals helps it shine.

## The myth and the stars

The Scales are linked with Astraea, the goddess of justice, whom many myths place next door as the constellation Virgo. In Roman times the stars of Libra were known as the claws of the Scorpion. The Romans later made them into a balance, a fitting symbol for the time of equal day and night.

The constellation Libra is modest, with no very bright stars. Its two main stars still carry Arabic names that mean the southern claw and the northern claw, a memory of the older picture. Sky Altar shows the Scales between the Maiden and the Scorpion along the path of the Sun.

## Libra in your chart

A Libra Moon feels best in calm, pleasant company and may avoid conflict at its own cost. A Libra rising often looks graceful, friendly and well dressed. The seventh house, the house of partners, carries a Libra flavour in every chart.
