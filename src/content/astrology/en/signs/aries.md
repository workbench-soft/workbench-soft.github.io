## Aries at a glance

- **Dates** about March 21 to April 19, when the Sun passes through the first 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** fire, shared with Leo and Sagittarius.
- **Modality** cardinal, the signs that start each season.
- **Ruling planet** Mars, the planet of drive and courage.
- **Symbol** the Ram, drawn as two curled horns: ♈.
- **Opposite sign** Libra.

## The character of Aries

Aries opens the zodiac year at the spring equinox in the northern hemisphere. Astrologers read it as the sign of beginnings: the first green shoot, the first step, the first word in a conversation. People with strong Aries placements are often described as quick to act and quick to speak.

The Ram does not like to wait. It sees a door and pushes it open to find out what lies behind. That makes Aries good at starting projects and at breaking a long silence in a group. Traditional texts call it a masculine, hot and dry sign, which in old language meant active, outgoing and energetic.

## Strengths

- **Courage** Aries is known for stepping forward when others hold back.
- **Honesty** the sign tends to say what it thinks, so people know where they stand.
- **Energy** a fresh start or a challenge lights it up.
- **Quick recovery** a typical Aries forgets a setback fast and tries again.

## Challenges

Every sign has a shadow side in astrology, and the Aries shadow grows out of its speed. Impatience is the classic one. An Aries may start five things and finish two, or answer before the other person has finished talking. A quick temper also shows up in the traditional portraits, though it tends to cool as fast as it flares.

Astrologers suggest that Aries types gain a lot from a pause. Counting to ten, sleeping on a big decision, or asking one more question before acting can turn raw drive into steady results.

## Aries in love and friendship

In relationships Aries is warm, direct and playful. The sign enjoys the chase and the early spark. It likes partners who can keep up, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and speak plainly. Long silences and hidden feelings tend to frustrate it.

As a friend, Aries is the one who suggests the road trip or the new restaurant. It defends the people it cares about. The other fire signs, Leo and Sagittarius, share its pace. The air signs, Gemini and Aquarius, feed its ideas. The [compatibility page for Aries](../../compatibility/aries/) walks through each pairing.

## Aries at work

Aries tends to do well where it can lead, compete or build something from scratch. Starting a business, sports, emergency work, sales and any job with clear goals and fast feedback suit the classic Aries profile. A routine with no change can feel like a cage to it. A wise Aries pairs up with a patient, detail minded colleague who finishes what it starts.

## The myth and the stars

The Greeks linked Aries to the golden ram that rescued the children Phrixus and Helle. The ram flew them away from danger, and its golden fleece later hung in Colchis, where Jason and the Argonauts went to find it. Zeus set the ram among the stars.

The constellation Aries itself is small. Its brightest star, Hamal, shines in the autumn evening sky. Because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now stands in the constellation Pisces at the March equinox, while the tropical sign of Aries still starts on that date. ## Aries in your chart

Your Sun sign is only one part of a birth chart. Aries can also show up as your Moon sign, which colours your feelings and moods, or as your rising sign, which shapes first impressions. A Moon in Aries reacts fast and honestly. An Aries rising often looks energetic and direct to strangers.
