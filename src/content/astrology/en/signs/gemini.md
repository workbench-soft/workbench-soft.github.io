## Gemini at a glance

- **Dates** about May 21 to June 20, the third 30 degrees of the tropical zodiac.
- **Element** air, shared with Libra and Aquarius.
- **Modality** mutable, the signs that close each season.
- **Ruling planet** Mercury, the planet of thought and speech.
- **Symbol** the Twins, drawn as two pillars joined top and bottom: ♊.
- **Opposite sign** Sagittarius.

## The character of Gemini

Gemini closes the northern spring, a time of busy roads, long evenings and flowers turning into seeds. Astrologers read the sign as the mind waking up. It wants to name things, compare them and pass the news along. Gemini is the first sign of the zodiac shown as people, and its theme is connection.

People with strong Gemini placements are often described as witty, quick and curious. They read the back of the cereal box, start conversations in queues and know a little about a lot. Two sides to one person is the classic image. Gemini can be playful one moment and thoughtful the next.

## Strengths

- **Curiosity** Gemini wants to know how things work and why people think as they do.
- **Words** writing, talking, teaching and translating come easily to the sign.
- **Flexibility** a sudden change of plan rarely throws it.
- **Humour** Gemini can lighten a heavy room with one remark.

## Challenges

The mind that jumps from idea to idea can scatter. A Gemini may start a book, three podcasts and a new language in the same week and finish none of them. Restlessness can look like a lack of commitment to people who need more steadiness.

Gemini is also said to talk its way around feelings. The traditional advice is to slow down, pick one thread and follow it to the end. A quiet moment can stay quiet.

## Gemini in love and friendship

For Gemini, a good relationship starts with a good conversation. It falls for minds first. It enjoys partners who surprise it, share jokes and keep learning. Routine and heavy silence can make it feel trapped, while space to see friends and explore keeps it happy.

Air signs Libra and Aquarius share its love of ideas. Fire signs Aries and Leo give it energy and fun. The [Gemini compatibility page](../../compatibility/gemini/) looks at each match.

## Gemini at work

Jobs with variety, people and information suit Gemini. Journalism, teaching, sales, social media, translation, travel and tech support all fit the classic profile. It likes to juggle tasks and gets bored by the same report every day. A good Gemini work life has a clear goal and enough change along the way to stay interesting.

## The myth and the stars

The twins are Castor and Pollux, the sons of Leda. Castor was mortal and Pollux was a son of Zeus. The brothers were so close that when Castor died, Pollux asked to share his own immortality. Zeus placed them together in the sky, where they spend their days half in the world of the living and half in the underworld.

The constellation Gemini is easy to spot thanks to its two bright stars, Castor and Pollux, standing side by side east of Orion. The Sun passes through Gemini around the June solstice. In Sky Altar the Twins appear as two linked figures in the Greek theme.

## Gemini in your chart

A Gemini Moon processes feelings by talking them through and often needs a friend to think out loud with. A Gemini rising tends to come across as chatty, youthful and alert.
