## Mutable in short

- **Signs** Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius and Pisces.
- **Season link** each closes a season, when one kind of weather turns into the next.
- **Key words** flexibility, change, learning, variety, service.
- **Aspect link** the four mutable signs form squares and oppositions with each other.

## What a modality is

Each sign has one of three modalities. Cardinal signs begin a season, fixed signs hold it and mutable signs prepare for the next. The word mutable comes from the Latin for changeable. Old texts also called these the common or double bodied signs.

## Why these signs are mutable

The mutable signs fall at the end of each season:

- **Gemini** at the end of spring, as it turns into summer.
- **Virgo** at the end of summer, at harvest time.
- **Sagittarius** at the end of autumn, as winter nears.
- **Pisces** at the end of winter, as spring stirs.

These are times of change, when the weather shifts and people get ready for what comes next. Astrologers read the mutable signs as bridges between one state and another.

## Double signs

Three of the four mutable signs have double symbols: the two Twins of Gemini, the two Fishes of Pisces and the half human, half horse Archer of Sagittarius. Virgo is often shown holding wheat as well as being a maiden, both the season and the person. Old astrologers saw these double images as a sign of two sided, flexible natures.

## What mutable energy is like

Astrologers read mutable signs as adapters. They learn fast, see many sides and help others through change. People with a lot of mutable energy are often described as curious, versatile, helpful and easy going. They like variety and do well where plans shift.

The shadow side is restlessness or a lack of direction. Mutable signs may change course too often, struggle to decide or bend too far to please others.

## The four mutable signs

- **Gemini** mutable air. Adapts through ideas and talk.
- **Virgo** mutable earth. Adapts through skill and service.
- **Sagittarius** mutable fire. Adapts through exploration and faith.
- **Pisces** mutable water. Adapts through feeling and imagination.

## Mutable energy in a chart

A chart heavy in mutable signs suggests a flexible, open, learning temperament. A chart with little mutable energy may describe someone who is steady or driven but finds change harder, and who benefits from trying new things in small, safe steps.

The mutable signs are linked with the cadent houses: the third, sixth, ninth and twelfth, which old texts saw as houses of learning and preparation.

## Mutable signs together

Mutable signs sit 90 or 180 degrees apart. Two mutable people can be easy company and full of ideas, though they may need someone else to make the final call.
