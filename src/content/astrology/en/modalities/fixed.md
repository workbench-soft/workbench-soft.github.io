## Fixed in short

- **Signs** Taurus, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius.
- **Season link** each sits in the middle of a season, when its weather is most settled.
- **Key words** steadiness, loyalty, persistence, focus, determination.
- **Aspect link** the four fixed signs form squares and oppositions with each other.

## What a modality is

Each sign has one of three modalities: cardinal, fixed or mutable. They describe how energy moves. Cardinal signs begin, fixed signs hold and mutable signs adapt. Every season of the year has one sign of each kind, in that order.

## Why these signs are fixed

The fixed signs fall in the heart of each season, when it feels most itself:

- **Taurus** in the middle of spring, when blossom is full.
- **Leo** in the middle of summer, at the height of the heat.
- **Scorpio** in the middle of autumn, when the leaves fall.
- **Aquarius** in the middle of winter, in the deep cold.

At these times the season is no longer starting and not yet ending. Astrologers read the fixed signs in the same light: they keep things steady.

## The four fixed signs in old art

The fixed signs carry a striking image. Their symbols, the Bull, the Lion, the Eagle often linked with Scorpio, and the Man of Aquarius, appear across ancient art. They show up in Babylonian guardian figures, in the Bible's vision of Ezekiel and in medieval paintings of the four evangelists. The bright stars Aldebaran, Regulus, Antares and Fomalhaut, near these constellations, were once called the four royal stars, the guardians of the sky.

## What fixed energy is like

Astrologers read fixed signs as keepers. They stay the course, finish what they start and stay loyal to people and causes. People with a lot of fixed energy are often described as dependable, determined and strong willed. They work well with long projects and lasting bonds.

The shadow side is stubbornness. Fixed signs may resist change, even helpful change, and hold on to habits or opinions after they stop serving.

## The four fixed signs

- **Taurus** fixed earth. Holds on to comfort, security and values.
- **Leo** fixed fire. Holds on to pride, loyalty and creative passion.
- **Scorpio** fixed water. Holds on to deep feelings and trust.
- **Aquarius** fixed air. Holds on to ideas, ideals and friends.

## Fixed energy in a chart

A chart heavy in fixed signs suggests a person with great staying power. A chart with little fixed energy may describe someone who adapts easily but finds it harder to stick to one path, and who benefits from routines, a long project or a commitment that grows over time.

The fixed signs are linked with the succedent houses: the second, fifth, eighth and eleventh.

## Fixed signs together

Because fixed signs sit 90 or 180 degrees apart, two fixed people may both dig in their heels. Once they agree, though, few teams are as loyal or as hard to stop.
