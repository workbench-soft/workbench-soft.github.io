## Cardinal in short

- **Signs** Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn.
- **Season link** each starts a season: the two equinoxes and the two solstices.
- **Key words** initiative, leadership, beginnings, action, ambition.
- **Aspect link** the four cardinal signs form squares and oppositions with each other.

## What a modality is

Besides an element, each sign has a modality, sometimes called a quality or a mode. There are three: cardinal, fixed and mutable. Each describes a way of using energy. Cardinal signs start things, fixed signs keep them going and mutable signs adapt and pass them on.

The three modalities follow the rhythm of the seasons. Every season has a cardinal sign at its start, a fixed sign in its middle and a mutable sign at its end.

## Why these signs are cardinal

The cardinal signs begin exactly at the four turning points of the year:

- **Aries** begins at the March equinox.
- **Cancer** begins at the June solstice.
- **Libra** begins at the September equinox.
- **Capricorn** begins at the December solstice.

The word cardinal comes from the Latin for hinge. These signs are the hinges on which the year turns. In the southern hemisphere the seasons are reversed, but the signs keep the same dates.

## What cardinal energy is like

Astrologers read cardinal signs as starters. They see what needs doing and set things in motion. People with a lot of cardinal energy are often described as proactive, ambitious and good at leading. They like a fresh project and a clear goal.

The shadow side is a weaker follow through. Cardinal signs may start many things and lose interest once the exciting part is over. They can also be bossy when they feel others move too slowly.

## The four cardinal signs

- **Aries** cardinal fire. Starts with courage and action.
- **Cancer** cardinal water. Starts with care and protection.
- **Libra** cardinal air. Starts with ideas and partnership.
- **Capricorn** cardinal earth. Starts with plans and structure.

Each has the same drive to begin, but in its own style. Read more in the articles on [Aries](../../signs/aries/), [Cancer](../../signs/cancer/), [Libra](../../signs/libra/) and [Capricorn](../../signs/capricorn/).

## Cardinal energy in a chart

Astrologers count the planets in each modality. A chart heavy in cardinal signs suggests a person who likes to take the lead and start new things. A chart with little cardinal energy may describe someone who prefers to build on or adapt what others begin, and who benefits from setting small, clear goals.

The four angles of the chart, the Ascendant, the Midheaven, the Descendant and the lowest point, are linked with the cardinal signs and the angular houses: the first, fourth, seventh and tenth.

## Cardinal signs together

Because cardinal signs sit 90 or 180 degrees apart, they form squares and oppositions. Two cardinal people may both want to lead. With respect, that shared drive can become a strong team. A pattern of four planets in the four cardinal signs is called a cardinal grand cross.
