## Life path 8 in short

- **Key words** ambition, authority, achievement, strength, resources.
- **Theme** to lead, organise and use power well.
- **Often linked with** Saturn in Western lore.
- **Personal year 8** a year of effort, results and responsibility.

## How you get an 8

A life path 8 comes when the reduced month, day and year of birth add up to 17, 26, 35 or 8. For example, someone born on 8 July 2000 adds 7 for July, 8 for the day and 2 for the year. That gives 17, and 1 plus 7 gives 8. The [numerology overview](../../numerology/overview/) explains the method.

## The character of 8

Eight, turned on its side, looks like the sign for infinity, and its two circles suggest balance between the inner and outer worlds. In Chinese culture 8 is a famously lucky number, because its word sounds like the word for prosperity. Numerologists read it as the number of achievement and authority. People with a life path 8 are often described as ambitious, confident and capable.

Eights like to set big goals and manage the resources to reach them. They are often good organisers and natural leaders in business or community life. They value results and respect hard work.

## Strengths

- **Ambition** eights aim high and work hard.
- **Organisation** they manage people and resources well.
- **Resilience** they recover from setbacks and try again.
- **Authority** others trust them to take charge.

## Challenges

The drive of 8 can turn into workaholism or a focus on status. Eights may find it hard to show softness or to rest. They can also feel the pressure of high expectations. Numerologists suggest that 8s use their strength generously, keep their work and life in balance and remember that success means more than money.

## Life path 8 in love

In love, 8s are loyal, protective and generous. They want a partner who is strong in their own right and supports their goals. They may need to make time for romance amid busy schedules. Eights often get on well with 2s, 4s and 6s, who offer stability and care.

## Life path 8 at work

Eights do well in leadership and management: business, finance, law, politics, property, sports coaching and running organisations. They like clear goals, real responsibility and rewards that match effort.

## The personal year 8

A personal year 8 often brings a focus on work, money and responsibility. Numerologists see it as a time of harvest for the efforts of earlier years, when hard work can bring recognition. It also asks for wise use of power and fair dealings.

## Questions for an 8

- **Goals** what would real success look like for you?
- **Balance** how can you make room for rest and loved ones?
- **Power** how could you use your strength to help others?
