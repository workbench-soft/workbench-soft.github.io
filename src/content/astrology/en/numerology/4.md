## Life path 4 in short

- **Key words** order, hard work, stability, loyalty, practical sense.
- **Theme** to build something solid that lasts.
- **Often linked with** Saturn, or Uranus in some modern books.
- **Personal year 4** a year of steady work and foundations.

## How you get a 4

A life path 4 comes when the reduced month, day and year of birth add up to 13, 31 or 4. A sum of 22 is usually kept as the master number 22. For example, someone born on 6 May 2000 adds 5 for May, 6 for the day and 2 for the year. That gives 13, and 1 plus 3 gives 4. The [numerology overview](../../numerology/overview/) explains the method.

## The character of 4

Four is the number of the square, the four corners of a house and the four directions. Numerologists read it as the number of structure and stability. People with a life path 4 are often described as reliable, hard working and practical. They like plans, lists and clear rules, and they finish what they start.

Fours value honesty and loyalty. They are the people others rely on to get things done properly. They prefer real results to big talk.

## Strengths

- **Reliability** fours keep their promises.
- **Organisation** they build systems that work.
- **Persistence** they keep going until the job is done.
- **Honesty** they are straight with others.

## Challenges

The steady nature of 4 can become rigid. Fours may resist change, work too hard or be too strict with themselves and others. They may also find it hard to relax or to try something without a plan. Numerologists suggest that 4s leave room for play and surprises.

## Life path 4 in love

In love, 4s are loyal, steady and devoted. They show love through practical care and long term commitment. They want a partner they can trust and build a home with. Fours often get on well with 2s, 6s and 8s, who share their wish for security.

## Life path 4 at work

Fours do well in work that needs skill, care and method: engineering, building, accounting, law, administration, crafts, science and project management. They are valued for doing things right.

## The personal year 4

A personal year 4 is a time to roll up your sleeves. After the fun of year 3, numerologists see it as a year to build foundations: work steadily, sort out finances, fix your home or improve your health habits. The effort may feel heavy, but it lays a strong base.

## Questions for a 4

- **Build** what would you like to build over the next few years?
- **Rest** where can you allow yourself a break?
- **Change** what small change could make your routine more fun?
