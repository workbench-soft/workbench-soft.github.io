## Life path 1 in short

- **Key words** leadership, independence, courage, originality, will.
- **Theme** to stand on your own feet and start new things.
- **Often linked with** the Sun and the sign Aries in Western lore.
- **Personal year 1** a year of new beginnings.

## How you get a 1

A life path 1 comes when the reduced month, day and year of birth add up to 10, 19 or 28, or to 1 directly. For example, someone born on 5 March 2000 adds 3 for March, 5 for the day and 2 for the year. That gives 10, and 1 plus 0 gives 1. The [numerology overview](../../numerology/overview/) explains the method step by step.

## The character of 1

One is the first number, the point where counting starts. Numerologists read it as the number of the self: the person who steps forward, decides and leads. People with a life path 1 are often described as independent, driven and original. They like to do things their own way and often feel happiest when they are in charge of their own path.

Ones tend to have strong opinions and a clear sense of what they want. They are pioneers who like to be first, whether that means starting a business, trying a new sport or speaking up in a meeting.

## Strengths

- **Leadership** ones take charge and give direction.
- **Courage** they try what others will not.
- **Originality** they find new ways of doing things.
- **Determination** they keep going after setbacks.

## Challenges

The strong will of 1 can turn into stubbornness or impatience with others. Ones may find it hard to ask for help or to share credit. They can also feel lonely at the top. Numerologists suggest that 1s practise listening and teamwork, and remember that real strength includes trusting others.

## Life path 1 in love

In love, 1s are loyal, passionate and protective. They like a partner who has their own interests and respects their independence. They may need to learn to give their partner an equal voice. Ones often get on well with 3s and 5s, who share their energy, and can learn patience from 2s and 6s.

## Life path 1 at work

Ones do well where they can lead or create something new: running a business, management, invention, design, politics, sport and freelance work. They prefer to set their own goals and may chafe under strict control.

## The personal year 1

A personal year 1 begins a new nine year cycle. Numerologists see it as a time to plant seeds: start a project, set fresh goals and take the first step on a new road. The energy is bold and forward looking. What you start now, they say, may grow over the following years.

## Questions for a 1

- **Lead** where would you like to take the lead in your life?
- **Share** who could you ask for help this week?
- **Start** what new thing have you been waiting to begin?
