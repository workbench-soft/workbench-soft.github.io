## Life path 6 in short

- **Key words** care, love, family, responsibility, harmony, beauty.
- **Theme** to look after people and create a loving home.
- **Often linked with** Venus in Western lore.
- **Personal year 6** a year of home, family and duty.

## How you get a 6

A life path 6 comes when the reduced month, day and year of birth add up to 15, 24 or 6. A sum of 33 is usually kept as the master number 33. For example, someone born on 2 July 2004 adds 7 for July, 2 for the day and 6 for the year. That gives 15, and 1 plus 5 gives 6. The [numerology overview](../../numerology/overview/) explains the method.

## The character of 6

Six is the number of the honeycomb, the most efficient way bees build a home. Numerologists read it as the number of care and responsibility. People with a life path 6 are often described as warm, caring and generous. They look after family and friends, offer good advice and make any place feel like home.

Sixes value harmony, beauty and fairness. They often have a gift for design, cooking, gardening or anything that makes life more pleasant for others.

## Strengths

- **Care** sixes look after the people around them.
- **Responsibility** they take duties seriously.
- **Warmth** they create a welcoming atmosphere.
- **Sense of beauty** they bring style and comfort to their surroundings.

## Challenges

The caring heart of 6 can turn into worry or control. Sixes may try to fix the problems of the people they love, give advice that was not asked for, or neglect their own needs. They may also expect perfection from themselves and their loved ones. Numerologists suggest that 6s let others grow in their own way and save some care for themselves.

## Life path 6 in love

In love, 6s are devoted, romantic and nurturing. They dream of a happy home and a lasting partnership. They need a partner who appreciates their care and shares it in return. Sixes often get on well with 2s, 4s and 9s, who share their warmth and values.

## Life path 6 at work

Sixes do well in caring and creative work: teaching, nursing, counselling, social work, interior design, hospitality, cooking and anything that serves a community. They like to feel that their work helps others.

## The personal year 6

A personal year 6 often brings a focus on home and family. Numerologists see it as a time for commitments, caring for loved ones, improving your home or taking on responsibility in a community. It is a year of the heart, with both joys and duties.

## Questions for a 6

- **Care** who would you like to care for more this year?
- **Self** how could you care for yourself as kindly as you care for others?
- **Home** what would make your home feel more like you?
