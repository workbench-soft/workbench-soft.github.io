## Life path 9 in short

- **Key words** compassion, wisdom, generosity, idealism, completion.
- **Theme** to serve a larger purpose and give back.
- **Often linked with** Mars in some books, or Neptune in others.
- **Personal year 9** a year of endings and letting go.

## How you get a 9

A life path 9 comes when the reduced month, day and year of birth add up to 18, 27, 36 or 9. For example, someone born on 9 September 1998 adds 9 for September and 9 for the day. The year gives 1 plus 9 plus 9 plus 8, which is 27, then 9. Nine plus 9 plus 9 is 27, and 2 plus 7 gives 9. The [numerology overview](../../numerology/overview/) explains the method.

## The character of 9

Nine is the last single digit, so numerologists read it as the number of completion. It also has a curious property: multiply any number by 9 and the digits of the result add up to 9. People with a life path 9 are often described as compassionate, wise and generous. They care about the world and want to make it better.

Nines often have broad interests, a love of art and a deep sense of fairness. Many feel at home with people from all walks of life. They tend to think beyond themselves.

## Strengths

- **Compassion** nines feel for others and offer help.
- **Wisdom** they see the bigger picture.
- **Generosity** they give time, ideas and support.
- **Creativity** many have artistic gifts.

## Challenges

The idealism of 9 can make it disappointed when the world falls short. Nines may give too much, find it hard to let go of the past or struggle with endings. They can also seem distant when they focus on big causes over close ties. Numerologists suggest that 9s care for themselves and accept that closing a chapter makes room for a new one.

## Life path 9 in love

In love, 9s are romantic, warm and giving. They want a partner who shares their values and supports their ideals. They may need to make sure their own needs are met too. Nines often get on well with 3s and 6s, who share their warmth and creativity.

## Life path 9 at work

Nines do well in work that serves others or the wider world: charity, teaching, medicine, social work, the arts, environmental work, international work and healing. They need to feel their work has meaning.

## The personal year 9

A personal year 9 closes the nine year cycle. Numerologists see it as a time to finish projects, clear out what no longer fits and reflect on the lessons of the past years. It prepares the ground for the fresh start of the next personal year 1.

## Questions for a 9

- **Purpose** which cause or community matters most to you?
- **Release** what are you ready to let go of?
- **Receive** how could you let others care for you too?
