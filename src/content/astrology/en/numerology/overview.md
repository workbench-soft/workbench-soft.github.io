## Numbers with a meaning

Numerology is the tradition of giving meaning to numbers. It treats each number from 1 to 9 as a kind of character, with its own strengths and lessons. By turning a birth date or a name into a single number, numerology offers a short portrait to reflect on.

The most popular number is the life path number, worked out from the full date of birth. Many people use it alongside their Sun sign as a second, simple lens on personality.

## Where numerology comes from

People have found meaning in numbers for thousands of years. The Greek thinker Pythagoras and his followers, around 500 BCE, taught that numbers lie at the heart of the universe, from music to the stars. Hebrew gematria and Chinese lucky numbers are other old traditions. Modern Western numerology, with its life path numbers, took shape in the early twentieth century, largely through American writers.

## How to find your life path number

The common method reduces each part of the date separately, then adds them:

1. Reduce the month to one digit. November is 11, which is a master number, so keep it as 11.
2. Reduce the day. The 28th becomes 2 plus 8, which is 10, then 1 plus 0, which is 1.
3. Reduce the year. 1990 becomes 1 plus 9 plus 9 plus 0, which is 19, then 1 plus 9, which is 10, then 1.
4. Add the three results: 11 plus 1 plus 1 is 13.
5. Reduce again: 1 plus 3 is 4. The life path number is 4.

If a sum along the way gives 11, 22 or 33, many numerologists keep it unreduced, since these are master numbers. The Sky Altar app does this sum for every person you save.

## Other numbers

Numerology also turns names into numbers. Each letter gets a value from 1 to 9 in a simple table: A is 1, B is 2, up to I as 9, then J starts again at 1. The sum of the full birth name gives the expression number, and the vowels alone give the soul urge number. These add detail to the life path.

## The personal year

Your personal year number changes each birthday. Add the reduced month and day of your birth to the reduced current year. Numerologists read each personal year from 1 to 9 as a stage in a nine year cycle: 1 for beginnings, 5 for change, 9 for completion.

## A gentle reminder

Numerology is a cultural tradition, not a science or mathematics. A number cannot predict your future or define who you are. Enjoy it as a playful prompt for reflection. Start with [life path 1](../../numerology/1/) or the master number [11](../../numerology/11/).
