## The Dog at a glance

- **Recent years** 1970, 1982, 1994, 2006, 2018, 2030, and next 2042.
- **Order** eleventh of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Earth.
- **Polarity** yang.
- **Hour** 7 pm to 9 pm.
- **Best friends** the Tiger and the Horse, and the Rabbit as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Dragon.

## The Dog in the Great Race

The Dog was one of the best swimmers in the race and should have done well. But the water was cool and the day was warm, and the Dog could not resist playing in the river. By the time it climbed out, ten animals had already arrived. The Jade Emperor gave it eleventh place. The story shows the Dog's honest, playful heart.

In Chinese culture the dog guards the home and stands for loyalty and good fortune. A dog arriving at a house was once thought to bring wealth.

## The Dog's character

People born in a Dog year are often described as loyal, honest and fair. They care deeply about justice and stand up for people who are treated badly. Many are dependable friends who keep secrets and keep promises. They work hard for the people and causes they love.

Dogs are protective. They are slow to trust strangers but devoted once a bond forms. They have a strong sense of right and wrong.

## Strengths

- **Loyalty** Dogs stay by their friends and family.
- **Honesty** they are sincere and straightforward.
- **Fairness** they defend what is right.
- **Responsibility** they take duties seriously.

## Challenges

The Dog's sense of duty can make it anxious or pessimistic. It may worry about what could go wrong, or judge too quickly. It can also be stubborn when it feels something is unfair. Traditional advice encourages the Dog to relax, to trust and to enjoy the moment.

## The Dog in love and friendship

In love the Dog is faithful, caring and protective. It wants a partner it can trust completely and gives total loyalty in return. It takes time to open up. Tradition pairs it best with the Tiger and the Horse, who share its honesty and warmth, and with the Rabbit, whose gentleness it protects.

## The Dog at work

Dogs do well in work that helps or protects others: law, policing, social work, teaching, medicine, nursing, charity and community roles. They are trusted team members who keep everyone on track.

## The five kinds of Dog

- **Metal Dog** 1970, 2030. Determined, loyal and principled.
- **Water Dog** 1982. Kind, calm and thoughtful.
- **Wood Dog** 1994. Warm, cooperative and sincere.
- **Fire Dog** 2006. Bold, energetic and passionate.
- **Earth Dog** 2018. Steady, responsible and caring.

## Dog years in the calendar

Dog years are often seen as times of fairness, loyalty and community spirit. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.

## Dogs in the sky

Chinese astronomy has its own heavenly dog, Tiangou, who was said to swallow the Sun or the Moon during an eclipse. People banged drums and pots to scare it away. The legend is a good example of how the zodiac animals and sky lore mix in Chinese culture.
