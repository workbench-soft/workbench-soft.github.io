## The Dragon at a glance

- **Recent years** 1964, 1976, 1988, 2000, 2012, 2024, and next 2036.
- **Order** fifth of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Earth.
- **Polarity** yang.
- **Hour** 7 am to 9 am.
- **Best friends** the Rat and the Monkey, and the Rooster as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Dog.

## The Dragon in the Great Race

Everyone expected the Dragon, who could fly, to win. It arrived fifth. When the Jade Emperor asked why, the Dragon said it had stopped to make rain for a village whose crops were dying, and then blew a helpful breath to push the struggling Rabbit to shore. The Emperor was moved by its kindness.

The Dragon is the only mythical animal in the cycle. In China it brings rain, rules rivers and seas and stands for power, luck and wisdom. For centuries it was the emblem of the emperor. Unlike the fire breathing dragons of European tales, the Chinese dragon is mostly a friendly force.

## The Dragon's character

People born in a Dragon year are often described as confident, ambitious and charismatic. They have big ideas and the energy to chase them. Many are natural leaders who inspire others with their enthusiasm. They are generous, proud and full of life.

Dragons are said to be lucky, and Dragon years often see a rise in births in Chinese communities. Dragons like to aim high and dislike being held back.

## Strengths

- **Confidence** Dragons believe in themselves and their goals.
- **Charisma** they draw people to them.
- **Energy** they work hard and play hard.
- **Generosity** they share success with those around them.

## Challenges

The Dragon's confidence can become pride or impatience with others. It may take on too much, or expect others to keep up with its pace. It can also be blunt. Traditional advice encourages the Dragon to listen, to accept help and to rest.

## The Dragon in love and friendship

In love the Dragon is passionate, generous and protective. It wants a partner who admires it and can stand on their own. It enjoys romance with flair. Tradition pairs it best with the Rat and the Monkey, who match its energy and wit, and with the Rooster, whose sharp eye it respects.

## The Dragon at work

Dragons do well in leadership roles and in fields that reward vision: business, politics, entertainment, law, architecture and anything they can build or lead. They like to be in charge and to see big results.

## The five kinds of Dragon

- **Wood Dragon** 1964, 2024. Creative, curious and generous.
- **Fire Dragon** 1976. Passionate, bold and competitive.
- **Earth Dragon** 1988. Steady, sociable and practical.
- **Metal Dragon** 2000. Strong willed, honest and ambitious.
- **Water Dragon** 2012. Calm, wise and flexible.

## Dragon years in the calendar

Dragon years are among the most celebrated. Many families hope for a Dragon baby, and the dragon dance fills the streets at the Lunar New Year. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.

## The Azure Dragon in the sky

In Chinese astronomy the Azure Dragon is one of the Four Symbols. It guards the east and the spring sky. Its stars stretch across the parts of the sky that Western maps call Virgo, Libra and Scorpius. Sky Altar's Chinese theme draws the sky in this tradition.
