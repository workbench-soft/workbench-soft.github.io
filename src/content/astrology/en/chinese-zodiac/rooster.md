## The Rooster at a glance

- **Recent years** 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017, 2029, and next 2041.
- **Order** tenth of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Metal.
- **Polarity** yin.
- **Hour** 5 pm to 7 pm, around sunset.
- **Best friends** the Ox and the Snake, and the Dragon as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Rabbit.

## The Rooster in the Great Race

The Rooster spotted a raft on the river bank and called the Goat and the Monkey to share it. The three crossed together, and the Rooster took tenth place. It is the only bird in the cycle. In Chinese culture the rooster's crow greets the dawn and chases away the spirits of the night, so it stands for punctuality, courage and a fresh start.

## The Rooster's character

People born in a Rooster year are often described as honest, observant and hard working. They are organised, punctual and proud of a job well done. Many dress with care and like to look their best. They speak frankly and expect others to do the same.

Roosters have high standards. They notice details and keep track of plans. They often wake early and get a lot done.

## Strengths

- **Honesty** Roosters say what they think.
- **Diligence** they work hard and keep their promises.
- **Observation** they notice what others miss.
- **Courage** they defend their views and their friends.

## Challenges

The Rooster's frankness can sound harsh, and its high standards can make it critical of itself and others. It may also enjoy attention a little too much. Traditional advice encourages the Rooster to soften its words and to praise as well as correct.

## The Rooster in love and friendship

In love the Rooster is loyal, attentive and protective. It shows love through practical care and keeping its word. It wants a partner who is honest and reliable. Tradition pairs it best with the Ox and the Snake, who share its order and calm, and with the Dragon, whose confidence it admires.

## The Rooster at work

Roosters do well in work that needs precision and order: accounting, administration, journalism, medicine, the military, fashion and public speaking. They like clear goals and recognition for their effort.

## The five kinds of Rooster

- **Earth Rooster** 1969, 2029. Reliable, practical and kind.
- **Metal Rooster** 1981. Confident, sharp and determined.
- **Water Rooster** 1993. Clever, calm and thoughtful.
- **Wood Rooster** 2005. Fair, honest and sociable.
- **Fire Rooster** 2017. Energetic, bold and outspoken.

## Rooster years in the calendar

Rooster years are often seen as times for order, hard work and getting things done. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.

## Five virtues of the rooster

An old Chinese saying credits the rooster with five virtues. Its crest shows culture, its spurs show courage, its fighting spirit shows bravery, its habit of calling hens to share food shows kindness, and its crow at dawn shows trust. Many families still hang rooster pictures for luck.
