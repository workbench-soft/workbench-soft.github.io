## The Tiger at a glance

- **Recent years** 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022, and next 2034.
- **Order** third of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Wood.
- **Polarity** yang.
- **Hour** 3 am to 5 am.
- **Best friends** the Horse and the Dog, and the Pig as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Monkey.

## The Tiger in the Great Race

The Tiger was strong and fast, but the river's current pushed it downstream again and again. It fought its way across by sheer will and arrived third, tired but proud. In Chinese tradition the Tiger is the king of the beasts. The lines on its forehead are said to form the character for king, 王. Tiger images guarded homes and children against harm.

## The Tiger's character

People born in a Tiger year are often described as courageous, confident and full of energy. They like to take the lead and stand up for others. Many are generous, warm and quick to help a friend in trouble. They love a challenge and are not easily scared.

Tigers can be passionate and dramatic. They act on their feelings and follow their own path. They have a strong sense of justice and dislike being told what to do.

## Strengths

- **Courage** Tigers face hard situations head on.
- **Leadership** they inspire others to follow.
- **Generosity** they share freely and protect the weak.
- **Energy** they bring life and drive to any group.

## Challenges

The Tiger's boldness can turn into rashness. It may jump in without thinking, or clash with authority. Its strong moods can flare and fade quickly. Traditional advice encourages the Tiger to pause before acting and to listen to calmer voices.

## The Tiger in love and friendship

In love the Tiger is passionate, romantic and loyal. It likes excitement and can be swept away by strong feelings. It needs a partner who respects its freedom and admires its spirit. Tradition pairs it best with the Horse and the Dog, who share its warmth and honesty, and with the Pig, whose kindness calms it.

## The Tiger at work

Tigers do well in roles that need courage and initiative: leadership, entrepreneurship, sport, the military, rescue work, the arts and activism. They thrive where they can make a difference and dislike routine without purpose.

## The five kinds of Tiger

- **Water Tiger** 1962, 2022. Calm, wise and open minded.
- **Wood Tiger** 1974. Friendly, fair and creative.
- **Fire Tiger** 1986. Lively, bold and optimistic.
- **Earth Tiger** 1998. Steady, responsible and thoughtful.
- **Metal Tiger** 2010. Confident, competitive and determined.

## Tiger years in the calendar

Tiger years are often seen as lively and full of bold changes. Many people feel a push to take brave steps in these years. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.

## The Tiger in other cultures

The Tiger is one of the Four Symbols of Chinese astronomy. The White Tiger guards the west and the autumn sky. In Korea, tigers appear in countless folk tales and paintings as clever, sometimes comic guardians of the mountains.
