## The Rabbit at a glance

- **Recent years** 1963, 1975, 1987, 1999, 2011, 2023, and next 2035.
- **Order** fourth of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Wood.
- **Polarity** yin.
- **Hour** 5 am to 7 am, the time of sunrise.
- **Best friends** the Goat and the Pig, and the Dog as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Rooster.

## The Rabbit in the Great Race

The Rabbit could not swim, so it hopped across the river from stone to stone. When it nearly fell in, it caught a floating log, and some versions say the kind Dragon blew it to shore. It arrived fourth. The tale shows the Rabbit's quick wits and its talent for finding a gentle way around problems.

In Chinese legend a Jade Rabbit lives on the Moon with the goddess Chang'e, pounding the elixir of life. That gives the Rabbit a link with the Moon, grace and long life.

## The Rabbit's character

People born in a Rabbit year are often described as gentle, polite and refined. They value peace, beauty and good manners. They are tactful, good listeners and loyal friends. Many have an eye for art, fashion and design and like a calm, pretty home.

Rabbits are careful. They think before they leap and prefer to avoid conflict. Their quiet charm often wins people over without effort.

## Strengths

- **Kindness** Rabbits are caring and considerate.
- **Tact** they know how to say difficult things gently.
- **Taste** they have a feel for beauty and style.
- **Prudence** they make careful, sensible choices.

## Challenges

The Rabbit's love of peace can make it avoid conflict even when it needs to speak up. It may be cautious to the point of missing chances, or worry too much. Traditional advice encourages the Rabbit to trust its own voice and take a small risk now and then.

## The Rabbit in love and friendship

In love the Rabbit is tender, romantic and devoted. It wants a calm, harmonious relationship and a partner who is kind and reliable. Tradition pairs it best with the Goat and the Pig, who share its gentle nature, and with the Dog, whose loyalty makes it feel safe.

## The Rabbit at work

Rabbits do well in work that needs taste, diplomacy and care: design, the arts, fashion, teaching, counselling, diplomacy, hospitality and literature. They prefer a calm, pleasant workplace and good colleagues.

## The five kinds of Rabbit

- **Water Rabbit** 1963, 2023. Intuitive, gentle and sensitive.
- **Wood Rabbit** 1975. Generous, kind and flexible.
- **Fire Rabbit** 1987. Warm, lively and expressive.
- **Earth Rabbit** 1999. Grounded, practical and calm.
- **Metal Rabbit** 2011. Determined, clear and ambitious.

## The Cat in Vietnam

In the Vietnamese zodiac the Cat takes the Rabbit's place. Many explain this as a difference in how the old word for the fourth branch was heard. People born in these years in Vietnam celebrate the clever, graceful Cat.

## Rabbit years in the calendar

Rabbit years are often seen as calm, gentle years, good for rest, art and mending relationships. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.
