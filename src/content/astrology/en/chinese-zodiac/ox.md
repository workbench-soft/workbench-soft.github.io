## The Ox at a glance

- **Recent years** 1961, 1973, 1985, 1997, 2009, 2021, and next 2033.
- **Order** second of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Earth.
- **Polarity** yin.
- **Hour** 1 am to 3 am.
- **Best friends** the Snake and the Rooster, and the Rat as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Goat.

## The Ox in the Great Race

The Ox was the strongest swimmer and should have won the race. It kindly carried the Rat across the river on its back, only to see the Rat jump ahead at the finish. The Ox came second without complaint. The legend sums up the Ox's nature: strong, generous and steady, even when others are quicker.

In farming China the ox pulled the plough and turned the fields. It stood for hard work, patience and an honest harvest.

## The Ox's character

People born in an Ox year are often described as reliable, calm and hard working. They do things carefully and see them through. They prefer facts to fancy talk, and they keep their promises. Many have a quiet strength that others lean on in hard times.

Oxen like routine and tradition. They are slow to anger, but once they make up their minds, it is hard to change them. They are often close to their families and loyal to old friends.

## Strengths

- **Diligence** Oxen work steadily and finish what they start.
- **Honesty** they say what they mean.
- **Patience** they wait for the right moment and the right result.
- **Strength** they stay calm under pressure.

## Challenges

The Ox's firmness can turn into stubbornness. It may resist new ideas or hold on to old ways after they have stopped working. It can also keep its feelings inside until they burst out. Traditional advice encourages the Ox to share its thoughts and to try something new now and then.

## The Ox in love and friendship

In love the Ox is faithful, protective and practical. It shows care through deeds more than words. It is not a flashy romantic, but it offers a stable, loving home. Tradition pairs it best with the Snake and the Rooster, who share its care and order, and with the Rat, whose liveliness brightens its days.

## The Ox at work

Oxen do well in work that rewards skill and persistence: farming, engineering, medicine, crafts, building, finance and management. They are trusted for their reliability and often rise slowly but surely.

## The five kinds of Ox

- **Metal Ox** 1961, 2021. Strong willed and hard working.
- **Water Ox** 1973. Patient, reasonable and diplomatic.
- **Wood Ox** 1985. Fair, kind and steady.
- **Fire Ox** 1997. Confident, energetic and outspoken.
- **Earth Ox** 2009. Sincere, practical and very loyal.

## Ox years in the calendar

Ox years are often seen as times for steady effort and building foundations. They reward patience more than speed. As with every animal, the year begins at the Lunar New Year in late January or February.
