## The Pig at a glance

- **Recent years** 1971, 1983, 1995, 2007, 2019, 2031, and next 2043.
- **Order** twelfth and last of the animals.
- **Fixed element** Water.
- **Polarity** yin.
- **Hour** 9 pm to 11 pm, the end of the day.
- **Best friends** the Rabbit and the Goat, and the Tiger as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Snake.

## The Pig in the Great Race

The Pig set off in good time, but it got hungry on the way. It stopped for a big meal, then lay down for a nap. When it finally reached the finish, the race was over, and it took the last place. The Pig did not mind. It had enjoyed the day. The story shows the Pig's easy going, pleasure loving nature.

In Chinese culture the pig stands for wealth, comfort and plenty. A family with a pig had food for the year, and the character for home, 家, shows a pig under a roof.

## The Pig's character

People born in a Pig year are often described as generous, kind and sincere. They enjoy good food, good company and the pleasures of life. Many are honest, trusting and slow to judge others. They work hard and like to share what they earn.

Pigs are peaceful. They avoid conflict and forgive easily. They are often the friend who brings snacks, remembers birthdays and makes each guest feel welcome.

## Strengths

- **Generosity** Pigs share freely and help others.
- **Sincerity** they are honest and mean what they say.
- **Warmth** they make people feel at home.
- **Optimism** they enjoy life and look on the bright side.

## Challenges

The Pig's trusting nature can make it naive, and its love of comfort can lead to overindulgence. It may also avoid hard truths to keep the peace. Traditional advice encourages the Pig to set healthy limits and to look closely before it trusts.

## The Pig in love and friendship

In love the Pig is affectionate, loyal and romantic. It wants a warm, cosy relationship and gives a great deal of care. It enjoys sharing meals, holidays and simple pleasures. Tradition pairs it best with the Rabbit and the Goat, who share its gentle heart, and with the Tiger, whose courage it admires.

## The Pig at work

Pigs do well in work that brings comfort and enjoyment: hospitality, food, entertainment, retail, the arts, nursing and charity work. They are friendly team members who keep the mood light.

## The five kinds of Pig

- **Metal Pig** 1971, 2031. Strong, honest and determined.
- **Water Pig** 1983. Gentle, kind and understanding.
- **Wood Pig** 1995. Warm, generous and cooperative.
- **Fire Pig** 2007. Energetic, brave and passionate.
- **Earth Pig** 2019. Calm, sociable and reliable.

## Pig years in the calendar

Pig years close the twelve year cycle. They are often seen as times to enjoy the harvest, rest and prepare for the new cycle that the Rat year will begin. The year starts at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.

## Zhu Bajie

The best known pig in Chinese literature is Zhu Bajie, Pigsy, from Journey to the West. He is greedy, lazy and funny, but also brave and loyal to his friends. Readers have loved him for centuries.
