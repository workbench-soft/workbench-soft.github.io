## The Goat at a glance

- **Recent years** 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015, 2027, and next 2039.
- **Order** eighth of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Earth.
- **Polarity** yin.
- **Hour** 1 pm to 3 pm.
- **Best friends** the Rabbit and the Pig, and the Horse as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Ox.

## Goat, Sheep or Ram

The Chinese word yáng covers goats, sheep and rams, so the eighth animal appears under all three names in English. In northern China it is often drawn as a goat, in the south as a sheep. All three share the same gentle, peaceful meaning.

## The Goat in the Great Race

The Goat could not swim far, so it teamed up with the Monkey and the Rooster. The three found a raft and crossed the river together. The Rooster spotted the raft, the Monkey cleared the weeds, and the Goat helped steer. The Jade Emperor gave the Goat eighth place for its teamwork. The story shows the Goat's cooperative, peaceful spirit.

## The Goat's character

People born in a Goat year are often described as gentle, kind and creative. They love beauty, art and nature, and they have a strong sense of empathy. Many are calm, polite and easy to be around. They prefer harmony to competition.

Goats often have a rich imagination and a talent for the arts. They feel most at home in a peaceful setting with people they trust.

## Strengths

- **Kindness** Goats are caring and generous with their time.
- **Creativity** they have artistic gifts and a fine eye.
- **Calm** they bring peace to tense situations.
- **Loyalty** they stay close to friends and family.

## Challenges

The Goat's gentle nature can tip into shyness or worry. It may avoid hard decisions or rely too much on others for support. Traditional advice encourages the Goat to trust its own judgement and to speak up for what it wants.

## The Goat in love and friendship

In love the Goat is tender, devoted and romantic. It wants a partner who gives it security and shows affection openly. It is a kind, considerate partner in return. Tradition pairs it best with the Rabbit and the Pig, who share its gentle heart, and with the Horse, whose energy lifts its spirits.

## The Goat at work

Goats do well in creative and caring work: art, design, music, writing, gardening, teaching, nursing and anything that brings beauty or comfort to others. They work best in a supportive team without harsh competition.

## The five kinds of Goat

- **Fire Goat** 1967, 2027. Warm, brave and expressive.
- **Earth Goat** 1979. Steady, kind and practical.
- **Metal Goat** 1991. Determined, gentle and artistic.
- **Water Goat** 2003. Sensitive, intuitive and easy going.
- **Wood Goat** 2015. Generous, caring and creative.

## Goat years in the calendar

Goat years are often seen as calm years that favour family, art and quiet progress. The coming Fire Goat year starts at the Lunar New Year on February 6, 2027.

## The Goat and the city of rams

The southern Chinese city of Guangzhou is nicknamed the City of Rams. Legend says five immortals rode into the city on five rams carrying ears of rice, and blessed it with freedom from famine. A famous statue of the five rams still stands in a city park.
