## The Rat at a glance

- **Recent years** 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020, and next 2032.
- **Order** first of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Water.
- **Polarity** yang.
- **Hour** 11 pm to 1 am, the start of the Chinese day.
- **Best friends** the Dragon and the Monkey, and the Ox as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Horse.

## The Rat in the Great Race

The Rat won the race by its wits. It could not swim well, so it asked the Ox for a ride across the river, then leapt off the Ox's head at the last moment to land first. The story gives the Rat its reputation: small in size, big in cleverness. In Chinese culture the Rat is also linked with wealth, since a rat in the grain store means there is grain to store.

## The Rat's character

People born in a Rat year are often described as smart, alert and charming. They notice opportunities quickly and know how to make the most of what they have. Many are good with money, saving carefully and spending wisely. They enjoy company and are often witty talkers with a wide circle of friends.

Rats are adaptable. Like their namesake, they can thrive almost anywhere and find a way through any maze. They tend to plan ahead and keep something in reserve for a rainy day.

## Strengths

- **Quick mind** Rats think fast and solve problems on the spot.
- **Resourcefulness** they make a lot out of a little.
- **Charm** they are sociable and easy to like.
- **Thrift** they plan and save with care.

## Challenges

The Rat's quickness can make it restless or anxious. It may worry about money or security more than it needs to. Its eye for opportunity can sometimes look like self interest to others. Traditional advice suggests that Rats share their talents generously and relax once in a while.

## The Rat in love and friendship

In love the Rat is affectionate, attentive and loyal once committed. It likes a partner who is clever and steady. It is often a devoted family member who looks after loved ones well. Tradition pairs it best with the Dragon, whose confidence it admires, the Monkey, whose wit matches its own, and the Ox, who gives it calm and security.

## The Rat at work

Rats tend to do well in business, finance, research, writing and any role that rewards quick thinking. They are good at spotting trends and can be excellent managers of resources. Routine without challenge bores them.

## The five kinds of Rat

- **Metal Rat** 1960, 2020. Determined and ambitious.
- **Water Rat** 1972, 2032. Wise, gentle and good with words.
- **Wood Rat** 1984. Creative and well liked.
- **Fire Rat** 1996. Energetic, bold and adventurous.
- **Earth Rat** 2008. Steady, practical and careful.

## Rat years in the calendar

A Rat year opens a new twelve year cycle, so many people see it as a time for fresh starts and new plans. The Lunar New Year can fall in late January or February, so people born early in a year should check the date.
