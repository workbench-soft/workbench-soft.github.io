## The Monkey at a glance

- **Recent years** 1968, 1980, 1992, 2004, 2016, 2028, and next 2040.
- **Order** ninth of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Metal.
- **Polarity** yang.
- **Hour** 3 pm to 5 pm.
- **Best friends** the Rat and the Dragon, and the Snake as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Tiger.

## The Monkey in the Great Race

The Monkey crossed the river on the raft with the Goat and the Rooster. It used its quick hands to clear the weeds that blocked the way. In some versions it also played tricks along the bank. The three friends arrived together, and the Monkey took ninth place.

## The Monkey King

No Chinese animal is more famous in story than the Monkey. Sun Wukong, the Monkey King of the classic novel Journey to the West, was born from a stone, learned magic, made chaos in heaven and later guarded a monk on his long trip to India. He is clever, brave, mischievous and loyal, the perfect picture of the Monkey sign.

## The Monkey's character

People born in a Monkey year are often described as clever, curious and fun. They learn quickly, solve problems in creative ways and love a joke. Many are lively talkers who make friends easily. They enjoy games, puzzles and anything new.

Monkeys are flexible and inventive. They can find a way out of almost any tight spot. They get bored quickly and need variety to stay happy.

## Strengths

- **Intelligence** Monkeys are sharp and quick to learn.
- **Creativity** they find new answers to old problems.
- **Humour** they bring fun and laughter.
- **Adaptability** they handle change with ease.

## Challenges

The Monkey's playfulness can turn into mischief or a lack of focus. It may get bored and leave tasks unfinished, or rely on cleverness instead of effort. Traditional advice encourages the Monkey to be patient and to use its talents for the good of others.

## The Monkey in love and friendship

In love the Monkey is lively, romantic and full of surprises. It wants a partner who can keep up with its wit and share its sense of fun. Tradition pairs it best with the Rat and the Dragon, who share its energy and cleverness, and with the Snake, whose depth intrigues it.

## The Monkey at work

Monkeys do well in work that needs quick thinking and creativity: science, technology, engineering, media, sales, entertainment and trading. They thrive on variety and new challenges.

## The five kinds of Monkey

- **Earth Monkey** 1968, 2028. Steady, kind and reliable.
- **Metal Monkey** 1980. Confident, independent and smart.
- **Water Monkey** 1992. Sensitive, flexible and clever.
- **Wood Monkey** 2004. Creative, curious and generous.
- **Fire Monkey** 2016. Energetic, bold and inventive.

## Monkey years in the calendar

Monkey years are often seen as lively times of new ideas and clever solutions. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.
