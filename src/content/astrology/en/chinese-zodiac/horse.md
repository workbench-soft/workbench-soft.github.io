## The Horse at a glance

- **Recent years** 1966, 1978, 1990, 2002, 2014, 2026, and next 2038.
- **Order** seventh of the twelve animals.
- **Fixed element** Fire.
- **Polarity** yang.
- **Hour** 11 am to 1 pm, the middle of the day.
- **Best friends** the Tiger and the Dog, and the Goat as a secret friend.
- **Clash** the Rat.

## The Horse in the Great Race

The Horse galloped strongly across the river and was sure of a good place. Just before the finish, the Snake that had hidden on its hoof slid off and startled it. The Horse reared, and the Snake slipped in ahead. The Horse came seventh, still proud of its run.

In China the horse stands for speed, freedom, travel and success. A popular wish for the Lunar New Year says, may success come at the speed of a galloping horse.

## The Horse's character

People born in a Horse year are often described as energetic, cheerful and independent. They love movement, travel and new experiences. Many are popular, warm and quick to laugh. They speak their minds and like to be on the go.

Horses value freedom above almost everything. They work hard when a task excites them and dislike being tied down by dull routine or strict rules.

## Strengths

- **Energy** Horses bring pace and enthusiasm.
- **Warmth** they are friendly and easy to like.
- **Independence** they think for themselves.
- **Optimism** they look on the bright side.

## Challenges

The Horse's love of freedom can make it restless or impatient. It may lose interest in a project once the excitement fades, or make quick decisions it regrets. Traditional advice encourages the Horse to pace itself and to finish the race it starts.

## The Horse in love and friendship

In love the Horse is passionate, romantic and fun. It falls quickly and wants a partner who shares its zest for life and gives it room. Tradition pairs it best with the Tiger and the Dog, who share its warmth and honesty, and with the Goat, whose gentleness calms it.

## The Horse at work

Horses do well in active, varied work: sales, travel, sport, journalism, performing, public relations and anything with movement and people. They like freedom in how they work.

## The five kinds of Horse

- **Fire Horse** 1966, 2026. Bold, passionate and free spirited.
- **Earth Horse** 1978. Kind, practical and steady.
- **Metal Horse** 1990. Strong willed, honest and adventurous.
- **Water Horse** 2002. Friendly, adaptable and quick.
- **Wood Horse** 2014. Sociable, fair and creative.

## The Fire Horse of 2026

The Fire Horse, which comes once every sixty years, has a famous reputation. In Japan, an old folk belief about women born in Fire Horse years led to a sharp drop in births in 1966. Today most people see the Fire Horse as a symbol of energy, courage and passion. The 2026 Fire Horse year began on February 17, 2026.

## Horse years in the calendar

Horse years are often seen as lively times of travel, movement and bold plans. The year begins at the Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid February.
